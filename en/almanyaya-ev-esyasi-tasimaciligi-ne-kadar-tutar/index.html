2026 Germany Moving Cost Estimation Guide

What is the Actual Cost of Moving Household Goods to Germany?

Correctly planning the budget in an international move means successfully completing half of the process. At Han Bros Cargo, we provide the most transparent and scientific answer to the question "how much will I pay?" from our customers who will move their home to Germany. Our pricing is based on m3 (cubic meter) volume calculation, and road expenses and customs clearance services are included in this figure.

Factors Shaping Costs

Moving fees to Germany are formed by the combination of many variables:

  • Item Volume (Cubic Meters): 1+1 homes usually occupy 10-15 m3, 2+1 homes 18-25 m3, and 3+1 homes 30 m3 and above. As volume increases, the unit price per m3 becomes more economical.
  • Freight (Road Fee): Road tolls, fuel costs, and ferry expenses are calculated according to the route (Adriatic or Balkan line).
  • Personal Belongings Customs Clearance: Our consultants' file opening, border crossing documents, and representation fees are reflected in the price.
  • Elevator and Floor Difference: The use of elevators and floor height in both Turkey and Germany are factors affecting labor costs.

Current Price Ranges (Estimated)

According to 2026 market conditions, m3 prices in partial (groupage) transport fluctuate within a certain range. In full home moves (Full Truck), progress is made over a fixed freight fee since a vehicle is allocated. We would like to remind you that insurance and packaging are included in the prices we offer as Han Bros Cargo, whereas "unregistered" companies in the market may charge additional fees later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is packaging included in the prices?

Yes, standard packaging (wrapping furniture, protecting large pieces) is included in our prices. Fine packaging (kitchenware, clothes) can be offered as an additional service.

Is there a price difference between Munich and Hamburg?

Yes, since the distance to southern German cities (Munich, Stuttgart) and northern cities (Hamburg, Bremen) is different, the road fee can change slightly.

Will I pay customs duty?

If you are moving under personal belongings (at least 6 months used goods) status and have completed your legal documents, you will be exempt from customs duty. Our quoted price includes customs consultancy except for any taxes.

When and how can I pay?

We usually plan our payments so that half is at the time of loading and half is before delivery after customs approval. Credit card and bank transfer options are available.

How can I get the best price?

Booking early (at least 4 weeks) and keeping your moving date flexible (complying with partial loading dates) will ensure you get the best price.
