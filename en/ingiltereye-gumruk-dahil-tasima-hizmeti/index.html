Transport Service to UK Including Customs

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer comprehensive solutions for transport service to UK including customs. We offer customs procedures, document preparation, tax calculations, post-Brexit procedures and shipping service as a single point solution.

Our Service Scope

  • Customs Procedures: Tracking and management of post-Brexit customs procedures
  • Document Preparation: Customs declaration, invoice and all required documents
  • Tax Calculations: Customs tax, VAT and other tax calculations
  • Transport Service: Household goods, furniture, vehicle and commercial freight transport
  • Delivery: Safe delivery to your address
  • Tracking and Support: 24/7 tracking and customer support

Post-Brexit Process

Our professional team manages the changed customs procedures after Brexit. We have current knowledge and experience in new customs rules, document requirements and tax structure.

Customs Procedures

  • Customs Declaration: Detailed customs declaration is prepared
  • Document Check: Check of all documents and deficiency detection
  • Tax Calculation: Customs tax, VAT and other taxes are calculated
  • Customs Tracking: Tracking and coordination of customs procedures
  • Approval and Delivery: Delivery after customs approval

Document Preparation

All customs documents and papers are prepared by our professional team. Invoice, certificate of origin, insurance documents and other required documents are completed completely.

Tax Calculations

Customs tax, VAT and other taxes are calculated transparently. You get service without hidden costs, at predetermined price.

Transport Process

  1. Survey and Planning: Freight inventory is taken and transport plan is created
  2. Document Preparation: Customs documents and required papers are prepared
  3. Packing: Professional packing and packaging is done
  4. Loading: Safe loading process is performed
  5. Transport: Transport is done by sea or road
  6. Customs Procedures: Customs procedures are completed in UK
  7. Delivery: Safe delivery is made to your address

Comprehensive Service Package

We provide service from a single point in customs procedures, document preparation, transport and delivery. All processes are managed by our professional team, you just receive delivery.
