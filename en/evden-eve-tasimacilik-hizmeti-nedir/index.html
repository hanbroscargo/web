What is Door-to-Door Moving? Guide and Tips

The Key to Comfortable Relocation: Door-to-Door Moving Service

Moving used to mean; days of packing rush, fear of items being damaged, and endless fatigue. However, today professional 'Door-to-Door Moving' service turns this process from a nightmare into a pleasant start. The door-to-door shipping solutions we offer as Han Bros Cargo is an 'end-to-end' process covering the disassembly and packaging of your belongings by expert teams from your current address, their safe transport to your new address, and their re-assembly and placement there. We do not just carry items, we save you time and energy in your new home.

Stages of International Level Door-to-Door Shipping

How do we implement the perfect moving plan for you?

  • Analysis and Planning: Our experts examine your items, determine the required packaging materials and vehicle, and create a special moving schedule for you.
  • Professional Packaging: Every piece, from your fragile items to your furniture, is packaged with shock-absorbing materials suitable for international shipping standards.
  • Safe Loading and Transport: Your belongings are loaded into our vehicles by being fixed against vibrations and transported through the safest routes by our experienced drivers.
  • Delivery and Placement: Your items are distributed according to rooms at your new address, furniture assemblies are made, and all packaging wastes are cleaned and delivered.

Why Should You Work with a Corporate Door-to-Door Company?

In door-to-door moving, 'corporateness' means the safety of your belongings and the protection of your rights. Working with a deep-rooted firm like Han Bros Cargo provides insured transport assurance, transparent pricing, and the possibility to find a professional interlocutor. Especially in international moves, command of customs procedures ensures that your belongings proceed without getting stuck at borders. Thanks to our technological infrastructure, you can track your vehicle instantly and have information at every second of the moving day. Our experienced team manages every load from the most sensitive antiques to the heaviest white goods with great care. Moving is no longer a burden for you, you are only a signature away from your new life with Han Bros Cargo.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between door-to-door moving and regular cargo?

While only transport is done in shipping, all details such as packaging, furniture assembly, white goods installation, and placement are included in door-to-door service.

What do I need to do before moving?

It is enough to take your personal belongings (jewelry, cash, special passports, etc.) with you. All remaining packaging and transport operations are handled by our expert teams.

Who packs the kitchenware?

If you have chosen our turnkey service, all your kitchen utensils are wrapped and boxed one by one by our professional staff.

Do you have elevator transport service?

Yes, we use our exterior elevator systems so that items do not damage the elevator shaft or stairs in high-rise buildings.

Do you transport locally or intercity/internationally?

As the main focus of Han Bros Cargo is to provide international (Turkey-Europe line) service, we offer professional door-to-door solutions at strategic points.
