Parcel Shipping from Antalya to Liverpool

Relocating from Antalya to Liverpool is not just a physical move, but a start to a new life. We make this transition process as comfortable as possible for you with our Antalya to Liverpool parcel shipping service.

Our Logistics Process from Antalya to Liverpool

For shipments from Antalya, we use road transport to access Europe. The distance between the two points is approximately 3,700 km, and our estimated delivery time is 14-16 days. We offer safe packaging with our Mediterranean region logistics network. We provide special solutions and advantages for those settling in the UK from touristic regions.

Parcel Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our service focused on Express Parcel and Package Delivery. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable. Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and entered into the tracking system. Additionally, you can benefit from our 48-72 hour delivery option for urgent shipments.

Professional Packaging and Customs

The safety of your belongings is our priority. For fragile items, we use a "kraft paper + bubble wrap" combination. On the customs side, our expert team manages the procedures that have become more complex after Brexit on your behalf.

How Does the Process Work?

  • Survey: Volume detection at your address in Antalya.
  • Operation: Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and tracked.
  • Customs: Export procedures from Antalya and import procedures in Liverpool.
  • Delivery: Scheduled delivery and installation support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there customs duties?

If you obtain ToR approval for personal effects, you won't pay taxes. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable.

When is payment made?

A reservation is made with a 30% down payment, and the remainder is collected upon delivery.

Do you have storage services?

Yes, we have secure warehouses in both Turkey and the UK. We can store your belongings for as long as you need.
