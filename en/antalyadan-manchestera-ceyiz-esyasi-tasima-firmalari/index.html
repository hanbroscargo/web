Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How long does the shipping take?

The average delivery time varies between 4 and 14 days, depending on the selected transport mode (Express Minivan or Truck) and customs density. We have regular flights on the Antalya to Manchester route.

2. Do I pay customs duty?

In personal effects (personal household items) transport, you may be exempt from customs duty if you meet certain conditions (Transfer of Residence - ToR). For commercial goods, taxation is made in accordance with the legislation. Our experts provide free consultancy on this subject.

3. Are my belongings insured?

Yes, your belongings are secured under All-Risk insurance or CMR insurance in all our transport operations. In case of possible damage, insurance procedures are followed by our company.

4. Can I do the packaging myself?

You can box your personal items (clothes, books, etc.), but it is strongly recommended that the packaging of furniture, white goods, and sensitive items be done by our professional team for insurance coverage and safe transport.
