Amazon UK FBA Logistics and Prep Services

Professional Logistics Solutions for Amazon UK Sellers

The key to success in the Amazon UK market is to deliver your products to Amazon fulfillment centers (FCs) smoothly, quickly, and in compliance with the rules. The increased difficulty of customs processes after the United Kingdom's departure from the EU (Brexit) has made logistics planning more critical than ever. At Han Bros Cargo, we offer end-to-end expertise in FBA shipments we carry out from Turkey to the UK.

Amazon FBA Prep Services

Amazon applies very strict standards for products entering its warehouses. Packaging or labeling errors can lead to your products being rejected. When you work with us, you have the following advantages:

  • FNSKU Labeling: FNSKU barcoding in accordance with Amazon standards is done for each of your products.
  • Palletization Standards: Loading is done according to specific pallet sizes and height rules (GMA pallets, etc.) accepted by UK Amazon warehouses.
  • Box Preparation: Placement of heavy box warnings and Amazon box labels is ensured.

UK Customs Process Post-Brexit

Customs declaration is now mandatory for loads going to the UK. Han Bros Cargo offers customs consultancy services at both the Turkey exit and the UK entry. Checking your EORI number and coordinating VAT and Customs Duty payments are tracked by our expert teams. With our DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) option, we manage all taxes on your behalf, ensuring your products pass door-to-door without barriers.

Warehouse Delivery Appointments (Carrier Central)

An appointment system is mandatory for deliveries made by truck to Amazon warehouses. Han Bros Cargo's logistics network organizes appointments through the Amazon Carrier Central system and is ready at the warehouse at the specified time. In this way, delays and additional costs arising from waiting are avoided.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does a shipment from Turkey to Amazon UK take?

In our road partial shipments, including customs clearance, the delivery time from Istanbul to UK warehouses is on average 7 to 10 business days.

Is it possible for you to stick barcodes on my products?

Yes, within the scope of value-added services (VAS), we professionally perform the packaging of your products and the sticking of Amazon FBA labels.

How can I pay VAT and Customs Duty?

If you wish, you can pay through your own EORI number, or you can transfer the process to us in exchange for a single freight invoice including all expenses with our DDP model.

Can Amazon reject my products at customs?

They can be rejected if packaging standards (non-standard pallets, missing labels, etc.) are not complied with. However, Han Bros Cargo's expertise guarantees that your products will be prepared to be 100% accepted.

Do you have a return management service?

Yes, we also provide support in the processes of collecting products returning from Amazon UK or unsold products (Removal Orders) in our partner warehouse in the UK and bringing them back to Turkey or re-labeling them.
