Weight and Volume Calculation in Transport: Essential Guide

In the international transport and logistics sector, the two most critical factors determining the cost of a shipment are weight and volume. At Han Bros Cargo, we provide a transparent calculation method so that our customers can accurately predict shipment costs in advance. The concept known in the industry as "Chargeable Weight" is a result of the relationship between the space occupied by the shipment and its actual mass.

Why is Volumetric Weight Calculated?

Logistics vehicles (aircraft, trucks, containers) have both a weight-carrying capacity and a volumetric capacity. For example, if you fill a truck entirely with down pillows, the vehicle will be far below its weight capacity but entirely full volumetrically. Therefore, pricing based only on actual weight would hinder the sustainability of logistics operations. Volumetric weight (Dim Weight) calculation ensures that light but high-volume cargoes are priced fairly.

Calculation Formula and Methods

Volumetric weight is calculated based on the external dimensions of the package (Width x Length x Height). According to industry standards, the result of this multiplication is divided by a specific coefficient:

  • Road Transport: Generally, the formula (Width x Length x Height) / 3000 is used.
  • Air Transport: According to international IATA standards, the formula (Width x Length x Height) / 5000 is applied.
  • Sea Transport: Generally, a principle of 1 cubic meter (m3) = 1000 kg is used for calculation.

Han Bros Cargo helps you optimize costs by using the most advantageous coefficient according to the type of your shipment. Actual weight and volumetric weight are compared, and billing is done based on whichever value is higher.

The Importance of Accurate Measurement

When measuring the dimensions of your shipments, you should also take into account any protrusions on the box, pallet feet, and protective packaging margins. Incorrectly provided dimensions may lead to unexpected additional costs during customs clearance or storage processes. When working with us, our expert team verifies your load with precision measuring devices at our Adana or operation centers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is volumetric weight (desi)?

Volumetric weight is the value obtained by multiplying the dimensions (width, length, height) of a package and dividing it by a specific coefficient. In the logistics sector, it represents the space cargo occupies within a vehicle.

Why isn't only the actual weight used?

Belongings that are light but take up a lot of space fill the capacity of the carrier vehicle despite having very little weight. Volumetric weight is used to eliminate this imbalance and ensure fair pricing.

What is the calculation standard in international transport?

The most common standards are 3000 for road transport and 5000 for air transport. Example: A box measuring 50x50x40 cm is considered 10 kg dim weight in air transport and 16.6 kg in road transport.

How is box-based calculation performed?

The width, length, and height of each box are taken in cm. The total volume of all boxes is calculated as m3 or total desi to reach the overall shipment value.

How does incorrect calculation affect costs?

If there's a difference between declared dimensions and actual ones, it can lead to changes in freight prices, corrections to customs declarations, and operational delays. Therefore, clear dimension declaration is critical.
