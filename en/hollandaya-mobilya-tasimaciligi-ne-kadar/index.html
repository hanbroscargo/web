Transparent and Economic Pricing in Holland Furniture Logistics

The first question of every customer planning to send furniture abroad is undoubtedly the cost. Although 'Holland furniture shipping pricing' depends on many variables, as Han Bros Cargo, we bring a transparent 'unit price' understanding to the sector. We optimize costs by using Holland's advanced logistics networks and our operational power at the Port of Rotterdam, reflecting this saving directly to our offers. We offer customized price packages according to the nature of your furniture to every point from the heart of Amsterdam to the modern neighborhoods of Eindhoven.

Estimated Price Ranges and Service Packages

The current market averages for 2026 to serve as a guide in your furniture shipments are as follows (your item list is required for a net price):

  • Single Furniture Shipping (1-3 m3): For example a dresser or a single bed; between €150 and €450.
  • Room Based Shipping (5-10 m3): For example a sofa set or just a bedroom; between €800 and €1,800.
  • Complete House Furniture (20-40 m3): All furniture of a 2+1 or 3+1 house; between €2,500 and €5,500.
  • Commercial Shipments from Factory to Address: Special corporate tariffs based on m3 for furniture stores and manufacturers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are prices calculated over cubic meters (m3)?

Yes, the main unit in international furniture logistics is m3. Volume is extracted according to the width, length, and height measurements of your furniture.

Do I pay customs tax for used furniture?

Used furniture that has belonged to you for more than 6 months can be carried tax-free (with exemption) within the scope of 'Personal Effects'.

Is an external elevator (Verhuislift) included in the price?

In buildings where an elevator is mandatory, such as in Amsterdam, this service is offered as an add-on package or included in the main offer depending on the situation.

How does the payment plan work?

Usually, a certain amount of down payment is collected at the moment of loading, and the balance is collected before the items arrive at the Holland address or at the moment of delivery.

Is an extra fee charged for furniture assembly?

Basic disassembly and assembly services are included in the price in our standard shipping packages; please specify for special design/complex products.
