Parcel Shipping Prices from Adana to Wales

The international transport sector requires trust and experience. As Turkey's leading brand in parcel shipping prices from Adana to Wales, we complete your journey starting from Adana to Wales with confidence.

Our Logistics Process from Adana to Wales

We use Mersin Port or road integration for shipments from Adana. The distance between the two points is approximately 3,900 km, and our estimated delivery time is 14-17 days. We serve with a large vehicle fleet from the Çukurova region. We offer special advantages to our customers with economic solutions for large-volume household goods shipments.

Parcel Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our Express Parcel and Package Shipping focused service. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable. Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and entered into the tracking system. Additionally, you can benefit from our 48-72 hour delivery option for urgent shipments.

2026 Pricing Policy

In operations departing from Adana, prices are determined based on the volume (cbm), weight of the goods, and the exact destination address. For loads arriving in Wales, highway tolls and ferry crossing costs are reflected in the price. Please request a free survey for a net figure.

Operation Stages

  • Survey: Volume detection at your address in Adana.
  • Operation: Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and processed in the tracking system.
  • Customs: Export procedures from Adana and import procedures to Wales.
  • Delivery: Scheduled delivery and installation support.
