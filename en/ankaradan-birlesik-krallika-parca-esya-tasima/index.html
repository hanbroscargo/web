Partial Item Shipping from Ankara to the United Kingdom

We offer corporate logistics for your partial item shipping from Ankara to the United Kingdom needs. In this process, which requires a journey of approximately 3,500 km on the Turkey and United Kingdom line, we are by your side, collecting from our Ankara logistics base.

Why Han Bros Cargo?

We act with the motto of attractive offers and protected service. We offer 'All-Risk' insurance guarantee against possible damages. In addition, you can move without experiencing a language barrier or procedural confusion with our experienced staff in partial item shipping processes.

Things to Consider in the Partial Item Shipping Process

Partial item shipping operation is not just about moving goods from one place to another. Especially full compliance with post-Brexit procedures and logistics dynamics via Istanbul or direct affect the process. Our company plans all these stages on your behalf.

Service Detail: We provide a cost advantage by transporting your small amount of belongings in the same vehicle (partial) with other customers' belongings. We also evaluate Truck or Minivan options according to the volume of your belongings.

Logistics Route and Timing

Our transport vehicles reach the destination through all UK ports, moving either via Istanbul or directly. The distance from Ankara to the United Kingdom is approximately 3,500 km, and delivery time may vary depending on customs procedures.

During the journey, you can monitor your load instantly with our vehicle tracking systems and get information 24/7 from our operations team.

TRANSPORTATION STAGES

  • Planning: Free survey and volume determination at your address in Ankara.
  • Preparation: Packaging of all goods according to international standards.
  • Customs Clearance: Preparation of Turkey exit and United Kingdom entry customs declarations.
  • Transfer: Secure journey with a distance of 3,500 km.
  • Delivery: Installation at the new address and collection of packaging waste.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does transport take?

On average, our vehicles departing from Ankara provide delivery within 10-14 days. Our Express service can take 4-6 days.

Are there customs duties?

If you meet the ToR (Transfer of Residence) conditions for personal effects, tax-free transport is possible. Our customs consultants will guide you for partial item shipping.

What does the insurance cover?

Our international transport insurance covers main risks such as accidents, overturning, fire, and theft comprehensively.

Conclusion

Contact us to get professional support for your partial item shipping requests from Ankara to the United Kingdom. As our team, we aim to offer you the best experience by operating from our Ankara logistics base.
