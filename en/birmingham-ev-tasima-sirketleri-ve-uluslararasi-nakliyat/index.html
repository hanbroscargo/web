Han Bros Cargo is with You While Moving to Birmingham

Birmingham, located right in the heart of England and being the country's second largest city, becomes a new starting point for thousands of families and students every year. Why Should You Choose Us on the Birmingham Route?

Birmingham's central location in the 'West Midlands' region is a great advantage for logistics operations. We reflect this advantage to our customers as cost and speed:

  • UK Customs (Post-Brexit) Expertise: We manage your tax exemption applications with our expert team in the ToR (Transfer of Residence) processes for entry of goods to the UK after Brexit.
  • Comprehensive Packaging and Assembly: Your furniture is disassembled by our carpenters, packaged at international standards, and installed in the rooms you want in your new home in Birmingham.
  • Partial Flight Advantage: Thanks to our regular weekly vehicles going to Birmingham, you can move economically without paying full truck prices even if you have only a few boxes or limited belongings.
  • Sigma Certified Security: We offer modern tracking systems and full comprehensive CMR insurance for the road safety of your belongings.

Birmingham Intra-City Distribution and Logistics

Thanks to Birmingham's strategic location on the 'M6' and 'M5' motorways, we provide fast access to all districts throughout Birmingham. Whether it's student belongings going to the Aston University area or antique furniture being moved to a luxury villa in Solihull; we come with equipment and permits suitable for Birmingham's narrow streets and parking protocols for every load. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the house moving time from Turkey to Birmingham?

Depending on customs procedures and ferry/tunnel crossings, delivery from Turkey to your door in Birmingham takes between an average of 12 and 16 days.

Will I pay customs duty while taking belongings to the UK?

If you are settling in Birmingham and have received the necessary ToR (Transfer of Residence) approval, you can take your personal belongings exempt from customs duty.

Do you have a solution for buildings with narrow streets within Birmingham?

Yes, for multi-story buildings in the center of Birmingham, we can deliver to every floor and street with our external facade elevators or small-volume transfer vehicles (vans).

What does the insurance of my belongings cover?

Our CMR insurance covers major risks such as accidents, fire, and theft. With our optional 'All-Risk' insurance, we also secure operational risks such as scratches and breakages.

Do you have a special price for student box shipments?

We have very suitable 'Partial Student Package' prices according to the number of boxes for our students going to universities in Birmingham (UoB, BCU, Aston).
