Professional and Secure Solutions for Furniture Shipping to France

Delivering Turkey's unique furniture designs to your home or office in France requires great logistics expertise. As Han Bros Cargo, with our specialized staff on the France line, we carry your sofa sets, bedrooms, dining rooms, and office furniture from your address in Turkey to all corners of France (Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Strasbourg, etc.) safely. Our 'France Furniture Shipping' service is not just a transport; it is a holistic operation covering special packaging that protects the sensitivity of your furniture, customs clearance, and on-site assembly upon request.

End-to-End Operational Process for Your Furniture

Our steps guaranteeing the safety of your furniture on the France journey:

  • International Packaging Standards: Your furniture is professionally packaged with air bubbles, corner protectors, and 'Kraft' papers durable for the long journey.
  • Customs and Legislation Management: We manage all document and declaration processes (TVA and customs declaration) required for the France entrance on your behalf with our expert brokers.
  • Weekly Regular Flights: With our truck flights organized weekly via road, we deliver your furniture quickly without waiting time.
  • Assembly and Placement Service: At your new address in France, we open the packages of your furniture, perform their setup, and deliver by cleaning the packaging waste.
  • Full COMPREHENSIVE Insurance: All your load is put under financial assurance with CMR insurance against possible risks.

Why Should You Choose Han Bros Cargo?

Furniture shipping is distinguished from general cargo services due to its voluminous and fragile nature. As Han Bros Cargo, we are dominant in the logistics dynamics of the furniture sector. Especially through the logistics bridge we have established from furniture production centers such as 'Inegol, Kayseri, Ankara' to France, we provide the best price and highest quality balance. With our customer support speaking both Turkish and French, we manage the entire process transparently and allow you to track the location of your truck 24/7 via GPS. We are always by your side with all our technical infrastructure for you to move to France with peace or to do furniture shopping.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does it take for furniture to arrive from Turkey to France?

Although it varies depending on customs density, our road shipments are completed in an average of 10 to 15 days.

Do I pay tax when sending furniture to France?

New furniture is taxed in commercial status; however, if you are moving to France within the scope of 'Personal Effects' (used items), you can benefit from tax exemptions.

Can I send only one sofa set?

Yes, with our 'Partial (Groupage)' service, you can send even a single piece by paying only for the space occupied by your item without renting the entire truck.

Who does the assembly of the furniture?

In line with your request, our professional installation teams in France carry your furniture into the room and perform the assembly.

What is done in case of damage?

Every shipment is insured. In case of a possible damage, the insurance process is initiated with the record kept and your financial loss is compensated.
