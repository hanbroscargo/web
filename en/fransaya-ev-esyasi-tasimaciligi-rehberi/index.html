A New Beginning in France: Entrust Your Belongings to Professionals

From Paris, the capital of romance, to Lyon, the gastronomy center, or Marseille, the pearl of the Mediterranean, every corner of France promises a new life. However, on the road to this dream, moving household goods from Turkey to France contains many logistical and legal details. The 'France Relocation Guide' and shipping services we offer as Han Bros Cargo aim to make this process as easy as possible for you. With our decades of European line experience and our command of French and Turkish customs rules, we take only your burden, not just your belongings.

Professional Steps on the France Road

The roadmap we follow for a safe and trouble-free shipping process:

  • Comprehensive Customs Consultancy: We provide expert support in preparing the documents required for you to benefit from the 'personal effects' exemption when moving to France (Transfer of Residence - TOR).
  • Packaging at International Standards: We use double-layer wrapping and shock-absorbing materials so that your items are not affected by vibrations during a journey of thousands of kilometers.
  • Weekly Regular Flights: We guarantee fast delivery by minimizing waiting times with our truck flights to France every week.
  • GPS Supported Live Tracking: You can monitor the journey of your belongings, which country we are in, and the customs status 7/24 via our mobile system.
  • Local Delivery and Installation: We perform the assembly of your furniture at your new address in France and offer you a turnkey home by cleaning up the packaging waste.

Why France with Han Bros Cargo?

France logistics requires experience especially due to 'Kamuran' (restricted entrance) zones and narrow street buildings in large metropolises. As Han Bros Cargo, we perform trouble-free delivery even in challenging cities like Paris with our exterior elevators and small distribution vehicles (shuttle). While putting all your items under financial protection with CMR insurance, we produce flexible solutions with our 'Partial' or 'Complete Truck' options suitable for every budget. Our operations team speaking both Turkish and French ensures that processes proceed fluently at local customs. We are always by your side as your logistics partner to turn your France dream into reality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does it take for household goods to go from Turkey to France?

Our shipments via road are completed in an average of 10 to 14 business days depending on customs density.

Who does the customs procedures?

Our expert customs brokers manage all customs and declaration processes on your behalf in line with the power of attorney you provide.

Can I take all kinds of items to France?

Furniture, white goods, and home textiles for your personal use can be moved within the scope of 'personal effects'. However, flammable, explosive materials, and commercial commodities are subject to different rules.

Are my items insured?

Yes, every shipment is within the scope of international CMR insurance. We can also arrange an 'All-Risk' full-coverage insurance policy upon request.

Can trucks enter every street in France?

No, especially in historic centers, truck entry is prohibited. In these cases, we deliver your items to the door by transferring them to small vehicles in our warehouse (shuttling).
