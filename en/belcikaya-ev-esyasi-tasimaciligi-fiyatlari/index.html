Factors Affecting Household Goods Moving Costs to Belgium

Planning a budget when moving from Turkey to Belgium (Antwerp, Brussels, Ghent, etc.) is one of the most critical stages of the process. As Han Bros Cargo, we apply a transparent and competitive pricing model that contains no hidden fees. Our Belgium household goods shipping prices vary according to the volume your belongings occupy in the truck (cubic meter/m3), the scope of the service to be offered, and the characteristics of your arrival address.

Belgium Shipping Price Calculation Method

Pricing in international house-to-house moving is usually volume-based. Here are the basic factors shaping your cost:

  • Volume (m3): The total space occupied by your belongings is the main determinant of the price. Since the average volumes of 1+1, 2+1, or 3+1 houses differ, calculation is done over the m3 unit price.
  • Service Package: There are price differences between only shipping (Door-to-terminal), standard (Door-to-door), or full-service (Including Packaging and Assembly) options.
  • Customs Procedures: Preparation of the documents required for customs exemption in Belgium and consultancy services are included in our quote; however, additional taxes that may occur (if there's no exemption) are specified separately.
  • Location Advantage: Shipments to logistics centers like Antwerp and its surroundings can have more economical freight prices compared to rural areas in further inland parts.

'Han Bros' Tips for Cost Savings

To make your Belgium move more economical, you can use our partial (piece goods) transport option. Instead of renting the entire truck, this system where the truck is shared with other customers by paying only for the volume of your items reduces costs by up to 30%. Additionally, you can benefit from our special discounts for early booking and off-season moves.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the average house moving prices to Belgium?

Although it varies according to volume, partial shipments start from 1,500€, while full truck shipments can go up to the 4,000€ - 6,000€ band depending on distance and additional services.

How is the cubic meter (m3) calculation done?

The total volume is found by taking 'Width x Length x Height' measurements of each of your belongings. You can learn your net m3 information with our free online survey.

Are customs clearance costs included in the freight price?

Yes, Turkey export customs clearance and Belgium arrival customs consultancy are included in the price in our standard packages. Local port costs to be paid in Belgium are usually specified in the quote.

Do I pay an extra fee for transport insurance?

CMR responsibility insurance is included in all our shipping quotes. However, 'All-Risk' commodity insurance made for high-value items is added for a small premium over the item value.

Is a special discount applied for students?

We have special 'Education Support' discounts for box shipments of our students going to universities in cities like Brussels or Ghent.
