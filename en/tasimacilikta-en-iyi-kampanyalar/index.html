A Shipment Full of Opportunities: Current Shipping Campaigns

As Han Bros Cargo, we develop seasonal and category-based campaigns for you to use your budget in the most efficient way while planning logistics processes. On our 'Best Campaigns in Shipping' page, we bring together the most current opportunities that will reduce your costs on the Turkey-Europe line, expand the scope of service, and increase your operational speed. Our aim is to make quality logistics service not just a transport, but also a profitable investment for our customers.

Star Opportunities in the Han Bros Cargo Campaign World

Our campaigns that are currently active or repeated at regular periods are:

  • Spring Cleaning Campaign: Every year in March and April, up to 20% extra discounts on packaging services in door-to-door moves.
  • SME Friendly Logistics Package: 'Customs Consultancy Gift' campaign for SMEs that will export abroad for the first time.
  • Bring a Friend Discount: Freight credit for both parties in case our existing customers who receive service from us direct an acquaintance to us.
  • University and Education Migration Support: Special suitcase/box shipping campaigns for academic staff and students moving abroad for master's or doctorate.

How Can You Be Informed and Benefit from Our Campaigns?

To not miss the best campaign opportunities, it is enough to follow our digital channels. By participating in the newsletter membership on our website, you can be the first to reach discount codes assigned specifically to you and limited-time flash opportunities. Also, with the 'Route of the Week' campaigns we share on our social media accounts, you can catch very surprising freight prices on the routes that are most intense that week (for example, Berlin-Ankara or Brussels-Istanbul). Remember, when the right campaign is combined with the right time, logistics is no longer an expense but a strategic advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I use the campaign codes?

You can enter the campaign codes sent to you into the 'Promotion Code' field on the quote form on our website or have them reflected on your invoice by specifying them to our customer representative.

How often are campaigns updated?

New period campaigns are announced every month. We also perform special 'Flash Campaigns' on religious holidays, New Year's Eve, and logistics season transitions.

Do you have special package campaigns for corporate firms?

Yes, we have corporate discount campaigns such as 'Gold Package' and 'Platinum Package' for firms that perform regular shipments or over a certain tonnage every month.

Does the insurance coverage change when I get a campaign/promotion price?

No, according to Han Bros Cargo standards, all shipments within the scope of the campaign are protected to the same degree with full comprehensive CMR insurance.

For which countries are there more shipment campaigns?

Much more frequent and advantageous campaigns are organized for Germany, Belgium, France, Austria, and the Netherlands routes where our network is strongest.
