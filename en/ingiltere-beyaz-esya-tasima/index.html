UK White Goods Transport

Han Bros Cargo offers professional white goods transport services to the UK. We provide special packing, safe transport and installation support for refrigerators, washing machines, dishwashers and all white goods.

Our White Goods Transport Services

  • Special Packing: Special packing materials and protection techniques for your white goods
  • Safe Transport: Careful transport operations with professional equipment and team
  • Installation Support: Installation and connection support after delivery
  • Insured Transport: All your white goods are protected with comprehensive insurance
  • All Brands: Service for all brands and models of white goods

White Goods Transport Process

Our white goods transport process consists of the following steps:

  • Inspection and Planning: White goods type, dimensions and delivery points are determined
  • Packing: Careful packing process with special packing materials
  • Loading: Safe loading and securing by professional team
  • Transport: Safe and insured shipping process
  • Delivery: Door to door delivery and installation support

Special Packing Techniques

We use special packing techniques for safe transport of your white goods. We protect your items with foam protection, styrofoam packaging and special box systems.

Installation Support

We offer installation and connection support after delivery. Professional team places your white goods and makes connections.

  • Professional team and modern equipment
  • Comprehensive insurance coverage and safe transport
  • Installation and connection support
  • On-time delivery guarantee
