Vehicle Transport from Turkey to UK

Han Bros Cargo offers professional vehicle transport services from Turkey to the UK. We safely transport your cars, SUVs, motorcycles and commercial vehicles to the UK.

Vehicle Transport Solutions

  • Enclosed Vehicle Transport: Fully protected container transport for valuable and luxury vehicles
  • Open Vehicle Transport: Economic solution, multi-vehicle transport option
  • Special Vehicle Carriers: Special equipment for large SUVs and commercial vehicles
  • Motorcycle Transport: Special securing systems for motorcycles and scooters
  • Roll-On Roll-Off (RORO): Direct vehicle loading via sea route

Vehicle Transport Process

  1. Inspection and Pricing: Transparent price quote based on vehicle type, distance and transport mode
  2. Vehicle Preparation: Vehicle cleaning, fuel level check and damage report
  3. Document Preparation: Registration, insurance, customs documents and Brexit documentation
  4. Loading: Professional loading, securing and protection operations
  5. Customs Procedures: Post-Brexit customs procedures and tax calculations
  6. Delivery: Door to door delivery in the UK and vehicle inspection

Post-Brexit Vehicle Transport

Vehicle transport to the UK after Brexit requires special documentation and procedures. Different customs codes, VED (Vehicle Excise Duty) and CO2 emission taxes are applied depending on vehicle type. Our professional team manages all these processes.
