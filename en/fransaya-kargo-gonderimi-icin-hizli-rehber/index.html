Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does it take for cargo from Turkey to France to arrive?

With our standard road service, delivery is made in 7-10 days, and with our express services, it takes an average of 3-5 business days.

Which products are prohibited at customs?

Flammable materials, fresh food products, and internationally prohibited commodities cannot be sent by cargo. Personal belongings and gifts usually pass without any problems.

How do I find out where my cargo is?

With the 'Track & Trace' (Tracking) number defined specifically for you, you can see the instantaneous location of your package from the panel on our website.

According to what is the shipping fee calculated?

Pricing is done according to which of the actual weight or volumetric weight (desi) of the package is larger and depending on the length of the route.

Do you perform pickups from home?

Yes, we receive your cargo from your door with our courier network across Turkey and provide door-to-door delivery to the new address in France.
