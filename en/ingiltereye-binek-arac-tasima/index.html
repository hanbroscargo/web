Passenger Car Transport to UK

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer special solutions for passenger car transport to UK. We prepare special transport solutions for sedan, hatchback and all passenger car models. We transport your vehicles safely with enclosed and open vehicle transport options.

Our Service Scope

  • Sedan Transport: Special transport solutions for sedan cars
  • Hatchback Transport: Suitable transport options for hatchback cars
  • SUV Transport: Transport service for SUV and crossover vehicles
  • Enclosed Transport: Your vehicles are transported in enclosed vehicles, protected
  • Open Transport: Economical open vehicle transport option
  • Insurance: Comprehensive vehicle insurance options

Transport Modes

  • Enclosed Vehicle Transport: Your vehicles are transported in enclosed special vehicles, protected from weather conditions and external factors
  • Open Vehicle Transport: Economical open vehicle transport option, more affordable price
  • Container Transport: Safe transport with special container, mid-range price

Transport Process

  1. Application and Information Gathering: Vehicle type, dimensions and delivery points are determined
  2. Transport Planning: Most suitable transport mode and route are determined
  3. Preparation: Vehicle preparation and document check are done
  4. Loading: Your vehicles are loaded safely
  5. Transport: Transport is done with selected transport mode
  6. Tracking: Transport process is tracked throughout
  7. Delivery: Delivered to your address in UK

Enclosed vs Open Transport

With enclosed transport option, your vehicles are completely protected from weather conditions, dust and external factors. Open transport option is a more economical solution. We can determine the most suitable option according to your vehicle type and budget.

Documents and Insurance

  • Vehicle Documents: Registration, insurance and required documents
    • Customs Documents: Customs declaration and customs procedures
  • Shipping Insurance: Comprehensive vehicle insurance options
  • Delivery Documents: Delivery documents and signature process

  • Enclosed and open transport options
  • Safe transport and comprehensive insurance
  • 24/7 tracking and support service
