Transparency in International Logistics: Online Shipment Tracking Panel

As Han Bros Cargo, we act with the principle of 'knowledge is power' and offer our customers the opportunity to monitor the journey of their items second by second. You don't need to wonder which route your loads going from Turkey to the UK, Germany to Luxembourg are on, which border gate they have passed, or when they will be delivered. Our advanced digital infrastructure allows us to track our entire vehicle fleet and cargo packages through a central system. This transparency is not just a technology, but also the most concrete indicator of the trust we give you.

How to Use the Tracking System?

Tracking your shipment with our user-friendly interface takes only a few seconds:

  • Prepare Your Reference Code: Use the special tracking number sent to you at the beginning of the shipment.
  • Visit the Panel: Click the 'Shipment Tracking' button on our website and type your code into the relevant box.
  • Watch the Details: See your cargo's current warehouse entries-exits, customs stages, and vehicle movements with one click.
  • Turn on Notifications: Receive automatic informational SMS and e-mails at important stop changes and before delivery.

Digital Monitoring Advantages

With the Han Bros Cargo tracking system, you don't just find an answer to the 'where' question; you can also see estimated delivery times (ETA) up to date, notice potential waits at customs in advance, and organize your receiver (or preparations at your new home) accordingly. Our tracking panel, encrypted in accordance with data security standards, informs only you by protecting your commercial secrets and personal data. With this system that simplifies complex logistics processes, keep your mind at ease while distances get shorter.

Frequently Asked Questions

I lost my tracking number, what should I do?

You can quickly relearn your tracking number by conveying your name, surname, and shipment date information to our customer service.

Why does the cargo seem to stay at a certain point for a long time?

This situation can usually be caused by customs procedures, resting periods, or loading processes of vehicles at collection centers.

Does live tracking work on mobile devices?

Yes, our shipment tracking system is mobile compatible; it works smoothly on every platform regardless of tablet, phone, or computer.

What should I do when I see the 'Delivered' phrase on the tracking system?

If the item has not reached you yet and you see this phrase, please call our 24/7 support line immediately and request confirmation of the status.

Can I also see the tracking of your local partners abroad?

Yes, our system works end-to-end; you can monitor the entire process from departure in Turkey to the last door in Europe via the Han Bros system.
