UK Commercial Vehicle Transport

UK commercial vehicle transport requires special solutions for businesses to transport their vehicle fleets safely and efficiently. As Han Bros Cargo, we offer professional transport services for vans, minibuses, trucks and all commercial vehicles.

Service Details

  • Van Transport: Special transport solutions for small and medium commercial vehicles.
  • Minibus Shipping: Safe and careful transport services for passenger transport vehicles.
  • Truck Transport: High capacity carriers and special solutions for large commercial vehicles.
  • Special Pricing: Bulk transport and special pricing options for businesses.
  • Insured Transport: All your commercial vehicles are transported under full insurance coverage.

Transport Process

Our commercial vehicle transport process is carefully planned according to businesses' needs and professional support is provided at every step.

  • Quote: Detailed price quote is prepared according to your vehicle specifications.
  • Planning: Transport date and route are determined and appropriate carrier is selected.
  • Preparation: Necessary checks are made to prepare your vehicles for transport.
  • Loading and Transport: Safe loading and transport process is carried out by professional team.
  • Delivery: Controlled delivery and inspection process is carried out at destination.
