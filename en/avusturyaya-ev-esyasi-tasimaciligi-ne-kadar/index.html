Moving Cost to Austria: What Expects Your Pocket?

When moving to Austria, the most frequently asked question is quite natural: "How much will this cost me?" In international door-to-door shipping, prices are determined not by grocery store calculations, but by cubic meter (m3) based logistics calculations. As Han Bros Cargo, with years of experience on the Austria route, we have prepared the clearest cost scheme for you. Whether it is a small student house or a large villa, we definitely have a solution for every budget.

Approximate Costs Based on Apartment Type

Considering the variables in the market (fuel, customs duties, etc.), our estimated packages are as follows:

  • Economic (1+1 Apartment): Generally covers between 10-15 m3 of items. It is the most affordable option using the partial shipping method. Ideal for young couples and students.
  • Standard (2+1 Apartment): Covers between 20-30 m3 volume. It is our most preferred "All-Inclusive" package, which includes packing, disassembly, and customs procedures.
  • Large Family (3+1 and Above): A system where a full truck is allocated or large shared models are applied for volumes of 40 m3 and above.

Hidden Criteria Affecting Cost

Remember that these details can change the price when receiving a quote:

  • Sofa and Furniture Type: L-shaped bulky sofas or classic carved heavy furniture occupy more volume than standard items and require more sensitive packing.
  • Intracity Accessibility: In ancient city centers like Vienna in Austria, streets are narrow. In cases where the truck cannot enter, a small transfer vehicle (shuttle) may need to be used.
  • Customs Exemption: If you have a residence permit and your belongings fall under personal belongings status, you are exempt from tax. Otherwise, the taxes incurred will be added to the cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is insurance included in your prices?

Yes, standard CMR insurance is included in most of the quotes we offer. Depending on the value of your belongings, we can also optionally recommend full comprehensive insurance.

How to get the cheapest Austria shipping price?

You can catch the best price by reserving your items at least 1 month in advance and accepting non-urgent delivery times (via partial method).

Can we pay by credit card?

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer a credit card payment option under certain conditions in order to facilitate cash management.

How much does setting up an elevator in Austria affect the price?

Since elevator use is a local rental service, it is reflected in the invoice as a flat additional fee depending on the building's status.

Does the figure change after receiving the price quote?

Written quotes we give after the survey are fixed throughout the contract as long as you do not change the item list.
