Modern and Secure Home Moving Solutions from Bursa to Belgium

Moving from Bursa, Turkey's industrial locomotive, to Belgium in the center of Europe is now much easier with the expertise of Han Bros Cargo. Bursa's strength in furniture production and its strategic location (Nilufer, Osmangazi, Yildirim, and Inegol) form a perfect operational center for our Belgium shipments. In household goods shipping we perform from Bursa to Belgium (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, etc.), we offer the fastest and safest routes with both road and sea (via Gemlik Port) alternatives.

Our Operational Advantages Originating from Bursa

Privileges we offer thanks to our wide network in Bursa:

  • Gemlik Port Integration: For our customers who prefer sea transport, we offer container solutions directly to Belgium ports via Gemlik.
  • Professional Inegol Furniture Packaging: With the packaging discipline we get from Inegol, the furniture capital of Bursa, we wrap your belongings with materials most resistant to international shipping.
  • Bursa Customs Ease: With our competence before the Bursa Customs Directorate, we quickly nationalize your personal effects declarations starting from Bursa.
  • 24/7 Technical Support: Our operation center in Bursa shares every moment of your vehicle on the Belgium road with you.

Han Bros Assurance on Your Belgium Journey

Belgium is a country that requires meticulousness in terms of settlement rules and customs procedures (E705 document, etc.). As Han Bros Cargo, we take the items we receive from your home in Bursa, carry them up to your rooms at your new address in Belgium, perform furniture assembly, and complete the process by collecting packaging waste. We prevent extra costs by guiding you through necessary 'ToR' and other EU legal processes for customs exemption.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are house moving prices from Bursa to Belgium determined according to?

Prices are calculated according to the total volume (m3) of your belongings, the loading floor in Bursa, and the distance of the destination address in Belgium.

Do you install my Inegol furniture in my home in Belgium?

Yes, our professional carpenter team disassembles your belongings in Bursa and reassembles them in their original form at your address in Belgium.

Do I need to be in Bursa for customs procedures?

As long as you provide us with a power of attorney and necessary documents, we manage all transactions without you needing to be actually present at customs.

How many days is the average transport time between Bursa and Belgium?

Our road partial flights take an average of 10-14 days, and our full truck shipments take 7-9 days depending on the customs situation.

Do you perform piano or antique item shipping?

Yes, we provide secure shipping from Bursa to Belgium for all kinds of sensitive and valuable items using special hanging systems and reinforced packaging.
