Door-to-Door Moving Service

With years of experience and our professional team, Han Bros Cargo provides international door-to-door moving services. We offer reliable transportation solutions across a wide geography from Turkey to England, Germany, Netherlands, France, Belgium, Austria and Luxembourg.

Our Service Scope

Our door-to-door moving service covers all processes from picking up your belongings from your home to delivering them to your new address. We are with you at every step, from the survey stage to professional packing, customs procedures, insurance and final delivery.

Survey and Planning

In our survey service, which is the first stage of the transportation process, our expert team visits your home and examines your belongings in detail. Volume, weight and special requirements are determined. This way, the most suitable transportation method and price quote are prepared. During the survey, fragile items, antique valuable pieces and products requiring special care are identified.

Professional Packing

We provide professional packing service to ensure your belongings are transported safely. Using special packaging materials, we apply appropriate packing methods for each type of item. Corner protectors for furniture, special boxes for glass items, and anti-static packaging for electronic devices are used. During the packing process, all items are recorded in the inventory list.

Customs Procedures

We fully manage customs procedures, which is one of the most important stages in international transportation. Our expert team, familiar with customs legislation, provides professional support in preparing all documents, arranging customs declarations and following customs procedures. We provide consultancy services on tax and fee calculations, exemption situations and special permits.

Transportation and Logistics

Your belongings are transported in specially designed closed trucks or containers. During transportation, climate control, security measures and insurance coverage are provided. The transportation route is optimized to ensure delivery in the shortest time and safest way. During transportation, you can track the location of your belongings in real-time with our 24/7 tracking system.

Delivery and Assembly

When your belongings arrive at your new address, our professional team performs the delivery. Optional services such as furniture assembly, item placement and packaging waste collection are also offered. After delivery, the condition of your belongings is checked and the insurance process is initiated in case of damage.

Insurance and Guarantee

We offer full insurance coverage in all our transportation services. An insurance policy is issued according to the value of your belongings and full protection is provided during transportation. In case of any damage, the compensation process is managed in coordination with the insurance company.

Customer Support

With our 24/7 customer support line, we are with you at every stage of the transportation process. Our professional team is ready for your questions, concerns or special requests. You can track the status, location and estimated delivery date of your belongings through our online tracking system.

Experience reliable, professional and quality service in door-to-door moving with Han Bros Cargo. Contact us for a free survey and price quote.
