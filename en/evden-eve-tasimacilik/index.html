International Door-to-Door Moving

Han Bros Cargo orchestrates your move from the current home to the final destination through a single coordination channel. Our experienced teams safeguard every item while completing customs and delivery milestones without disruption.

Planning and Coordination

  • Survey: Inventory, building access and special requirements are assessed in detail.
  • Timeline: Packing, loading, customs and delivery dates are scheduled with clear milestones.
  • Communication: A dedicated project manager keeps you informed throughout the journey.

Packing and Protection

  • Professional Materials: Layered protection is applied with materials selected for volume and fragility.
  • Furniture Handling: Technicians dismantle and reassemble furnishings at destination.
  • Label System: Barcoded labelling directs each box to the correct room.

Logistics Flow

  1. Preparation: Photography, inventory documentation and insurance registration are completed.
  2. Loading: Goods are transferred to vehicles equipped with shock-absorbing systems.
  3. Transit: Customs files, permits and tracking updates are issued regularly.
  4. Delivery: Items are positioned and assembled according to the room plan.

Why Han Bros Cargo?

  • Expertise: Proven moves across European and American destinations.
  • Customs Management: Skilled advisors handle documentation and compliance.
  • Constant Support: Real-time updates and advisory services from start to finish.
  • Insurance: Comprehensive policies protect your belongings end-to-end.
