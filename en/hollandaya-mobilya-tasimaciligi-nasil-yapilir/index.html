How to Ship Furniture to Netherlands? Step-by-Step Process Guide

Professional Workflow in Furniture Shipping to Holland

Moving furniture from Turkey to Holland (Netherlands) is more than a simple shipping process; it requires technical knowledge, customs expertise, and meticulous operational planning. As Han Bros Cargo, we manage the process from the moment your furniture is picked up from your home or manufacturer in Turkey until it is placed in your new room in Holland with the 'Zero Error' principle. The answer to the question 'how is furniture shipping to Holland done?' is hidden in 5 basic stages that we have optimized over the years. This process ensures that your items travel in the safest way, both legally and physically.

Step-by-Step Regulation and Logistics Processes

The Holland journey of your furniture passes through these stages:

  • Appraisal and Planning: The volume (m3) of your items, the degree of sensitivity, and the customs regime (personal effects or commercial?) are determined.
  • Professional Disassembly and Packaging: Your large furniture is disassembled by our carpenters. It is packed in 'International Shipping' standards using bubble wrap, kraft paper, and corner protectors.
  • Loading and Customs Preparation: Packed furniture is stacked in our trucks. Inventory lists and declarations required for Dutch customs are prepared by our expert team.
  • International Transfer: Our truck reaches the Dutch border in an average of 7-10 days by following the Balkans and Central Europe route.
  • Site Delivery and Installation: At your address in Amsterdam, Rotterdam, or Utrecht, your furniture is carefully carried inside, installed, and delivery is completed by cleaning packaging wastes.

Special Delivery Solutions for Dutch Houses

The characteristic narrow entrance houses and steep stairs of Holland are elements that can make furniture delivery difficult. As Han Bros Cargo, we work with local 'Verhuislift' (External Elevator) partners in Amsterdam and Rotterdam for such cases. Taking your furniture in from the balcony or window both prevents damage to the building structure and eliminates the risk of your furniture being scratched. We don't just carry; we offer engineering solutions that adapt to the local conditions of Holland. Your furniture, which are the most important parts of your new life, are in safe hands with Han Bros assurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an invoice required for newly purchased furniture?

If the furniture is brand new, an invoice is mandatory for customs. Used furniture older than 6 months can be carried tax-free within the scope of 'Personal Effects'.

Is the installation service included in the price?

Yes, unless otherwise stated, our standard offers include 'disassembly' and 'installation' services.

How many days does furniture shipping to Holland take?

Delivery is made within an average of 10-14 calendar days from the departure of Turkey, including customs procedures.

What kind of precaution do you take for sensitive glass furniture?

We provide maximum security by offering special 'wooden crating' service for glass lids and mirrors.

Will my furniture be checked at Dutch customs?

The customs administration can open and check your packages according to risk analysis. Our expert consultants accompany this process and accelerate the process.
