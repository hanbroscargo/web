Bristol Cargo Shipping and Logistics Guide

Secure and Fast Logistics Line in Your Bristol Shipments

Bristol, the largest trade and art center in the southwest of England, is an important destination for cargos from Turkey. As Han Bros Cargo, we deliver your shipments with the most optimized routes using the strategic location of the Port of Bristol and Bristol Airport (BRS) connections. Whether it's a commercial export item or a box of personal value; 'speed and trust' is our primary priority on the Bristol route.

Shipping Models and Processes to Bristol

Our cargo options that we customize according to your need are as follows:

  • Road Partial Cargo: With our weekly regular trucks, we deliver your small amounts of cargo to your address in Bristol without entering full truck costs.
  • Express Air: We provide delivery within 2-3 business days via Bristol airport for your critically important documents or light packages.
  • Customs Clearance Services: We provide document control and consultancy support compliant with UK regulations so that you don't get stuck at Bristol customs.
  • Regional Distribution Network: Besides Bristol city center, we also perform door delivery to surrounding regions such as Bath, Weston-super-Mare, and Gloucester.

Digital Tracking and Transparent Pricing in Logistics

While sending cargo to Bristol with Han Bros Cargo, you can monitor every moment of your shipment through our tracking system. We do not include hidden fees in our pricing policy; we share freight, customs, and insurance items transparently. Considering Bristol's narrow historic streets and heavy traffic, we provide service up to the point closest to your address with our small distribution vehicles at the last stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does cargo shipping to Bristol take on average?

Shipments from Turkey to Bristol via road take between an average of 12 and 15 days depending on customs transitions; and air takes 2-3 days.

Which products are prohibited at Bristol customs?

Sharp tools, explosives, and unauthorized food products whose entry is prohibited to the UK are also valid for Bristol customs. You can consult our expert for a detailed list.

Do you handle the insurance of my cargo?

Yes, all our Bristol shipments are protected by CMR insurance. We can also add 'Commodity Insurance' for your valuable cargos.

Do you deliver Bristol airport cargos to the door?

Yes, we perform customs discharge of cargos coming by air and deliver to your final address in Bristol with our 'Last Mile' vehicle.

What are the cargo fees calculated according to?

Fees are determined according to the weight and volume (desi) of the cargo and the distance of the delivery address to Bristol center.
