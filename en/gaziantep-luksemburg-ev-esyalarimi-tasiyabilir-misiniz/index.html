Gaziantep's Power to the Heart of Luxembourg: Household Goods Shipping

Moving from a city like Gaziantep, the heart of industry and trade, to Luxembourg, the financial center of Europe, requires meticulous logistics planning. As Han Bros Cargo, we combine our local field experience in Gaziantep with our international transportation standards. We pick up all your household appliances from your furniture to white goods, from antiques to personal items from your address in Gaziantep and deliver them safely to the remotest corners of Luxembourg. We support our speed in Luxembourg's customs processes, known for its small country advantage, with our modern vehicle fleet and experienced personnel.

Why Should You Choose Us on the Gaziantep - Luxembourg Line?

The advantages we offer on this special route are as follows:

  • Fast Customs and Central Location: Thanks to Luxembourg's central location, your belongings reach the target address much faster from our European distribution center.
  • Professional Packaging Service: Our expert team in Gaziantep packages your belongings with special materials that are shock-absorbing and moisture-preventing, suitable for international long-distance conditions.
  • Full Comprehensive Insurance: Throughout the journey, your belongings are secured against all kinds of risks with CMR and additional personal effects insurance.

Moving from Gaziantep to Luxembourg with Han Bros Cargo is no longer a source of stress, but a pleasant start.

Passport copy, residence permit, item list and documents showing the moving date in Gaziantep are usually sufficient for customs exemption.

Yes, even if you only have a few pieces of belongings that need to go from Gaziantep to Luxembourg, you can send them at affordable prices with our partial transport service.

Absolutely. We offer special crating service in Gaziantep for these types of special items and deliver them with great meticulousness in Luxembourg.
