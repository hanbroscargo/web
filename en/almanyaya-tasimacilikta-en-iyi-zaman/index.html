Why is Timing Important for Shipping to Germany?

Success in international transport operations is related not only to choosing the right company but also to determining the right time. With over 20 years of experience at Han Bros Cargo, we have observed that seasonal fluctuations can affect both shipping prices and delivery times by up to 30%. When sending items or vehicles from Turkey to Germany, shifting your schedule by a few weeks can provide significant savings in your budget.

Seasonal Peaks and Their Effects

Periods called "High Season" in the logistics sector are times when demand exceeds supply. On the Germany line, especially between April and September is the busiest period:

  • Summer Months (June-August): Due to schools being on break and vacation periods, residential move demand peaks. During this time, carrier and truck capacities fill up quickly, and prices can rise above the general average.
  • New Year and Holiday Periods: At the end of December and before religious holidays, processing times may extend due to reduced personnel at customs and holiday breaks.
  • Winter Conditions: Heavy snowfall experienced on some routes (especially Balkan crossings) in January and February can lead to 2-4 day delays in road transport.

Periods Providing Cost Advantage

If you are planning a budget-friendly transport, you should target "Off-Season" periods. Generally, October, November, and March are times when logistics traffic is more balanced and companies offer special campaigns. At Han Bros Cargo, we ensure you benefit from partial loading (groupage) advantages at much more affordable prices for early bookings made during these periods.

Tips for Choosing the Right Time

Follow these steps before finalizing your move date:

  1. Book at Least 1 Month in Advance: Early booking not only fixes the price but also ensures the vehicle gets the most suitable order on the route.
  2. Pay Attention to Public Holidays: Check Federal holidays (Feiertage) in Germany. On these days, truck driving bans are in place and customs are closed.
  3. Monitor Weather Reports: Analyze road conditions together with your shipping consultant, especially for winter shipments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest month to move to Germany?

Generally, October and November are the months when the most economical quotes are received since the summer peak has ended and the winter lull has not yet begun.

Is vehicle transport risky in winter?

With our modern logistics fleet and experienced drivers, winter conditions are not an obstacle. Only in cases of heavy snow or storms, short-term waits may occur at customs crossings for safety reasons.

Does early booking affect the price?

Absolutely. At Han Bros Cargo, applications made 4-6 weeks before the shipment date are offered a fixed price guarantee that is more advantageous than current spot market prices.

How do holiday days in Germany affect shipping?

In Germany, heavy vehicle traffic is prohibited on Sundays and public holidays. Therefore, planning should be done to ensure the shipment's arrival date does not coincide with these dates.

Which time frame is suitable for urgent shipments?

For your urgent shipments, following the departure days of our regular weekly services (usually Tuesday and Friday) minimizes the waiting time of your items in the warehouse.

Timing is everything in logistics. Han Bros Cargo consultants are at your service 24/7 to determine the most suitable schedule for you and produce budget-friendly solutions!
