Office Moving Services from Istanbul to Wales

Our team is a leader in office moving services from Istanbul to Wales. Our logistics operation, starting from Istanbul and ending in Wales, is carried out safely through the Edirne Kapıkule border gate.

Key Considerations in Office Moving

Office moving operations are more than just moving goods. Logistics dynamics, especially special delivery in the Wales region and the route via Edirne Kapıkule, affect the process. Our company plans all these stages on your behalf.

Service Detail: We transport your workplace and office furniture and archives to your new address in a planned manner without causing loss of business. We also evaluate Truck or Minivan options based on the volume of your items.

Logistics Route and Timing

Our transport vehicles move through the Edirne Kapıkule border gate and reach the destination via Cardiff/Newport. The distance between Istanbul and Wales is approximately ~3100 km, and delivery times may vary depending on customs procedures.

You can track your cargo in real-time through our vehicle tracking systems and receive 24/7 information from our operations team during the journey.

Why Han Bros Cargo?

We operate with the motto of best prices and insured service. We provide "All-Risk" insurance coverage against potential damages. Additionally, with our experienced staff in office moving processes, you can relocate without facing language barriers or procedural confusion.

Operation Steps

  • Planning: Free survey and volume determination at your address in Istanbul.
  • Preparation: International standard packaging of all goods.
  • Gümrükleme: Preparation of Turkey exit and Wales entry customs declarations.
  • Transfer: A secure ~3100 km journey across Europe.
  • Delivery: Installation at the new address and collection of waste materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the transport take?

On average, our vehicles departing from Istanbul deliver within 10-14 days. Our express service takes 4-6 days.

Are there customs duties?

Tax-free shipping is possible if you meet the ToR (Transfer of Residence) requirements for personal effects. Our customs consultants will guide you through the office moving process.

What does the insurance cover?

Our international transport insurance fully covers major risks such as accidents, overturning, fire, and theft.

Conclusion

Contact us for professional support regarding your office moving requests from Istanbul to Wales. As a company, we aim to provide you with the best experience with our weekly regular express truck departures.
