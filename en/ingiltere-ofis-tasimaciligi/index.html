UK Office Moving

Han Bros Cargo offers professional office moving services to the UK. We serve with professional transport solutions for office equipment, IT infrastructure, furniture and commercial goods, minimal business interruption guarantee.

Our Office Moving Services

  • Office Equipment Transport: Service for desks, chairs, cabinets and all office equipment
  • IT Infrastructure Transport: Special service for computers, servers and network equipment
  • Office Furniture Transport: Special vehicles and equipment for large office furniture
  • Minimal Business Interruption: Business interruption is minimized with weekend or evening moving
  • Installation Support: Furniture and equipment installation support after delivery

Office Moving Process

Our office moving process consists of the following steps:

  • Inspection and Planning: Office inventory, equipment list and moving plan
  • Scheduling: Moving time is planned to minimize business interruption
  • Packing: Special packing and protection for delicate equipment
  • Loading: Safe loading and securing by professional team
  • Transport: Safe and insured shipping process
  • Delivery and Installation: Delivery and installation support at new office
