Budget Planning on Your France Journey: What Determines Shipping Costs?

Thousands of families and professionals move from Turkey to France every year, and the most curious subject in this process is the moving costs. Household goods shipping prices to France vary depending on many factors, from the total volume (m3) of your belongings to the logistics difficulty of your destination in France. As Han Bros Cargo, we offer you the most optimized and transparent price quotes with our deep experience on the France line. We eliminate surprise costs by taking into account every scenario, from the narrow streets in the heart of Paris to the steep slopes in the French Alps or the tourist density of the Côte d'Azur region.

Regional Factors Affecting France Shipping Rates

France's geographical structure and local rules play an important role in pricing:

  • Paris and Metropolitan Areas: Due to 'ZFE' (Low Emission Zone) rules and narrow streets in central Paris, small vehicle transfer (shuttling) may sometimes be required, which may affect the cost.
  • Southern France and Holiday Resorts: In regions such as Nice, Cannes, and Saint-Tropez, operation times may be extended due to road bans and traffic density, especially during the tourism season.
  • Distance and Kilometer-Based Freight: Every kilometer traveled by our trucks departing from Turkey (approximately 3,000 - 3,500 km) is priced based on fuel and highway (Péage) expenses.
  • Item Volume (m3): The basic unit of pricing is m3; however, you can protect your budget by paying only for the space occupied by your items with the partial (groupage) option.

Tips for Economical Moving

To reduce costs while moving to France; we recommend you to take advantage of early booking discounts by finalizing your shipment date at least 1 month in advance. Also, the professional de-assembly and volumetric optimization of your items will ensure that they take up less space inside the vehicle, pulling down the freight rate. In our 'All-Inclusive' packages offered as Han Bros Cargo, customs, insurance, and door delivery expenses are clearly stated; thus, you will not encounter unexpected items at the France border. With our free survey service, we provide professional support for your budget planning by seeing your items on site and making the most accurate m3 calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average price for moving a complete house from Turkey to France?

Prices vary according to the number of rooms in the house and item density (m3); moving a 2+1 house can range between 2,500 EUR and 4,500 EUR depending on the distance and additional services.

Is there a price difference for cities outside Paris (Lyon, Bordeaux, etc.)?

Yes, while cities on the main route (Paris, Lyon) can be more economical, a kilometer difference is applied for distant coastal towns or inland regions.

Are customs duties included in the price quote?

If you are moving within the scope of personal effects (TOR), customs duty is usually exempt between Turkey and France. However, file expenses are included in our quote.

How much can I save with partial shipping?

If you have a small amount of items, you can save up to 50% compared to renting a full truck with the partial (sharing items with others) method.

Is the elevator rental cost included in the price?

It can be included in our package in line with your request; local elevator rental fees in France vary from region to region according to the structure of the buildings.
