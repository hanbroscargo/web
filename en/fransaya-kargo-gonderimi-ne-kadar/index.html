Basic Dynamics Determining France Cargo Fee

Main factors used as a basis while preparing our quote:

  • Volumetric Weight (Desi/M3): The space occupied by your package is compared with its actual weight. According to logistics standards, pricing is done over whichever is higher.
  • Destination City and Region: While cities close to main terminals such as Paris, Lyon, and Marseille can be more economical, a distance difference is applied for coastal towns or mountainous regions in the inner parts.
  • Delivery Speed: While delivery is provided in a few days with our 'Express Service' options, you can send at much more affordable prices in a slightly longer time with 'Economic Service'.
  • Customs and TVA Consultancy: We prevent surprise payments by informing you in advance about TVA (VAT) and customs duty issues that may occur in shipments to France.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of sending a small box to France?

Although it varies according to box dimensions and weight, we have very competitive starting prices for small packages between 1-5 kg.

Do I pay extra tax at French customs?

For commercial products, VAT (TVA) and customs duty may arise according to French laws. However, there may be exemption limits for low-value gifts or samples.

Is there a price difference for cities outside Paris (Lyon, Nice, etc.)?

While major cities on our main routes are priced standardly, a small regional difference is applied for distant distribution points.

How long does it take for me to get a price quote?

You can get a price for your cargo in seconds via the quote form on our website or by calling our customer service.

Is door-to-door delivery included in the cargo fee?

Yes, Han Bros Cargo quotes cover the entire process of pickup from the address in Turkey and delivery to the address in France unless otherwise stated.
