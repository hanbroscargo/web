Austria: Strategic Logistics Center in the Heart of Europe

Austria, one of the most important crossroads of Central Europe, is the key country of the logistics corridor stretching from Turkey to Western Europe. As Han Bros Cargo, we act with the awareness that Austria is not only a transit country but also a high-volume destination. From the artistic texture of Vienna to the industrial power of Graz, from the technological infrastructure of Linz to the tourism potential of Salzburg, we produce special transportation solutions for every state (Länder) of Austria.

Why 'Han Bros Cargo' on the Austria Route?

Austria shipping requires managing the geographical challenges of the Alps and strict European Union regulations. As Han Bros Cargo, we make this route easier for you:

  • Schengen and Customs Expertise: Austria is a Schengen and EU member. This situation makes it mandatory to prepare documents such as ATR and T1 flawlessly in the customs processes of commercial or individual belongings coming from Turkey.
  • Safe Driving with Alpine Discipline: Our vehicle fleet is equipped with low-emission (Euro 6) and high-security equipment suitable for Austria's winter conditions and mountainous roads.
  • Comprehensive Insurance (CMR): Each of your loads going to Austria is under full guarantee within the framework of the international CMR agreement and with additional 'All-Risk' options.
  • Personal Effects Moving Service: For families settling in Austria, we offer customs exemption guidance and door-to-door (up to inside the house in Vienna) turnkey service.

State-Based Logistics Distribution

We have a local partner network in each region such as Lower Austria, Upper Austria, Styria, and Tyrol. With our Vienna-based operations, from the moment your belongings enter the Austria customs, they are evacuated and delivered to your address in compliance with local legislation. Thanks to our regular weekly flights, we minimize waiting time in both partial and full truck loads.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does the house moving process to Austria take on average?

Shipments from Turkey to Austria via road are completed between an average of 7 and 10 business days depending on customs and transit crossings.

Which documents are mandatory at Austria customs?

Invoice and ATR document for commercial loads; change of residence document (Meldezettel) and detailed inventory list for household goods are the basic documents.

Can I send just a few boxes or a piano to Austria?

Yes, thanks to our 'Partial Shipping' service, you can deliver your small-volume loads at economical prices without having to rent a full truck.

What happens if my belongings are damaged during transport?

Your belongings are protected by CMR insurance. Additionally, if you request, we also perform 'All-Risk' transport insurance over the full value of the item.

Do large trucks enter narrow streets in Vienna city center?

Our trucks go as far as the customs point. For narrow streets in Vienna city center, we deliver your belongings to your door by transferring them to small distribution vehicles (Vans).
