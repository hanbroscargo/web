Logistics Comparison Between European Countries: Which Route is Right for You?

When moving from Turkey to Europe or sending commercial goods, each country's unique customs regimes, road taxes, and logistics infrastructure directly affect the process. At Han Bros Cargo, we have compared the main destinations we ship to most. This guide will help you determine the most suitable country or route for your budget and time.

Germany vs. the Netherlands: The Heart of Logistics

These two countries are the regions where loads departing from Turkey go most densely. Germany offers distribution opportunities all over Europe with its central location, while the Netherlands (especially the port of Rotterdam) is unrivaled in maritime and road integration. Customs processes in Germany are very disciplined and fast; in the Netherlands, logistics costs (storage, etc.) are at competitive levels.

United Kingdom (UK): The Brexit Factor

The UK, unlike other European countries, is now outside the EU customs union. This situation requires extra documentation like ToR1 for shipments to the UK. In terms of duration, it is 2-3 days longer than Germany, but with Han Bros Cargo's expertise, these processes are managed as easily as a standard EU shipment.

France and Austria: Geographical Challenges

Austria is a country where logistics times can lengthen in the winter months due to Alpine passes and winter conditions. France, on the other hand, shows differences in delivery times between cities due to its large geography. In both of these countries, local parking permits and the use of elevators within the city are of more critical importance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which European country has the cheapest shipping?

Due to distance and volume density, Bulgaria and Greece are usually the cheapest. However, in Western Europe, Germany and the Netherlands offer the most economical options in partial shipments thanks to high vehicle traffic.

Which country finishes customs procedures the fastest?

Customs clearance procedures are usually finalized within 24 hours in Germany and Belgium, where Han Bros Cargo has its own warehouses and consultants.

Is a single insurance valid for all European countries?

Yes, the CMR insurance we use in our shipments covers transit crossings across the entire European continent starting from Turkey and delivery in the destination country.

What is the average delivery time across Europe?

Our deliveries to Western Europe (Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium) by standard road usually take 10-14 days, while it takes 4-6 days in our express services.

Did Brexit increase UK prices much?

Although there has been some increase in prices due to extra customs and document costs, we reflect this increase to our customers at a minimum level thanks to our logistics operation depth.
