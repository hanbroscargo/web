UK Car Shipping

Han Bros Cargo offers professional car shipping services to the UK. We provide safe transport solutions for all brands and models of vehicles.

Vehicle Transport Options

  • Enclosed Vehicle Transport: Protected transport for valuable and luxury vehicles. Provides complete protection from weather and external factors.
  • Open Vehicle Transport: Economic solution. Affordable transport option for standard vehicles.
  • Special Vehicle Carriers: Specially equipped carriers for large vehicles and SUVs.

UK Vehicle Transport Process

Our vehicle shipping process consists of the following steps:

  • Inspection and Pricing: Transparent price quote based on vehicle type, distance and transport mode
  • Document Preparation: Preparation of vehicle registration, insurance documents and required paperwork
  • Loading: Careful loading and securing by professional team
  • Transport: Safe and insured shipping process
  • Delivery: Door to door delivery and vehicle inspection

Insurance Coverage

All our vehicle shipments are protected with comprehensive insurance. Fast solution and compensation process is guaranteed in case of damage. We offer high value insurance options for valuable vehicles.

Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are important when shipping vehicles to the UK after Brexit. We provide professional support regarding vehicle documents, customs declarations and tax calculations.

Pricing

UK car shipping prices are determined by vehicle type, distance, transport mode (enclosed/open) and additional services. You are informed in advance with transparent pricing.

Why Han Bros Cargo?

  • Experienced team for all brands and models of vehicles
  • Enclosed and open vehicle transport options
  • Comprehensive insurance coverage and safe transport
  • Customs procedures and document preparation support
  • On-time delivery guarantee
