Packing Conditions and Expert Advice for Moving to Belgium

The Vital Importance of Packing in Belgium Moving

In a moving process from Turkey to Belgium, the answer to the question "Is packing required?" is not just yes, it's "Professional packing is mandatory." Considering a road route of approximately 3,000 kilometers, Balkan and Central European crossings, and ultimately strict Belgium customs controls, a load that is not correctly packaged both increases the risk of damage and can cause delays during customs inspection.

Why is Standard Packing Not Enough?

Blanket or simple stretch film wraps used in ordinary domestic shipping are insufficient in international long-haul transport. As Han Bros Cargo, we apply the following packing requirements in Belgium shipments:

  • Customs Standards: Belgium customs officers have the authority to open boxes they find suspicious. Boxes that are packed transparently and orderly, and have their content (Packing List) specified on them, pass through customs much faster.
  • Condensation (Moisture) Protection: Since different climate zones (from Turkey's heat to Europe's humid winter) are crossed along the way, 'Screed' kraft paper and moisture-barrier packaging are used so that the belongings are not affected by moisture.
  • Physical Strength: Due to the overlapping of loads inside the truck and road vibration, double-corrugated (BC wave) hard boxes must be used so that the boxes in the lower layer do not burst.

Things to Consider When Doing Your Own Packing

If you want to pack some items yourself to protect your budget, Han Bros Cargo experts recommend following these rules: Do not fill your boxes to the brim but do not leave gaps either (gaps cause the box to crush). Place heavy items like books in small boxes, and light items like linens in large boxes. Always write short content information in French or English on each box (Kitchenware - Personal Effects, etc.).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Belgium customs open and look at all boxes?

No, usually an X-Ray scan is performed. However, irregular and insufficiently packed boxes have a higher risk of being physically opened as they may raise suspicion of contraband products.

What is done for sensitive electronic devices if there's no original box?

The Han Bros Cargo team applies 'Hard-Box' (hard cardboard armoring) and foam-supported protection for televisions or monitors without an original box.

Does the packing service extend the shipping time?

On the contrary, a professionally packed home usually shortens the total shipping process by 1-2 days thanks to loading speed and customs transition speed.

Do you bring the packing materials?

Yes, if you bought packing-inclusive service, our teams bring everything with them, from 1st class boxes to bubble wraps and hanging textile wardrobes.

Is there a special condition for antique items?

Yes, we recommend 'Crating' (wooden boxing) for furniture carrying antique value. On the way to Belgium, we 100% cut off the item's contact with external impacts.
