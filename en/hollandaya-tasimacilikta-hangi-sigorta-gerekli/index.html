What Insurance Is Required for Shipping to Netherlands?

  • Basic insurance: Covers standard transportation risks.
    • <All-risk insurance: Protects against damage, theft, and natural disasters.
  • Special risk insurance: Tailored coverage for high-value items or specific conditions.
