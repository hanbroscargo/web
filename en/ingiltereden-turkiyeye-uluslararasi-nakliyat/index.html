International Shipping from UK to Turkey

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer comprehensive service for international shipping from UK to Turkey. With our experienced team, we are with you regarding household goods, furniture, vehicle and commercial goods transport. We provide professional support in customs procedures, document preparation and safe delivery.

Our Service Scope

  • Household Goods Transport: Door to door moving service
  • Furniture Transport: Furniture and decoration items transport
  • Vehicle Transport: Passenger car, commercial vehicle and motorcycle transport
  • Commercial Freight Transport: Commercial goods and merchandise transport
  • Customs Procedures: Tracking of post-Brexit customs procedures
  • Document Preparation: All customs documents and papers

Transport Modes

  • Sea Transport: Transport with containers and ro-ro ships
  • Road Transport: Direct delivery with special vehicles
  • Air Transport: Air cargo for fast and urgent shipments
  • Combined Transport: Sea, road and air transport combination

Transport Process

  1. Survey and Planning: Freight inventory is taken and transport plan is created
  2. Document Preparation: Customs documents and required papers are prepared
  3. Packing: Professional packing and packaging is done
  4. Loading: Safe loading process is performed
  5. Transport: Transport is done with selected transport mode
  6. Customs Procedures: Customs procedures are completed in Turkey
  7. Delivery: Safe delivery is made to your address

Customs Procedures

Our professional team manages the changed customs procedures after Brexit. We provide full support in customs declaration, tax calculations and customs tracking.

Document Preparation

  • Customs Declaration: Detailed customs declaration is prepared
  • Invoice and Documents: Commercial invoice and required documents
  • Certificate of Origin: Certificate of origin when needed
  • Insurance Documents: Shipping insurance documents

  • Comprehensive international shipping service
  • Household goods, furniture, vehicle and commercial freight transport
  • Expert experience in post-Brexit customs procedures
  • Professional document preparation and tracking
  • Safe delivery and comprehensive insurance
