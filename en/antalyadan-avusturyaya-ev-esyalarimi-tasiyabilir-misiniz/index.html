Household Goods Shipping from Antalya to Austria: Professional Solutions

Moving from Turkey's tourism capital Antalya to Europe's aesthetic center Austria (Vienna, Graz, Salzburg, Linz) is now much safer with Han Bros Cargo's expertise. In our operations carried out from every point of Antalya (Muratpaşa, Konyaaltı, Alanya, Manavgat, etc.), we develop logistics strategies specialized for Austria's mountainous geography and the Alpine region. Whether on snowy roads in winter or in high traffic in summer, your belongings are entrusted to us.

Points to Consider When Moving to Austria

Austria applies certain customs rules in the shipping of personal belongings (used household items). At Han Bros Cargo, we guide you through this process:

  • Customs Exemption: For your belongings to enter tax-free, they must have been registered in your name and used for at least 6 months. We professionally prepare the inventory list to be presented to Austrian authorities.
  • Alpine Region Logistics: Due to Austria's geographical structure, narrow and sloped roads, and snowy passes in winter can affect the shipment. We offer uninterrupted service with our modern fleet and equipment suitable for winter conditions.
  • Parking Permits: Especially in large cities like Vienna, we provide support in obtaining the necessary permits (Halteverbot) for parking trucks in front of the building on the moving day.

Shipping Options and Insurance

We offer two main shipping methods from Antalya to Austria. For your small-volume items, economical 'Partial (Groupage)' shipping is available, while for full household goods, a private 'Full Truck' option exists. All our shipments are under international CMR insurance, and your belongings are under Han Bros Cargo assurance from loading to delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does shipping from Antalya to Austria take?

The delivery time from Antalya to Austria by road is usually completed within 8 to 12 days, depending on customs processes.

How is the pricing done?

Our pricing is done according to the cubic meter (m3) volume occupied by your items. The more items there are, the lower the unit price per m3. We can determine the net figure with our free survey service.

Who will handle the customs clearance of my items?

Han Bros Cargo's expert customs consultants conclude both Turkey exit and Austria entry procedures on your behalf legally and quickly.

Will there be any problems crossing the German border?

No, our shipments go with a single sealed truck from the moment they enter the EU border until the destination, which maximizes security.

Do you disassemble and reassemble my furniture?

Yes, we perform the disassembly of furniture in your home in Antalya and finish the process by completing the installations in your new home in Austria.
