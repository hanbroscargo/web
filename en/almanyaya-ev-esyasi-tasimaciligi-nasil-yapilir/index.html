Household Goods Shipping to Germany: Step-by-Step Operation Guide

An international moving process is not just about boxes moving from one place to another; it's the result of serious legal and operational planning. At Han Bros Cargo, we implement a standardized roadmap to ensure our customers settling in Germany complete this process without stress. Here are the basic stages of moving from Turkey to Germany.

1. Free Survey and Planning

Every successful moving operation starts with a correct preliminary analysis. Our surveyors come to your address to inspect furniture to be moved, items to be boxed, and sensitive materials on-site. At this stage:

  • Volume Measurement: The net cubic meter (m3) that the items will occupy inside the truck is calculated.
  • Risk Analysis: Elevator requirements, narrow corridors, or special packaging needs are determined.
  • Schedule: Packing day, departure date, and estimated delivery time are clarified.

2. Professional Packaging and Labeling

It should not be forgotten that your belongings will travel thousands of kilometers. Han Bros Cargo teams protect each piece using shock-absorbing bubble wraps, kraft papers, and furniture blankets. Special crating techniques are applied especially for kitchenware and electronic devices. Each box is numbered and labeled in accordance with the customs list (inventory).

3. Customs Clearance and Border Crossings

After your belongings are loaded onto the truck, export customs procedures from Turkey are initiated. If your items are under "personal belongings" status (household items used for at least 6 months), our experts prepare the necessary declarations for tax exemption at German customs. Our vehicle passing through Balkan and Central European countries is sealed when entering the EU border and dispatched to the destination customs in Germany.

4. Delivery to Address and Installation

Your belongings approved by German customs are brought to your door. Furniture parts that were disassembled are reassembled by Han Bros Cargo's local partners or our own teams. Items are placed in relevant rooms, and packaging waste is collected by our team, completing the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does the entire process take in total?

The household goods moving process from Turkey to Germany is usually completed within 10 to 15 days, depending on customs density and traffic conditions.

Should I give power of attorney for customs procedures?

Yes, a notarized customs power of attorney is required for our customs consultants to act on your behalf. A sample text will be provided to you by us.

What happens if my belongings are damaged?

Our entire shipping process is under CMR insurance and additional transport insurance. Damages noticed at the time of delivery are recorded with a report and compensated under the insurance policy.

Do you provide elevator moving services in Germany?

Yes, we can organize external elevator installation through our local partners, especially for old structures in Germany, to prevent damage to belongings.

Which items cannot be taken abroad?

According to customs rules, it is prohibited to transport food items, flammable-explosive chemicals, and brand new products with commercial value under personal belongings status.

You are safe on your Germany journey with Han Bros Cargo. Wake up in your new home with peace of mind thanks to our professional process management and customer-oriented solutions!
