Quick Delivery Models We Offer Between Diyarbakir and Netherlands

Our logistics alternatives you can choose according to your urgency:

  • Minivan Express Shipping: We perform door-to-door delivery from Diyarbakir to the Netherlands in just 4-5 business days with our special panel van vehicles without getting stuck in truck restrictions on the road.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does the fastest cargo from Diyarbakir to Netherlands take?

Delivery is performed within an average of 4-6 days with our minivan express service, and within 2-3 business days with our air cargo service.

Are express cargo prices much more expensive than other methods?

Although the speed factor affects the cost, our minivan service is much more economical than renting a full truck and provides great savings from time.

What is the weight limit for my cargo?

In our air cargo and minivan services, shipments can be made starting from 1 kg in box-based shipments up to minivan capacity (1.3 tons).

How do processes work at Netherlands customs?

Your proforma invoices are digitally processed before your shipment reaches customs, and tax payments and declaration processes are quickly completed by our team.

Do you pick up cargo on the weekend?

Yes, for urgent cases, we can pick up in Diyarbakir on the weekend and start the shipment process immediately.
