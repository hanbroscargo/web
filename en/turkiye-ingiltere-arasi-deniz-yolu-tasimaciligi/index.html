Sea Transport Between Turkey and UK

When shipping by sea between Turkey and UK, if you want economical and reliable service, Han Bros Cargo offers you the most suitable solutions. We are at your service with our expert team in container options, port procedures and customs procedures.

Services Offered

  • Container Shipping: Flexible transport solutions with FCL and LCL container options.
  • Port Procedures: Professional port procedures at Turkish and UK ports.
  • Customs Procedures: All customs procedures and documentation processes.
  • Tracking System: You can track the status of your shipments 24/7.

Shipping Process

Our sea transport process is planned and professional:

  • Quote Preparation: A detailed quote is prepared according to the characteristics of your cargo.
  • Container Selection: FCL or LCL container option is determined.
  • Loading: Safe loading process is carried out at Turkish port.
  • Sea Transport: Sea transport is carried out with reliable shipping companies.
  • Customs Procedures: Customs procedures are completed in UK.
  • Delivery: Your cargo is delivered to the specified address.
