Strategic Planning and Local Knowledge in Holland Furniture Shipping

Moving furniture from Turkey to Holland (Netherlands) is not only about covering distance but progressing without error in one of the densest and most organized logistics networks in Europe. This 'comprehensive guide' we prepared as Han Bros Cargo illuminates every step from your furniture leaving its manufacturer in the Aegean or Marmara until its arrival at the Holland lands famous for Tulip Gardens. Although the geographical structure of Holland (being flat) offers a logistics advantage, the architectural structure in historical city centers (narrow buildings) creates the need for special equipment. Our guide was prepared to turn these dynamics to your favor.

The Main Arteries of Holland Logistics: Rotterdam and Others

The basic elements determining the route in your furniture shipment are as follows:

  • Rotterdam Port Advantage: Rotterdam, the world logistics center, offers unique speed and capacity in sea-road transfers. Temporary accommodation of your furniture in our warehouses here lowers the costs.
  • Amsterdam & The Hague Dynamics: The narrow streets and historical texture in these cities make the use of 'small vehicles' and 'elevators' mandatory in deliveries. Han Bros provides access to this equipment at any moment.
  • Flat Geography & Highway Network: The excellent highway infrastructure of Holland minimizes the vibration of your furniture on the truck and increases the possibility of 'just-in-time' (Just-in-Time) delivery.
  • Eindhoven & Utrecht Logistics Hubs: Shipments made to these regions have strategic importance in terms of distribution speed in land transportation.

Flawless Delivery in Holland with Han Bros Cargo

We don't just carry your furniture, we make them parts of your living space in Holland. Our expert teams prepare all 'Inventory' lists required for personal effects exemption (Verhuizing) at Dutch customs. During delivery, every detail from the screws of your furniture separated during disassembly to the installation diagrams is under our control. Thanks to our storage areas in Rotterdam, if your house is not ready yet, we can keep your furniture in safe environments away from humidity. For us, every sofa set, every bedroom is the beginning of a new life and it is our duty to perfect this beginning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an elevator mandatory for furniture delivery in Holland?

Since the stairs in historical Amsterdam and Rotterdam buildings are very narrow and steep, the use of 'Verhuislift' (External elevator) is mandatory by 90%.

Do I need to have my own furniture insured?

Han Bros Cargo protects every shipment with CMR insurance, but we recommend you to have an additional 'All-Risk' policy for your very valuable/antique furniture.

Which documents are mandatory for Holland shipping?

Photocopy of identity card, item list (packing list), residence certificate or employment contract are the basic documents for customs.

Should I choose partial shipping or a complete truck?

If your furniture is less than 20 m3, the partial (groupage) option is much more economical. In shipments over 30 m3, we can allocate a special complete truck for you.

Does VAT occur in second-hand furniture?

Furniture taken for personal use and used for at least 6 months are exempt from VAT and customs duty.
