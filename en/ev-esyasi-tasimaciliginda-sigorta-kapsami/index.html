Your Belongings' Safety is Our Priority: Insurance Solutions

International household goods shipping covers a long journey of thousands of kilometers and different customs transitions. In this process, the physical safety of your items is as important as having financial assurance against possible risks. The 'Comprehensive Insurance Packages' we offer as Han Bros Cargo protect your belongings against all external factors from the moment of loading to the moment of delivery. While taking the most advanced security measures in our modern logistics network, we ensure our customers' peace of mind with insurance support.

What is in Our Insurance Coverage?

Policy details offered with Han Bros Cargo assurance:

  • All-Risk Policy: It is the highest level of protection method covering fire, accident, natural disasters, and all large-scale physical damages that may occur during transport.
  • CMR Insurance: Within the scope of the international CMR contract, which is standard in road transport, each of your cargoes is under legal assurance within the transport limits.
  • Damage Assessment and Compensation Process: In case of a possible damage, our professional expert team reports in a very short time, initiates the compensation process, and immediately removes the customer's victimization.
  • Full Value Insurance: We protect your financial loss by 100% by creating special policies based on declarations reflecting the true market value of your belongings.

Address of Assurance: Han Bros Professionalism

Insurance is not just a piece of paper, it is the guarantee of the promise given by a logistics firm. As Han Bros Cargo, we see each of your items as our own trust. The 'Packaging at International Standard' service performed by our trained personnel is actually the most important part of the insurance process; because preventing damage before it occurs is our basic vision. We keep operational security at the highest level by monitoring our vehicles 24/7 with our GPS tracking system. Our insurance policies are prepared in cooperation with respected insurance organizations with worldwide validity. Thus, our expatriate citizens or international working customers focus only on their new addresses while delivering their memories to us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it mandatory to have insurance?

In international shipping, CMR insurance is standard as a minimum; however, it is recommended to have an individual policy for full protection.

What should I do in case of damage?

You should check your items during delivery and if you notice a damage, you must have the transport personnel keep a record and report the situation to us within 24 hours.

Can private collection or antique items be insured?

Yes, for special items such as antiques or high-value paintings, we create a special 'Valuable Goods' policy in line with the expertise report.

Is the insurance fee included in the shipping price?

Standard shipping insurances are usually included in our quote, but broad packages such as 'All-Risk' are calculated separately according to the declared value of the item.

How long does the compensation payment take?

After the necessary documents and expert report are completed, the payment is usually made within 15-30 business days with the approval of the insurance company.
