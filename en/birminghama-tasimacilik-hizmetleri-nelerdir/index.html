Han Bros Cargo Expertise in the Birmingham Logistics Network

Birmingham, the industrial heart and transportation center of England, is a strategic node for shipments from Turkey. As Han Bros Cargo, with our deep logistics network in Birmingham, we deliver all kinds of cargo from your commercial loads to your personal belongings safely and quickly. The transportation services we offer to all regions of Birmingham (including B1-B99 postcodes) are not just a transport, but a full-scale solution partner support.

Multimodal Transport Options for Birmingham

We use different logistics channels according to the urgency of your load and your budget:

  • Road Transport (Weekly Flights): Our trucks departing from Turkey reach our Birmingham warehouse directly via ferry/tunnel connection through France or Belgium. It is the most economical and commonly used method.
  • Air Cargo (Express Service): For very urgent samples or valuable items, we provide fast customs clearance and door delivery through Birmingham Airport (BHX).
  • Customs and Legislation Support: We fully comply with the UK import rules changing after Brexit and manage your ToR1 or commercial invoice processes flawlessly.
  • Intra-City Distribution and Storage: From our customs-bonded warehouses in Birmingham, we distribute your loads to your addresses in the city center (Aston, Bullring, Digbeth, etc.) with our small van vehicles.

Trust and Technology in Logistics

Transparency is our most important principle in our Birmingham shipping services. With our GPS-based vehicle tracking system, you can see where your cargo is instantly. Additionally, our UK teams experienced in Birmingham's local traffic restrictions and parking bans coordinate the entire process from municipal permits to equipment supply so that no problems occur at the unloading address.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for loads going to Birmingham to clear customs?

In case documents are complete, customs procedures for road loads are usually completed within 1-2 business days at ports or bonded warehouses.

Do you have storage facilities in Birmingham?

Yes, you can safely keep your belongings for short or long terms in our customs-bonded and free warehouses located near Birmingham.

Are UK taxes included in the freight price?

The freight price covers the shipping service. If you don't have a tax exemption (ToR), VAT and fees requested by the UK customs are paid by the customer (excluding our DDP service).

Do you perform special transport for sensitive technological products?

Yes, we provide shipping using suspended vehicles and anti-static packaging products for IT devices or sensitive machines.

Do you pick up Birmingham airport cargos from the address?

Yes, in case the cargo comes by air, we can perform customs discharge from BHX airport and deliver it to your final address in Birmingham.
