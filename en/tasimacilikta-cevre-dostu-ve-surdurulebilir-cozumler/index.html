Eco-Friendly Logistics and Sustainable Transport Solutions

Sustainable Logistics Transformation for Future Generations

The logistics industry, the lifeblood of world trade, is also one of the areas with the highest environmental impact. As Han Bros Cargo, we adopt the 'Green Logistics' model in order to minimize the effects of our shipping activities on nature and build a sustainable future. Sustainable transport is not only an eco-friendly choice but also a strategic responsibility that increases operational efficiency and contributes to global climate goals.

Green Logistics Practices at Han Bros Cargo

The sustainable steps we have integrated into our logistics processes to protect nature are as follows:

  • Low-Emission Vehicle Fleet: We use modern vehicles with Euro 6 and above emission standards to reduce carbon emissions in road transport.
  • Recyclable Packaging Systems: We take care that paper, cardboard, and plastic materials used in moving processes are highly recyclable.
  • Route Optimization: We reduce fuel consumption by planning the shortest and most efficient routes and prevent our trucks from making empty kilometers.
  • Intermodal Transport: By integrating sea and road, we reduce the carbon footprint in long-distance shipments by up to 40%.

Our Sustainability Vision and Social Responsibility

We plan not only today but also tomorrow in our logistics processes. Thanks to our paperless office and digital customs processes, we save tons of paper annually. Additionally, we develop projects aiming to increase environmental awareness with each of our successful international shipments. With the sustainable reporting options we offer to our business partners, we support our corporate customers in tracking their own carbon footprints and reaching their green supply chain goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does green logistics increase transport costs?

On the contrary, route optimization and efficient fuel use reduce operational costs in the long run, allowing us to offer more competitive prices to our customers.

Are your packaging materials biodegradable?

Yes, a large part of the boxes we use are produced from recycled paper and are regained to the economy in our waste management process.

How can I track my carbon footprint?

In the special logistics reports we prepare for our corporate customers, we share estimated carbon emission data per shipment.

Why is intermodal transport more eco-friendly?

Since it combines high-capacity transport modes such as ships and trains, it significantly reduces the amount of emission per truck.

What is Han Bros Cargo's sustainability goal?

Our goal is to be one of the leading green logistics companies in the sector by reducing our operational carbon emissions by 50% until 2030.
