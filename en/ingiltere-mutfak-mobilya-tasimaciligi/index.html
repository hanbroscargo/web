UK Kitchen Furniture Transport

Han Bros Cargo offers specialized expertise in kitchen furniture transport to the UK. We are experienced in moving special items like kitchen cabinets, countertops, wall units and kitchen storage systems.

Kitchen Furniture Transport Features

  • Special Protection Systems: Special corner protectors and wrapping techniques for countertops and granite surfaces
  • Modular System Transport: Safe transport by disassembling kitchen modules into parts
  • Appliance Integration: Protected transport of built-in ovens, hoods and dishwashers
  • Granite and Marble Transport: Special transport solutions for delicate natural stone surfaces
  • Installation Service: Post-delivery installation support option in the UK

Kitchen Furniture Transport Process

  1. Pre-Inspection: Determining kitchen dimensions and special requirements
  2. Disassembly Plan: Detailed planning for safe dismantling
  3. Protective Packing: Individual protection and labeling for each piece
  4. Special Transport Container: Dedicated section for kitchen items
  5. Customs Procedures: Special document preparation for kitchen items
  6. Installation Support: Installation service in the UK if needed

Why Han Bros Cargo for Kitchen Furniture Transport?

  • Special transport techniques and experience for kitchen furniture
  • Expert approach for granite, marble and natural stone surfaces
  • Safe integration of electrical kitchen appliances
  • Technical knowledge in modular system transport
  • Full service package with installation support
