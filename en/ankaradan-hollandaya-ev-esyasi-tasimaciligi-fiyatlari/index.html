Moving Prices from Ankara to Netherlands 2026

Moving Household Goods from Ankara to the Netherlands: Transparent Pricing Guide

Moving from Turkey's capital Ankara to the heart of Europe, the Netherlands (Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Eindhoven, etc.), can turn into a very easy process with the right logistics planning. At Han Bros Cargo, with our Ankara-based operational network, we offer the most economical and reliable solutions for your household items going to the Netherlands. In this guide, we detail the current shipping costs for 2026 and the factors affecting prices.

Basic Criteria Determining Costs

When receiving a shipping quote on the Ankara-Netherlands line, the figures you encounter are shaped according to these criteria:

  • Volume (m3): The net space your belongings occupy inside the truck. The unit price is usually calculated per m3.
  • Customs Services: Exemption applications we will make under personal belongings status and customs consultancy services are included in the price.
  • Route and Freight: Transportation costs of vehicles departing from Ankara reaching the Netherlands via the Balkan route through the Kapıkule border gate.
  • Packaging and Assembly: Disassembly and packaging services performed by our professional teams are optionally added to the packages.

Moving Packages from Ankara to the Netherlands

We offer three different service levels suitable for every budget:

  1. Economy (Partial) Package: A budget-friendly option where you share road costs with other senders for small amounts of belongings.
  2. Standard Package: The most preferred module including wrapping items, customs clearance, and door-to-door delivery.
  3. VIP Package: Turnkey moving; covers professional packaging, furniture installation, and elevator moving services.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does shipping from Ankara to the Netherlands take?

Delivery time from Ankara to the Netherlands by road usually varies between 12 and 15 days, depending on customs density.

Will I pay customs duty?

If your belongings fall under personal items (at least 6 months used goods) and you have a residence/work permit in the Netherlands, you will be exempt from customs duty.

Do you deliver to every city in the Netherlands?

Yes, from Amsterdam to Rotterdam, Utrecht to Maastricht, we have door-to-door services to all provinces and cities of the Netherlands.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Yes, as Han Bros Cargo, we offer our customers secure payment options with credit card installments or bank transfers.

Are my items insured?

All our shipments are under CMR insurance. Additionally, if you request, we also provide full comprehensive transport insurance over the declared value of your items.
