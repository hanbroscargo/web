From Eskisehir to the Heart of Europe: Furniture Shipping Solutions

Sending furniture from Eskisehir, which is adjacent to Turkey's important furniture production and trade centers, to Belgium, the capital of the European Union, is now much easier and more reliable with Han Bros Cargo. We deliver the new furniture you bought from Eskisehir or the export products of your store to all cities in Belgium such as Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, and Bruges with professional standards. We meticulously manage every stage of furniture shipping that requires sensitivity, from the loading point in Eskisehir to the final delivery address in Belgium.

Our Areas of Expertise in Eskisehir-Belgium Furniture Shipment

Privileges we offer for the safe journey of your furniture:

  • Packaging at International Standards: Your items are armored against impacts with bubble wraps, corner protectors, and cardboard supports suitable for furniture transport at your address in Eskisehir.
  • Customs and Declaration Support: Our team coordinates all document processes such as commercial invoice and certificate of origin required for new furniture at Belgium customs.
  • Weekly Regular Flights: We minimize the waiting time with our Belgium vehicles departing from Eskisehir and provide delivery via the fastest road route.
  • Elevator Loading and Unloading: We perform furniture pickups from high floors in Eskisehir with our special exterior elevators without damage.

Why Should You Choose Han Bros Cargo?

Furniture shipping is distinguished from ordinary cargo services due to its voluminous and fragile structure. As Han Bros Cargo, we prevent time loss with our command of the long transition processes at Belgium customs gates (Kapıkule, etc.). While we fully secure the material value of your furniture with CMR insurance, we can also offer installation service at your address in Belgium with our professional assembly team. On this logistics bridge stretching from Eskisehir's local industry to Belgium's modern homes, we maintain the balance of affordable price and high quality. From the moment your load leaves Eskisehir, it can be tracked 24/7 via GPS until the final delivery point in Belgium.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the furniture shipping price from Eskisehir to Belgium determined?

Pricing is calculated according to the total volume (m3) of the furniture, the quality of the packaging material, and the distance of the destination city in Belgium.

Can I send only one set of sofas?

Yes, we have partial (groupage) service for shipments of all scales, from a single piece to the furniture of a complete house.

Will there be taxes at Belgium customs?

In new furniture shipments, VAT and customs duty may arise over the commercial invoice. However, if it is in personal effects (used) status, there may be exemptions.

What is the average delivery time in days?

A furniture truck leaving Eskisehir makes delivery to the address in Belgium in an average of 10 to 14 days depending on customs transition density.

Who will assemble my furniture in Belgium?

In line with your request, we provide the service of carrying your furniture into the room and assembling it with our professional installation teams in Belgium.
