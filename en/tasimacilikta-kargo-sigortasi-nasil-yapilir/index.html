How Is Cargo Insurance Done in Shipping?

Cargo insurance is critical for ensuring the safe transportation of goods. Han Bros Cargo manages this process step-by-step, offering solutions tailored to your needs.

  • Value declaration: The value of the cargo is determined.
  • Risk analysis: Potential risks during transportation are evaluated.
  • Policy selection: Choose from all-risk, basic risk, or special risk policies.
