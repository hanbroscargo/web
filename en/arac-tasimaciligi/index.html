Vehicle Shipping Service

As Han Bros Cargo, we professionally handle the international transportation of your cars, motorcycles, commercial vehicles and special vehicles. We have extensive experience in vehicle shipping from Turkey to European countries and from Europe to Turkey.

Transportation Methods

We offer different transportation methods for the safe transport of your vehicles. Options include enclosed truck transportation, open truck transportation and container transportation. Special enclosed transportation service is offered for valuable, classic or luxury vehicles. Open transportation is a more economical option and suitable for standard vehicles.

Car Shipping

We offer special transportation solutions for your passenger cars. Your vehicles are safely transported in specially designed vehicle transport trucks. Before transportation, the vehicle condition is recorded in detail and photographed. These records are used for inspection during delivery. Your vehicle's fuel level, mileage information and existing damages are documented.

Motorcycle Shipping

We offer special packaging and transportation service for your motorcycles. Motorcycles are wrapped and secured with special protective packaging. They are transported in special containers or enclosed trucks to minimize damage risk. In motorcycle shipping, accessories, helmets and other equipment are also safely packaged.

Commercial Vehicle Shipping

We offer special logistics solutions for your commercial vehicles such as trucks, minibuses and panel vans. Since commercial vehicles are usually larger in size, special transportation planning is made. The most suitable transportation method is determined considering weight and size limitations.

Classic and Luxury Vehicle Shipping

We offer premium transportation service for your classic, antique or luxury vehicles. Special enclosed containers, climate control and extra security measures are provided for these vehicles. Insurance coverage appropriate to the value of the vehicles is arranged during transportation.

Preparation and Documents

We provide support in preparing the necessary documents for vehicle shipping. We provide consultancy services on registration, insurance documents, customs documents and special permits. Information is provided on vehicle preparation before transportation, fuel level, battery condition and tire inspection.

Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are of critical importance in international vehicle shipping. Our team, familiar with customs legislation, provides professional support in preparing all customs documents, tax calculations and following customs procedures. Temporary import, permanent import and export procedures are managed.

Insurance and Guarantee

We offer comprehensive insurance options in all our vehicle shipping services. An insurance policy is issued according to the value of your vehicles and full protection is provided during transportation. In case of damage, the compensation process is managed in coordination with the insurance company.

Tracking and Communication

You can track your vehicle's transportation process in real-time through our online tracking system. Transportation route, location information and estimated delivery date are shared through the system. We are always with you with our 24/7 customer support line.

Delivery

When your vehicles arrive at the destination, a detailed inspection is performed before delivery. Vehicle condition, mileage information and damage inspection are carried out. During delivery, vehicle inspection is performed together with the customer and the delivery document is signed.

Reliable, professional and quality service in vehicle shipping with Han Bros Cargo. Contact us for a free price quote.
