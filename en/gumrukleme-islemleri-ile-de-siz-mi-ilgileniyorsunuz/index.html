Your Guide in the Customs Labyrinth: Han Bros Professional Customs Clearance

Undoubtedly, the most complex and meticulous stage of international transportation is customs procedures. Although the concept of 'customs' means uncertainty and additional cost for many people, this process is completely under control with Han Bros Cargo. Our response to the question 'Do you also handle customs clearance?': 'Yes, from the very beginning to the very end!'. We manage the entire bureaucratic process from the Turkey export declaration to the European Union or United Kingdom arrival customs procedures on your behalf with our expert customs brokers. All you have to do is send the necessary documents to us and focus on your new address.

Services We Offer in the Customs Clearance Process

Our customs operations include these items to facilitate your move:

  • Personal Effects Exemption Management: Guidance in filling out forms such as 'Transfer of Residence' (ToR) required for you to fully benefit from your tax exemption rights in household goods shipping.
  • Correct Declaration and Classification: Coding of your items in accordance with customs tariffs (HS Codes) and preventing potential punitive situations.
  • Document Control and Preparation: Making packing lists, invoices, and authorization documents 100% compliant with the legislation.
  • Port and Terminal Operations: Fast transition protocols that optimize the waiting times of our vehicles at customs points.

Why is Customs Clearance Easier with Han Bros?

Han Bros Cargo sees customs processes not as a separate business from transportation, but as an integral part of the service. Thanks to our in-house experts and strategic customs partners, we follow legislation changes instantly. We have a deep knowledge base especially on new rules on the UK line after Brexit or personal effects regulations in EU countries. Incorrect customs clearance is a loss of both time and money; we provide you with both. Your belongings are where they should be, on the road, not at the border.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I give a power of attorney for customs procedures?

Yes, it is a legal requirement for you to give an authorization document/power of attorney addressed to the relevant customs directorates so that we can carry out the customs procedures of your items on your behalf.

Will my items be opened and checked at customs?

Customs authorities have the right to perform physical examination in random or suspicious situations within the scope of risk analysis; this is a standard procedure.

Will I pay customs duty?

If you meet certain conditions (at least 6-12 months of use, etc.) within the scope of personal effects (second-hand household items), you are usually exempted from tax. In commercial loads, customs duty varies according to the type of item.

How long does the customs process take?

If the documents are complete, customs clearance procedures are usually completed within 1-3 business days.

Is the customs brokerage fee included in the shipping price?

The scope of customs clearance services (included or excluded items) is clearly stated in Han Bros Cargo quotes. We usually include our standard brokerage support in our packages.
