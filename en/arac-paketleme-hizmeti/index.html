Vehicle Packing Service

Han Bros Cargo prepares automobiles, motorcycles and heavy-duty fleets for international transport with a tightly controlled packaging workflow. This guide highlights how we mitigate risk while tailoring protection to each vehicle profile.

Planning and Assessment

  • Pre-Inspection: Bodywork, accessories and vehicle class are reviewed to define the ideal protection strategy.
  • Scheduling Blueprint: Packing, loading and delivery are aligned with clearly defined milestones.
  • Regulatory Review: Destination customs and port requirements are confirmed in advance to accelerate documentation.

Protection Technologies

  • Surface Defense: Paint-safe wraps, breathable membranes and electrostatic bags preserve exterior finishes.
  • Chassis Securing: Wheel chocks, frame locks and anti-slip bases minimise vibration during transit.
  • Interior Care: Steering, seating, console and infotainment components receive dedicated covers.

Execution Steps

  1. Preparation: We handle detailing, photography and accessory inventory.
  2. Material Selection: Films, foams, timber crates and impact absorbers are chosen to match the vehicle.
  3. Packing: Layered protection safeguards both exterior and interior surfaces.
  4. Labelling: Global tracking IDs and warning labels deliver full traceability.
  5. Final Check: Integrity reports verify the packaging before handover.

Solutions by Vehicle Type

  • Cars: White-glove handling for premium and classic models.
  • Motorcycles: Foldable crates and locking rails secure two-wheel shipments.
  • Commercial Fleets: Scalable packing programs for light and heavy-duty vehicles.
  • Special Projects: Certified protocols for collection and racing vehicles.

Insurance and Monitoring

  • Comprehensive Policy: Coverage spans the entire packing and logistics journey.
  • Status Updates: Digital dashboards deliver continuous progress reports.
  • Storage Options: Climate-controlled facilities protect vehicles during holding periods.

Budget Design

We document cost drivers such as materials, fleet size, project duration and auxiliary services, keeping your financial roadmap transparent and manageable.
