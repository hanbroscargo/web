New Furniture Shipping Service

As Han Bros Cargo, we provide expert service in new furniture shipping from Turkey to abroad. We safely transport your furniture purchased from Turkey's quality furniture manufacturers to European countries.

Our Service Scope

Our new furniture shipping service covers all processes from picking up your furniture from the manufacturer to delivering it to your new address. We offer special logistics solutions for factory-delivered furniture. Your furniture is delivered undamaged, packaged and ready for assembly.

Factory Delivery and Collection

We collect your furniture directly from the manufacturer or store. During collection, the packaging condition of the furniture is checked and damage inspection is performed. Re-packaging service is offered for furniture with damaged or missing packaging. All furniture is recorded in the inventory list during collection.

Professional Packaging

We provide professional packaging service for the safe transport of your new furniture. Furniture is wrapped with special protective packaging materials. Corner protectors, foam wraps and special cardboard boxes are used. Special wooden crates or cardboard packaging are prepared for large furniture. During the packaging process, furniture brand, model and serial numbers are recorded.

Transportation and Storage

Your furniture is transported in specially designed enclosed trucks or containers. Special arrangements are made during transportation to prevent furniture from damaging each other. Climate control protects against humidity and temperature changes. Temporary storage service is also offered when necessary.

Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are an important stage in new furniture shipping. Our team, familiar with customs legislation, provides professional support in preparing all customs documents, invoice preparation and following customs procedures. We provide consultancy services on tax calculations, VAT refund procedures and special permits.

Assembly and Delivery

When your furniture arrives at your new address, optional assembly service is offered. Our professional assembly team places and assembles your furniture. After assembly, it is checked that the furniture is in working condition. Packaging waste is collected and sent for recycling.

Insurance and Guarantee

We offer comprehensive insurance options in all our new furniture shipping services. An insurance policy is issued according to the value of your furniture and full protection is provided during transportation. In case of damage, the compensation process is managed in coordination with the insurance company.

Furniture Types

We transport all types of furniture such as bedroom sets, seating groups, dining tables, wardrobes, nightstands, bookcases and office furniture. Special transportation planning is made for large furniture. Safe packaging and transportation service is also offered for small furniture and accessories.

Tracking and Communication

You can track your furniture's transportation process in real-time through our online tracking system. Transportation route, location information and estimated delivery date are shared through the system. We are always with you with our 24/7 customer support line.

Pricing

New furniture shipping pricing is determined according to furniture volume, weight, transportation distance and special requirements. Special discounts are offered for bulk furniture shipments. Contact us for a free survey and price quote.

Reliable, professional and quality service in new furniture shipping with Han Bros Cargo. We safely transport your furniture from Turkey to Europe.
