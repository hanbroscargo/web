Factors Affecting Germany Shipping Durations and Delivery Schedule

When establishing a logistics link between Turkey and Germany, one of the most frequently asked questions is "When will my items arrive?" At Han Bros Cargo, we optimize time for you with our standard and express options in road transport. Not only the road distance but also many external factors such as customs procedures, border density, and weather conditions determine the shipment duration.

Estimated Times by Transport Type

Arrival times of different transport modes we offer according to your needs are as follows:

  • Standard Road (Full Truck): If our vehicle is allocated only to you, the average arrival time between Turkey and Germany after loading is between 5 and 7 days.
  • Partial Shipping (Groupage): In this system where the loads of different customers are combined, the delivery time is generally between 10 and 14 days, including collection and customs processes.
  • Express Panel Van Transport: We reach German customs within 48-72 hours with our small and fast vehicles that do not enter the large vehicle congestion at border gates.
  • Air Cargo: It is the fastest option; the airport-to-airport process is 1-2 days, while the door-to-door process can take 3-4 days.

Critical Factors That May Extend the Duration

To avoid disruptions in logistics planning, you should consider these details:

  • Border Gate Density: Truck queues at Kapıkule and Hamzabeyli border gates can create +1 or +2 day delays periodically.
  • Customs Inspections: Deficiencies in the item declaration or the customs inspection process can stop the shipment. Han Bros Cargo experts prepare the documents beforehand to speed up this process.
  • Weekend and Holiday Bans: Truck driving bans (Fahrverbot) on Sundays and public holidays in Germany can affect arrival duration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days do procedures at German customs take?

If your documents are complete and the declaration is opened without issues, customs registration procedures are usually completed on the same day or within 24 hours at most.

Can I track my vehicle during shipment?

Yes, all our vehicles have GPS tracking systems. You can get regular information about the vehicle's route and location every day from our customer representative.

Do snowfall or storms affect duration much?

In the winter months, in case of extreme snowfall experienced on Balkan crossings (Bulgarian-Serbian line), delays of 1-3 days may occur for security reasons.

What is the fastest delivery option?

The fastest door-to-door road solution is our "Express Panel Van" service. With this method, delivery is possible within 3-4 days, including customs.

Do you give a guarantee for delivery on a specific day?

While a definite day guarantee cannot be given in international bonded transport, thanks to our operational planning, we deliver by sticking to the predicted schedule at a 95% rate.
