Shipping times to Germany vary depending on the selected service type and many factors. Standard service is the most economical option and delivery is usually made within 7-10 business days. This time covers collection, packing, customs procedures, and transport stages. Express service is faster and provides delivery within 3-5 business days, but costs 40-50% more than standard service. Priority service delivers within 2-4 business days with special route planning for special situations. Customs processing times normally take 1-2 days, but can extend to 3-5 days during peak periods or special situations. Route distance is important; shipments starting from Turkey's west are faster. Season volume (summer months, holiday periods) can extend times by 2-3 days. Weather conditions are also effective; times can extend in winter months due to snow and ice.
