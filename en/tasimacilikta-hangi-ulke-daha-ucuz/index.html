Smart Choices on the Logistics Map: Country-Based Cost Comparison

The answer to the question 'Which country is cheaper?' in international shipping is not just a calculation based on kilometers. Factors determining the cost of a shipment include that country's customs regimes, highway taxes, local warehouse costs, and general labor expenses. As Han Bros Cargo, with our deep dominance in the Europe and UK lines, we explain to our customers which route is more economical with data. For example, a country that seems close in terms of distance may cost more than a further point due to complex customs procedures. Strategic logistics planning is the first rule of protecting your budget.

Cost Comparison Criteria on the European Line

Basic dynamics creating price differences between countries are as follows:

  • Customs Union vs. Third Countries: While shipments made to EU member countries such as Germany, France, and Netherlands have Customs Union advantages; UK (after Brexit) may require special procedures.
  • Central Location Advantage: Since Germany and Belgium are located in the logistics heart of Europe, transfer and distribution costs are usually lower.
  • Port and Terminal Fees: Netherlands (Rotterdam) and Belgium (Antwerp), which have strong maritime connections, offer cheaper options for cargo with transoceanic connections.
  • Personal Effects Exemption Differences: Each country's 'Transfer of Residence' exemption rules and limits may vary.

Determine the Most Economical Route with Han Bros Cargo

As Han Bros Cargo, no matter where the delivery point is, we offer 'combined transport' and 'partial loading' advantages that will lower the cost. For example, we provide serious savings by sending a load going to Luxembourg via Belgium transfer. Instead of asking 'Which country is cheaper?', we respond to the question 'How to go most cheaply to that country?' with Han Bros expertise. Frequently Asked Questions

Why are Bulgaria and Romania cargo rates cheaper?

Since these countries are the points closest to the Turkish border, distance costs (fuel, driver per diem) are at minimum.

Is moving to the UK more expensive than European countries?

Although some difference has arisen due to additional customs declarations after Brexit, we minimize this difference thanks to Han Bros Cargo's direct line.

In which country is the customs process faster and cheaper?

Usually Netherlands and Germany, where the customs digitalization rate is high, are advantageous in terms of processing speed and reduce waiting costs.

Why do prices change even though it is the same distance?

Highway tolls, truck driving bans, and local distribution labor costs in that country can differentiate prices at the same kilometers.

Can you suggest the cheapest route to me?

Yes, according to the urgency of your shipment, we create the most suitable option for your budget by offering road, partial, or transfer options.
