Furniture Shipping from Adana to the UK

The international transport sector requires trust and experience. As Turkey's leading brand in furniture shipping from Adana to the UK, we complete your journey starting from Adana to the UK with confidence.

Our Logistics Process from Adana to the UK

We use Mersin Port or road integration for shipments from Adana. The distance between the two points is approximately 3,900 km, and our estimated delivery time is 14-17 days. We serve with a large vehicle fleet from the Çukurova region. We offer special advantages to our customers with economic solutions for large-volume household goods shipments.

Furniture Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our Furniture Export and Installation focused service. For the export of furniture you purchased from Turkey to the UK, an E-Archive invoice and ATR circulation certificate are required. We collect directly from the factory or store and load onto the truck after palletizing. Additionally, you can benefit from our direct shipment advantage from İnegöl, Kayseri, or Istanbul furniture markets.

Professional Packaging and Customs Clearance

The safety of your belongings is our priority. We use a 'kraft paper + bubble wrap' combination for your fragile items. On the customs side, our expert team manages the procedures that became more difficult after Brexit on your behalf.

Transportation Plan

  • Survey: Volume detection at your address in Adana.
  • Operation: We collect directly from the factory or store and load onto the truck after the palletizing process.
  • Customs: Export procedures from Adana and import procedures to the UK.
  • Delivery: Scheduled delivery and installation support.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the payment made?

A 30% down payment is required for reservation, and the remainder is collected upon delivery.

What does the insurance cover?

Our All-Risk insurance covers risks such as accidents, fire, and theft during the process from packaging to delivery of the goods.

Are there customs duties?

If you receive ToR approval in the status of personal effects, you do not pay tax. For the export of furniture you purchased from Turkey to the UK, an E-Archive invoice and ATR circulation certificate are required.
