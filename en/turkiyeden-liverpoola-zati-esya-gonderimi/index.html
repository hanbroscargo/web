Personal Effects Shipping from Turkey to Liverpool

Our team is a leading name in personal effects shipping from Turkey to Liverpool. Our logistics operations starting from Turkey and ending in Liverpool are carried out safely via the European highway.

Key Considerations in Personal Effects Shipping

Personal effects shipping operation is more than just items moving from one place to another. Especially post-Brexit customs procedures and logistics dynamics on the European highway line affect the process. Our logistics department plans all these stages on your behalf.

Service Detail: We provide consultancy on customs duty exemption (ToR1) for your personal (personal) items. This is the most suitable solution for your non-commercial belongings. We also evaluate Truck (TIR) or Minivan options according to the volume of your belongings.

Logistics Route and Timing

Our transport vehicles move via the European highway and reach the destination through Liverpool Port. The distance between Turkey and Liverpool is approximately 3,000-4,000 km, and the delivery time may vary depending on customs procedures.

During the journey, you can monitor your load instantly with our vehicle tracking systems and get information from our operations team 24/7.

Why Choose Han Bros Cargo?

We operate with our motto of the most suitable price and trouble-free service. We offer "All-Risk" insurance assurance against potential damages. In addition, with our experienced personnel in personal effects shipping processes, you can move without experiencing language barriers or procedural confusion.

Workflow Steps

  • Planning: Free survey and volume determination at your address in Turkey.
  • Preparation: International standard packaging of all items.
  • Customs Clearance: Preparation of Turkey export and Liverpool entry customs declarations.
  • Transfer: A safe journey of 3,000-4,000 km.
  • Delivery: Installation at your new address and collection of waste materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the shipping take?

On average, our vehicles departing from Turkey provide delivery in 10-14 days. Our express service may take 4-6 days.

Are there customs duties?

If you meet the ToR (Transfer of Residence) conditions within the scope of personal effects, tax-free transport is possible. Our customs consultants will guide you for Personal Effects Shipping.

What does insurance cover?

Our international transport insurance fully covers major risks such as accidents, overturning, fire, and theft.

Conclusion

Contact us to get professional support for your Turkey to Liverpool personal effects shipping requests. Our team aims to provide you with the best experience, coordinated by experts from our headquarters.
