Bringing Your Vehicle to Holland: Step-by-Step Shipping Process

When moving from Turkey to Holland, one of the most curious topics is whether you can bring your vehicle with you. As Han Bros Cargo, we transform this process from being a 'burden' into a professional shipping operation for you. Holland is a country with certain rules and tax (BPM) exemptions regarding vehicle import. A vehicle shipment that is not planned correctly can result in high taxes and bureaucratic obstacles. With our closed-box tow trucks and customs experts, we pick up your vehicle from your address in Turkey and deliver it seamlessly to your new doorstep in Holland.

Required Documents and Exemptions in Holland Vehicle Shipping

To pass your vehicle through customs 'with Exemption' (Tax-Free), it is generally critical that you provide these conditions:

  • Ownership Period: The vehicle must have been registered to you for at least 6 months and must have been used in Turkey.
  • Residence Transfer: You must be moving your residence from Turkey to Holland and document this (such as RDW records).
  • Document List: Vehicle registration, notarized power of attorney, copy of passport, and vehicle insurance breakdown are the main documents.
  • Power of Attorney Management: The Han Bros team manages all the legal authorizations required to conduct customs procedures on your behalf.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I pay customs duty for the vehicle in Holland?

If you bring your vehicle within the scope of personal effects and provide the necessary conditions (6 months ownership, etc.), you are usually exempt from customs duty and VAT.

In how many days will my vehicle reach Holland?

Depending on our shipment date and customs density, our average delivery time is 10-14 days.

Do you carry vehicles with LPG?

Yes, all LPG vehicles that comply with safety standards and are registered in the license can be carried.

Can I also send my personal belongings inside my vehicle?

Yes, you can put your personal items in the vehicle's trunk up to a certain weight limit and in a way that does not obstruct the view.

Do you handle the Holland license plate (Registration) procedures?

We manage the process up to the customs clearance (import) stage. You need to take the RDW appointments for plate change and local registration procedures yourself.
