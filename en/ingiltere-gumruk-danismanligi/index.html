UK Customs Consultancy

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer customs consultancy services to the UK. We provide professional support regarding post-Brexit customs procedures, document preparation, tax calculations and exemption applications.

Our Customs Consultancy Services

  • Customs Procedures: Post-Brexit customs procedures and process management
  • Document Preparation: Preparation of customs declarations, household goods list and required documents
  • Tax Calculations: Customs tax, VAT and other tax calculations
  • Exemption Applications: Customs exemption applications and follow-up
  • Smooth Process Management: Smooth management of all customs procedures

Customs Procedures Process to UK

Our customs procedures process consists of the following steps:

  • Consultancy: Detailed information about customs procedures
  • Document Preparation: Preparation and control of required documents
  • Tax Calculation: Calculation of customs tax and other taxes
  • Application: Customs declarations and exemption applications
  • Follow-up: Tracking and process management of customs procedures
  • Approval: Approval and completion of customs procedures

Post-Brexit Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are mandatory for shipments to the UK after Brexit. Customs declarations and tax calculations must be made for household goods, commercial goods and other shipments.
