Dowry Transport Services from Ankara to the UK

We offer professional solutions for your dowry transport services from Ankara to the UK needs. In this process, which requires a journey of approximately 3,500 km on the Turkey and United Kingdom line, we are by your side, collecting from our Ankara logistics base.

Things to Consider in the Dowry Transport Process

Dowry transport operation is not just about moving goods from one place to another. Especially UK customs legislation and logistics dynamics via Istanbul or direct affect the process. As Han Bros Cargo, we plan all these stages on your behalf.

Service Detail: We offer sensitive dowry transport for newly married couples. We take extra care of your fragile items. We also evaluate Truck or Minivan options according to the volume of your belongings.

Why Han Bros Cargo?

We act with the motto of attractive offers and guaranteed service. We offer 'All-Risk' insurance guarantee against possible damages. In addition, you can move without experiencing a language barrier or procedural confusion with our experienced staff in dowry transport processes.

Logistics Route and Timing

Our transport vehicles reach the destination via Dover or Felixstowe, moving either via Istanbul or directly. The distance from Ankara to the UK is approximately 3,500 km, and delivery time may vary depending on customs procedures.

During the journey, you can monitor your load instantly with our vehicle tracking systems and get information 24/7 from our operations team.

PROCEDURE Steps

  • Planning: Free survey and volume determination at your address in Ankara.
  • Preparation: Packaging of all goods according to international standards.
  • Customs Clearance: Preparation of Turkey exit and UK entry customs declarations.
  • Transfer: Secure journey with a distance of 3,500 km.
  • Delivery: Installation at the new address and collection of packaging waste.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does transport take?

On average, our vehicles departing from Ankara provide delivery within 10-14 days. Our Express service can take 4-6 days.

Are there customs duties?

If you meet the ToR (Transfer of Residence) conditions for personal effects, tax-free transport is possible. Our customs consultants will guide you for dowry transport.

What does the insurance cover?

Our international transport insurance covers main risks such as accidents, overturning, fire, and theft comprehensively.

Conclusion

Contact us to get professional support for your dowry transport services from Ankara to the UK. As Han Bros Cargo, we aim to offer you the best experience by operating from our Ankara logistics base.
