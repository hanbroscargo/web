Our Privacy Policy

When you visit our website, some information is collected through cookies to improve your use of our site and the services offered on our site. This information, except for your identity information, is used to better present the services on our site and improve the user experience. Cookies are used to remember your user preferences, optimize the functioning of our site and provide you with a better experience.

The collected information is not shared with third parties and is used only to increase the performance of our site and improve our services. Users can prevent the use of cookies by changing their browser settings. However, you should remember that in this case, some parts of our site may not work properly.

Your privacy is extremely important to us. We take the necessary technical and administrative measures to ensure the security of your personal information. If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, you can contact us.
