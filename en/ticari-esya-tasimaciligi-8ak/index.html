Commercial Goods Shipping Service

As Han Bros Cargo, we provide comprehensive logistics solutions for commercial goods and cargo transportation. We provide professional transportation services to meet the international trade needs of businesses.

Our Service Scope

Our commercial goods shipping service covers all processes from collection to delivery of commercial cargo. Options include partial cargo, full cargo, palletized cargo and container transportation. We offer logistics solutions tailored to business needs.

Partial Cargo Transportation

We provide partial cargo transportation service for small and medium-sized commercial cargo. In partial cargo transportation, cargo from different customers is combined in the same vehicle to provide cost-effective transportation. This method is economical especially for small-volume commercial shipments.

Full Cargo Transportation

We provide full cargo transportation service for large-volume commercial cargo. In full cargo transportation, the entire vehicle or container is allocated to a single customer. This method is a faster and safer option for large-volume shipments.

Palletized Cargo Transportation

We offer special transportation solutions for palletized commercial cargo. Pallets are loaded and unloaded with special forklifts. In palletized cargo transportation, safe securing of cargo and damage prevention measures are taken. Service is provided for standard pallet sizes and special pallet sizes.

Container Transportation

We provide container transportation service for large-volume commercial cargo. 20-foot and 40-foot container options are available. In container transportation, arrangement and securing of cargo inside the container is important. Service is also provided for special container types (refrigerated, open-top, side-opening).

Packaging and Wrapping

We provide professional packaging service for the safe transport of your commercial goods. Special packaging methods are applied according to product type. Special protective packaging for fragile products, anti-static packaging for electronic products are used. Sturdy cardboard or wooden crate packaging is prepared for industrial products.

Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are of critical importance in commercial goods shipping. Our team, familiar with customs legislation, provides professional support in preparing all customs documents, invoice preparation, certificate of origin preparation and following customs procedures. Import, export and transit procedures are managed.

Insurance and Guarantee

We offer comprehensive insurance options in all our commercial goods shipping services. An insurance policy is issued according to the value of your cargo and full protection is provided during transportation. In case of damage, the compensation process is managed in coordination with the insurance company.

Tracking and Reporting

You can track your commercial cargo transportation process in real-time through our online tracking system. Transportation route, location information and estimated delivery date are shared through the system. Special reporting services are offered for businesses. Monthly, weekly and daily transportation reports are prepared.

Delivery Options

We offer different options for delivery of your commercial goods. Options include door-to-door delivery, warehouse delivery, port delivery and terminal delivery. Delivery time and location are planned according to customer needs. Express delivery service is also offered.

Special Solutions

We offer logistics solutions tailored to the needs of different sectors. Special transportation planning is made for different sectors such as textiles, food, electronics, automotive spare parts, machinery and equipment. Special services are offered for cold chain transportation, dangerous goods transportation and valuable goods transportation.

Reliable, professional and quality service in commercial goods shipping with Han Bros Cargo. Contact us for your business logistics needs.
