When comparing shipping methods, several important factors should be considered. Road transport is the most common choice because it is fast, secure, and flexible; delivery is made within an average of 6-9 days. Sea freight is ideal for large-volume items and is more economical; however, delivery time is longer (10-15 days). Air cargo is the fastest option but is expensive; preferred for urgent shipments (1-3 days). Time factor is the most important factor in method selection; air cargo for urgent shipments, road or sea transport for normal shipments. Cost factor affects method selection; sea freight for economical option, road transport for balanced option, air cargo for fast option. Security factor is maintained at high levels in all methods; your items are transported safely in all cases. Capacity factor affects item volume; sea freight for large-volume items, road transport for medium-volume items, air cargo for small-volume items.
