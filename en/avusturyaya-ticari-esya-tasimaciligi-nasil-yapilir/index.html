Austria Commercial Freight and Export Guide

Expert Solutions in Austrian Commercial Logistics

Austria is an indispensable market for Turkish exporters with its strategic location in the heart of Europe and its strong economy. As Han Bros Cargo, we safely transport your commercial loads (textile, furniture, automotive spare parts, industrial machinery, etc.) from Turkey to all Austrian states (Wien, Oberösterreich, Steiermark, etc.). With the awareness that commercial shipping means 'on-time delivery' and 'correct customs clearance,' we provide end-to-end logistics support to our B2B business partners.

Commercial Shipment Processes and Documentation

The most critical pillar of international commercial transport is documentation. An incorrect invoice or a missing document can cause days of waiting at the border. Han Bros Cargo experts manage these processes on your behalf:

  • E-Export and Micro-Export: We provide fast customs clearance for your low-volume commercial shipments within the scope of ETGB.
  • Customs Consultancy: Tracking documents such as ATR, EUR.1, and Certificate of Origin and opening the customs declaration for arrival in Austria.
  • Incoterms Compliance: Operation and cost analysis according to delivery terms such as EXW, DAP, DDP.
  • CMR Document: We ensure correct management of the international road transport document at every stage of the shipment.

Palletized and Partial Commercial Transport

Not every shipment needs to fill a full truck. Han Bros Cargo reaches your client in Vienna or Linz even with a single pallet under economic conditions with its partial commercial trucks departing regularly every week. Our palletized transport standards (Euro Pallet) minimize damage risk while increasing loading and unloading speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery time in commercial transport?

Your commercial loads departing from Turkey by road are delivered in an average of 8-10 business days, including customs clearance. For more urgent loads, our express service, which takes 4-5 days, is available.

Which product groups do you transport to Austria?

We transport almost all kinds of commercial products except hazardous materials (ADR). We specifically specialize in textiles, construction materials, and spare parts logistics.

Where are the customs procedures performed?

Exit procedures are done at main customs like Istanbul, Ankara, or Izmir; while arrival customs clearance is completed in our contracted customs warehouses in Austria (such as Zollamt Wien).

Does your insurance scope protect commercial loads?

Yes, all our commercial shipments are under CMR Insurance. We also offer full comprehensive transport insurance over the invoice value for valuable loads.

Is there special pricing for B2B customers?

We provide special freight prices and payment conveniences to our corporate customers who make regular shipments, based on an annual volume commitment.
