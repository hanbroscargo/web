We Expertly Transport Your Furniture from Turkey to Belgium

The transportation of your furniture, which is the soul of your home, from Turkey to Belgium (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, etc.) is not just a loading-unloading process; it's a complex process that includes carpentry, precise packaging, and customs expertise. As Han Bros Cargo, we deliver all your furniture groups, from newly bought furniture to antique-value family heirlooms, with professional meticulousness even to the narrowest streets of Belgium.

'Han Bros' Standards in Furniture Logistics

We apply the following standards so that your furniture maintains its form during the approximately 3,000-kilometer journey on the Belgium route:

  • Professional Disassembly: Your cabinets, bed units, and large tables are disassembled by our expert carpenters. Each piece is marked with a special coding system for ease of assembly.
  • Tiered Packaging System: Your furniture is armored first with soft materials preventing scratches, then with shock-absorbing bubble wraps, and finally with hard corner protectors.
  • Elevator Shipping Supports: As a solution to the narrow stair structures in the historic buildings of Brussels and Ghent, we safely evacuate your furniture from windows using our external facade elevators.
  • Customs and Exemption Support: We prepare your inventory lists and required declarations so that your belongings enter the Belgium customs exempt from tax within the scope of 'personal effects.'

City-Based Delivery Planning

Thanks to our proximity to the Antwerp port and our local logistics network in Brussels, we quickly distribute your furniture within the city. From the busy customs area of Antwerp to the calm canals of Ghent, we dominate local shipping rules at every point. With our weekly partial flights, even if you don't move your entire home, you can send just a sofa set to Belgium at very economical freight prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is tax incurred in Belgium for newly bought furniture?

Belgium customs may demand VAT and customs duty for furniture bought newer than 6 months. However, your second-hand personal furniture is within the scope of exemption.

Do you perform the assembly of the furniture at our address in Belgium?

Absolutely! Disassembly and assembly service are included in our standard package. Our carpenters deliver your furniture by setting them up in the rooms you want.

Do you transport custom-made furniture like kitchen cabinets or children's rooms?

Yes, we re-install all kinds of modular or fixed furniture by disassembling them on-site in a way suitable for your new home in Belgium.

Can I send just one console or piano to Belgium?

Of course. Thanks to our Partial (Groupage) service, you can send a single piece of valuable furniture without having to fill the entire truck.

What happens if my furniture is scratched during transport?

All your furniture is under CMR insurance. Since we perform professional packaging, the risk is minimal; however, in case of a mishap, we provide compensation for your damage through the insurance process.
