Classic Car Transport to UK

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer special service for classic car transport to UK. We prepare special transport solutions for antique and classic cars. We transport your classic vehicles safely with careful packing, high value insurance and expert team.

Our Service Scope

  • Classic Car Transport: Special transport for antique and classic cars
  • Careful Packing: Special packing and protection for classic cars
  • Enclosed Transport: Your vehicles are transported in enclosed special vehicles
  • High Value Insurance: Special insurance packages for classic cars
  • Expert Team: Experienced team in classic car transport
  • Special Tracking: Special tracking and monitoring for classic cars

Classic Car Transport

Classic and antique cars require special attention and care. Special packing, protection and transport methods are used for transport of these vehicles. Special transport plan is prepared for each classic car.

Careful Packing

  • Special Covers: Classic cars are protected with special covers
  • Sensitive Area Protection: Headlights, windows and chrome parts are specially protected
  • Securing: Vehicle is secured safely
  • Humidity Control: Humidity and weather conditions are controlled

Enclosed Transport

Classic cars are always transported in enclosed vehicles. They are completely protected from weather conditions, dust and external factors. Safe transport is provided with special enclosed vehicles.

High Value Insurance

We offer special insurance packages for classic cars. Insurance coverage suitable for vehicle value is determined and comprehensive protection is provided.

Transport Process

  1. Evaluation: Classic car evaluation and condition assessment is done
  2. Packing Plan: Special packing plan is prepared
  3. Careful Packing: Vehicle is carefully packed and protected
  4. Loading: Safe loading is done to enclosed vehicles
  5. Transport: Transport is done with special tracking
  6. Control: Control and tracking is done at each stage
  7. Delivery: Careful delivery is made in UK

Expert Team

Our experienced team in classic car transport shows special attention for each classic car. We transport your classic vehicles safely with years of experience.
