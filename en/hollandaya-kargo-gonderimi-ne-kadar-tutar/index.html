Cargo Shipping Fees and Price List to Netherlands 2026

Cost Transparency in Holland Cargo Shipments: Net Information, Net Price

The most wondered subject while sending cargo abroad is undoubtedly the question 'how much does parcel shipping to Holland cost?'. Holland (Netherlands) is one of the European countries with the most package traffic from Turkey. As Han Bros Cargo, we offer you the lowest unit cost by categorizing this traffic for both micro shipments (1-5 kg documents and gifts) and macro packages (furniture or samples over 30 kg). There is no room for the 'surprise extra fee' logic in our pricing; we clarify all components from customs processes to the delivery point at the very beginning.

Factors Affecting Cargo Price and Calculation Method

The cost of your shipment is determined according to these 3 basic criteria:

  • Volumetric Weight (Desi): The actual weight of the cargo is compared with the area it occupies (Width x Length x Height / 5000). Whichever value is higher, the price is calculated over that unit.
  • Service Speed: Whether you prefer Amsterdam delivery in 24-48 hours with 'Express Service' or 7-10 days 'Economic Road' directly affects the price by 40%.
  • Shipment Content: There may be differences in terms of customs clearance expenses between commercial samples and gifts for personal use.

Tips to Catch the Most Suitable Price

It is very easy to lower your Holland cargo expenses with Han Bros Cargo. In your bulk shipments (more than one package), you can lower the unit price by up to 20% by taking advantage of 'Single Consignment' discounts. Also, you can get rid of desi (volume) excess by preparing your packages in the most suitable sizes for the content instead of unnecessarily large boxes. We do not reflect extra 'Remote Area' fees for deliveries made to main centers like Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague with the strength of our local network. Our goal is to reach our business partners and individual customers in Turkey to the Holland market and their loved ones in the most economical way.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 5 kg package go to Holland for?

Although prices change according to current oil prices and service selection, you can learn our most competitive 'Mini-Package' tariffs for small packages from our website or by calling us.

Is door-to-door delivery included in the price?

Yes, all cargo offers we give cover pickup from the address and delivery to the address (Door-to-Door) services.

Does the customs duty belong to the buyer?

In personal shipments without an IOSS number, Dutch customs may request tax for values over 22/150 Euro. These taxes are usually paid to the local authorities by the buyer.

Is there a discount for student cargo?

We have special 'Student-Care' discounted tariffs for our students studying in Holland for their book and personal item shipments.

What is the fastest delivery time?

With our air express service, a cargo departing from Istanbul is usually out for distribution via Amsterdam Schiphol Airport the next business day.
