Parcel Shipping from Bursa to Liverpool

Are you looking for a reliable solution away from the complexity of logistics processes? With the corporate solutions we offer for parcel shipping from Bursa to Liverpool, your belongings are collected from Bursa and delivered to your door in Liverpool.

Professional Packaging and Customs Clearance

The safety of your belongings is our priority. We use a kraft paper and bubble wrap combination for fragile items. Our expert team manages post-Brexit customs procedures on your behalf.

Parcel Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our express parcel and package shipping service. For shipments under 300 kg, Minivan Express or air cargo options are more suitable. Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and entered into the tracking system. You can also benefit from our 48-72 hour delivery option for urgent shipments.

Our Logistics Process from Bursa to Liverpool

We use integrated Ro-Ro lines and road transport for shipments from Bursa. The distance is approximately 3200 km, and our estimated delivery time is 10-14 days. We offer special advantages with our experienced team and regular weekly departures.

Process Steps

  • Survey: Volume assessment at your address in Bursa.
  • Operation: Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and processed in the tracking system.
  • Customs: Export from Bursa and import procedures to Liverpool.
  • Delivery: Scheduled delivery and installation support.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is payment due?

Reservation is made with a 30% advance payment; the balance is collected on delivery.

What does the insurance cover?

Our All-Risk insurance covers accident, fire and theft risks from packaging to delivery.

Are there customs duties?

If you obtain ToR approval for personal effects status, you may be exempt from tax. For shipments under 300 kg, Minivan Express or air cargo options are more suitable.

Do you offer storage services?

Yes, we have secure warehouses in both Turkey and the UK. You can store your belongings for as long as you need.
