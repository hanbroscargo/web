UK Express Cargo Service

Han Bros Cargo offers express cargo services to the UK. We provide fast and reliable service with 24-48 hour delivery for urgent shipments, priority processing, 24/7 tracking system and safe shipping.

Our Express Cargo Services

  • 24-48 Hour Delivery: Fast delivery guarantee for urgent shipments
  • Priority Processing: Express shipments are processed with priority
  • 24/7 Tracking System: You can track the status of your shipment in real-time
  • Safe Shipping: All shipments are insured and safely transported
  • Door to Door Delivery: Collected from your address and delivered to delivery address

Express Cargo Process

Our express cargo process consists of the following steps:

  • Shipment Acceptance: Your shipment is quickly accepted and registered to system
  • Priority Processing: Express shipments are processed with priority
  • Fast Transport: Fast transport process with special express route
  • Tracking: Shipment status is tracked with 24/7 tracking system
  • Delivery: Door to door delivery within planned time

24/7 Tracking System

You can track the status of your shipment in real-time. You can learn where your shipment is and when it will be delivered with online tracking system.

Safe Shipping

All express shipments are covered by insurance. Fast solution and compensation process is guaranteed in case of damage. We offer high value insurance options for valuable shipments.
