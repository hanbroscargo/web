A New Start in Holland: Step-by-Step Moving and Life Guide

Moving from Turkey to Holland (Netherlands) is not only taking your belongings from one country to another, but also stepping into a new culture and legal regulation. As Han Bros Cargo, we are the logistics architect of this big change. This guide, which we have gathered under the title 'Everything about household goods shipping to Holland', was prepared with the experiences we gained from thousands of successful shipments. We reduce your moving stress to zero with our operational power suitable for all kinds of settlements, from the narrow and steeply staircase canal houses of Amsterdam to the ultra-modern tall buildings of Rotterdam. Moving to Holland is not an adventure, but a project that needs to be managed well.

'Verhuizing' Rules to be Considered in Dutch Customs

Dutch customs have one of the most organized systems in the world in the import of personal effects (used household items). For a tax-free transition, these steps are critical:

  • BSN Number and Registration: Having registered with the municipality (Gemeente) in Holland accelerates the process.
  • Used Item Condition: The items must have been in your ownership for at least 6 months and must not have a commercial purpose.
  • Inventory List: A detailed and honest list (we offer English/Dutch support) indicating the content of each box of your items must be prepared.
  • Exemption Application: Our expert customs brokers obtain the necessary approvals from the Dutch customs office on your behalf.

Logistics Operation: From Packaging to Installation

Holland houses, especially buildings in historical centers, are famous for their narrow entrances and steep stairs. That's why as Han Bros Cargo, we include the 'External Elevator' (Verhuislift) service into our standards during delivery together with our local partners in Holland. Taking your items safely through the window both reduces the risk of damage and accelerates the process. At the packaging stage, we use high-strength materials resistant to vibrations on European roads. We do the disassembly of your furniture in Turkey and install it wherever you want in your room in Holland, leaving you only the joy of your new home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to move to Holland?

The door-to-door moving process takes an average of 10-14 calendar days, including customs approval.

Do I need to be in Holland for the customs exemption?

For your file to be opened, you must have started your registration (BRP) process in Holland or be able to submit your employment contract.

Can you keep my belongings in your warehouse for a certain period of time?

Yes, if you haven't rented your house in Holland yet, we can keep your belongings in our secure warehouses in our Amsterdam and Rotterdam locations.

Is there a prohibited or restricted product?

Products such as alcohol, tobacco products, fresh foods, and weapons cannot be carried within the scope of personal effects or are subject to high tax.

Do you carry my pets?

Currently, we are focused only on item and vehicle logistics, but we can direct you to our reliable pet transport agencies.
