Our Bank Account Information

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer secure and fast payment transactions for our international transportation services. You can make your payments using the bank account information listed below. It is important to use IBAN and SWIFT codes correctly for all payments.

Payment Methods and Important Considerations

You can make payments for our international transportation services via bank transfer, EFT, or international transfer methods. Important points to consider when making payments:

  • Account Holder: You must use "HAN BROS INTERNATIONAL REMOVALS TRANSPORT KARGO LTD" as the account holder name for all payments.
  • IBAN Accuracy: Pay attention to spacing and character errors when copying IBAN numbers.
  • SWIFT Code: Always specify the SWIFT code for international transfers.
  • Currency: Use the appropriate account for the currency you will pay in (TRY, USD, EUR, GBP).
  • Reference Information: Including your quote number or shipment tracking number in the payment description helps match your transactions faster.

Post-Payment Procedures

After making your payment, it is important to share your payment receipt or transfer document with our customer service. This way:

  • Your payment is processed quickly in our system
  • Your shipment preparation process begins
  • Your tracking number can be sent to you
  • Quick resolution is provided in case of any issues

Secure Payment Transactions

As Han Bros Cargo, we care about the security of all your payment transactions. Our bank information is only shared through our official website and customer representatives. When making payments:

  • Use the information you received from our official channels
  • Be cautious of suspicious emails or messages
  • Verify with our customer service before payment
  • Follow our official announcements in case of changes to our bank information

International Transfers

Important information about international transfer procedures for our customers making payments from abroad:

  • SWIFT Code Usage: You must always specify the SWIFT code for international transfers. For İş Bankası: ISBKTRIS, For Garanti Bankası: TGBATRISXXX
  • Transfer Duration: International transfers are usually completed within 2-5 business days.
  • Transfer Fees: Interbank transfer fees may vary. Check transfer fees with your bank before making payment.
  • Exchange Rates: Exchange rates at the time of payment will apply. Contact your bank for current rates.

Customer Service Support

If you have any questions or need help regarding payment transactions, our customer service is available 24/7. You can share your payment receipts, transfer documents, or any payment-related questions with us.

As Han Bros Cargo, we help you manage your international transportation services in the best way with secure and fast payment transactions. You can use the IBAN and SWIFT codes listed in the tables above for your payment information.
