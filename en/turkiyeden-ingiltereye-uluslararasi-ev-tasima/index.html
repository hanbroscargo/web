Professional Home Moving Service from Turkey to the UK

International home moving from Turkey to the UK is a process that requires expertise due to post-Brexit procedures. Han Bros Cargo is with you with 2026 updated rules from customs to delivery.

International Shipping Procedures

Customs and ToR1 Processes

The most critical step in moving household goods to the UK is ToR1 (Transfer of Residence) approval:

  • ToR1 application and customs relief consultancy
  • Preparation of goods list and official approval processes
  • Professional completion of customs declarations
  • Coordination with HMRC (UK Customs)

Required Documents

  • Passport copy and visa status
  • ToR1 application number
  • Detailed packing list (Inventory)
  • Address info or accommodation declaration in the UK
