International home moving from Turkey to UK

International Home Moving from Turkey to UK

International home moving from Turkey to the UK is a process that requires complex procedures and careful planning. Han Bros Cargo provides expert support at every stage of this process.

International Transport Procedures

Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are of critical importance in household goods transport to the UK after Brexit:

  • Preparing household goods list and notary approval
  • Filling customs declarations
  • Tax calculation and payment
  • Customs inspection and approval process
  • Release procedures

Document Requirements

  • Passport and visa documents
  • Residence permit or work permit
  • Household goods inventory list (detailed and notary approved)
  • Purchase invoices (for valuable items)
  • Insurance documents
  • Customs declarations

Prohibited and Restricted Items

Points to consider when moving to the UK:

  • Flammable and explosive substances (gasoline, gas cylinder, fireworks)
  • Toxic and dangerous chemicals
  • Weapons and ammunition
  • Plant and animal products (special permit required)
  • Cash (certain limits apply)

Post-Brexit Changes

Important changes in household goods transport to the UK after Brexit:

  • Customs procedures became mandatory
  • Tax calculations changed
  • Document requirements increased
  • Inspection processes extended
  • Additional costs occurred

Transport Process

  1. Pre-preparation: Document preparation and goods inventory
  2. Packing: Customs compliant packing
  3. Loading: Loading with customs documents
  4. Transport: Safe international transport
  5. Customs: Customs procedures and inspection
  6. Delivery: Delivery in the UK

Cost Structure

International home moving costs include:

  • Transport fee (distance and volume based)
  • Customs fees
  • Taxes and duties
  • Insurance fees
  • Packing and packaging
  • Storage (if needed)
