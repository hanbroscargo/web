Vehicle Transport to Germany: Price and Service Details

When considering sending a vehicle from Turkey to Germany, the most curious topic is costs. At Han Bros Cargo, we manage the vehicle transport process both economically and securely. Vehicle transport prices to Germany vary based on the vehicle's model and size, the starting point and arrival city, and the preferred transport method. With our transparent pricing policy, we offer our customers clear solutions far from hidden costs.

Factors Affecting Pricing

In the logistics world, vehicle transport is not evaluated like a standard cargo. The main factors determining costs are:

  • Vehicle Type and Size: The transport cost of a standard passenger car differs from an SUV or a commercial panel van. The space occupied by the vehicle and its weight directly affect the freight price.
  • Delivery Point: Distances between southern cities like Munich or Stuttgart and northern cities like Hamburg or Berlin can create price differences.
  • Transport Method: Using an open carrier (multi-carrier) is the most economical option, while using a closed carrier or a special trailer requires a more premium service fee.
  • Customs and Insurance: Shipping insurance based on the vehicle's value and customs consultancy services are added to the total cost.

Professional Process Management

Han Bros Cargo collects your vehicle from your door and manages the entire process up to the customs point in Germany or your specified address. One of the most critical points in vehicle transport is securing the vehicle safely to the carrier. Our expert loading team makes your vehicle's tire and chassis connections according to international standards, providing full protection against vibrations that may occur along the way.

Customs Clearance and Personal Belongings Status

If you are returning to Germany permanently or have residence rights, you can take your vehicle under "personal belongings" status to take advantage of tax benefits. However, this process requires detailed legislation knowledge. Our consultants calculate the taxes (European taxes similar to VAT and excise duty) to be paid based on your vehicle's age and model and inform you in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the cost of vehicle transport to Germany calculated?

The cost is calculated based on the vehicle's make and model, size, departure city in Turkey, and arrival city in Germany. Current exchange rates and fuel costs also play a role in pricing.

What is the vehicle transport duration?

The arrival of a vehicle from Turkey to Germany by road takes an average of 7 to 12 business days, depending on the route and customs density.

Are customs duties included in the price?

The provided shipping quote usually covers transport and insurance costs. Since customs duties vary based on the vehicle's value and your legal status, they are calculated separately and paid to the customs administration.

Can crashed or non-running vehicles be transported?

Yes, crashed or mechanically faulty vehicles whose wheels can turn can be loaded onto our transport vehicles with the help of a crane and transported.

What does the insurance coverage include?

All loads in our vehicles are under CMR insurance. Additionally, additional casco/shipping insurance specific to your vehicle's value is made to secure all risks during the journey.
