International Shipping from Izmir to the UK

Our team is a leader in the sector for international shipping from Izmir to the UK. Our logistics operation, starting from Izmir and ending in the UK, is safely carried out via Çeşme or Istanbul port connections.

Key Considerations in International Shipping

International shipping is more than just moving items from one place to another. Especially UK customs regulations and logistics dynamics via Çeşme or Istanbul port connections affect the process. Our team plans all these stages on your behalf.

Service Detail: With our cross-border logistics solutions, we offer professional process management from A to Z, including customs clearance. We also evaluate Truck (TIR) or Minivan options based on the volume of your belongings.

Logistics Route and Timing

Our transport vehicles move via Çeşme or Istanbul port connections, reaching the destination through Dover or Felixstowe. The distance between Izmir and the UK is approximately 3,400 km, and delivery times may vary depending on customs procedures.

You can track your cargo in real-time through our vehicle tracking systems and receive 24/7 information from our operations team during the journey.

Why Choose Han Bros Cargo?

We operate with the motto of budget-friendly costs and trouble-free service. We provide "All-Risk" insurance against potential damages. Additionally, with our experienced personnel in international shipping processes, you can move without facing language barriers or procedural confusion.

Process Steps

  • Planning: Free survey and volume determination at your address in Izmir.
  • Preparation: International standard packaging of all belongings.
  • Customs: Preparation of export declarations from Turkey and import declarations to the UK.
  • Transfer: A secure journey of approximately 3,400 km.
  • Delivery: Delivery to the room at your new address and collection of waste materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the transport take?

On average, our vehicles departing from Izmir deliver within 10-14 days. Our express service takes 4-6 days.

Are there customs duties?

Tax-free shipping is possible if you meet the ToR (Transfer of Residence) requirements for personal effects. Our customs consultants will guide you through the international shipping process.

What does the insurance cover?

Our international transport insurance fully covers major risks such as accidents, overturning, fire, and theft.

Conclusion

Contact us for professional support regarding your international shipping requests from Izmir to the UK. Our logistics department aims to provide you with the best experience through our Aegean region distribution network.
