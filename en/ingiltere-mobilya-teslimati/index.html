UK Furniture Delivery

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer door to door furniture delivery services to the UK. We deliver your furniture safely with professional assembly service, insured transport and on-time delivery guarantee.

Furniture Delivery Services

  • Door to Door Delivery: Full service from your home to new address
  • Professional Assembly: Post-transport furniture assembly and installation service
  • Insured Transport: All transports are covered by insurance
  • On-Time Delivery: Delivery guarantee on specified date and time
  • Careful Packing: Careful packing and protection of your furniture

Furniture Delivery Process

Our furniture delivery process is managed professionally and orderly:

  • Planning: Delivery date and time planning is done
  • Packing: Furniture is carefully packed and protected
  • Transport: Safe and controlled transport process
  • Delivery: On-time delivery to specified address
  • Assembly: Optional assembly and installation service
