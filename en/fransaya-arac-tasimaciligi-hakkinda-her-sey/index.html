We Bring Your Vehicle Safely to France: Step-by-Step Shipping Guide

When moving from Turkey to France (from Paris to Nice, from Lyon to Marseille) or when selling/sending your vehicle there, your biggest concern may be safe shipment and complex customs procedures. As Han Bros Cargo, we eliminate these concerns with our expertise in vehicle transportation. With our modern auto carrier fleet and our team dominant in international legislation, we take all kinds of motor vehicles from passenger cars to luxury SUVs, from classic vehicles to motorcycles from your door in Turkey and deliver them to their new address in France.

Solutions We Offer in France Vehicle Shipping

Professional steps we offer in your vehicles' European journey:

  • Expert Customs Support: We provide full consultancy on France's strict import rules and compliance with EU environmental standards (Euro norms).
  • Tax Exemption in Personal Effects Status: If you are moving to France and your vehicle has been registered to you for at least 6 months, we guide you to bring your vehicle without paying customs duty.
  • Insured and CMR Guaranteed Transport: Your vehicle is put under full financial protection with CMR insurance from the moment of loading.
  • Multiple and Single Towing Options: We offer 8-car large auto carriers according to your budget or single closed-mode tow trucks for urgent/special shipments.
  • Live GPS Tracking: We allow you to instantly monitor where your vehicle is and which country it is passing through during the journey.

Why France Vehicle Shipping with Han Bros Cargo?

France logistics requires attention, especially due to 'Carte Grise' (License) and tax processes. As Han Bros Cargo, thanks to the network we have established with our French customs brokers, we ensure that your vehicle proceeds without getting stuck at the France entrance. We use special fixing and protection techniques to protect your vehicles from external factors (stone chips, weather conditions, etc.) along the way. By offering our customers not just a transport but a turnkey service, we also guide them in temporary plate transactions or license change processes. Prepare to drive freely on the roads of France by entrusting your vehicle to Han Bros experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to send a vehicle from Turkey to France?

Depending on the route and customs density, vehicle shipping is completed in an average of 10 to 15 days.

Do I pay tax when bringing a vehicle to France?

If you are going for settlement purposes (with a residence permit) and you meet the conditions (6-month rule, etc.), you may be exempt from tax. Otherwise, commercial import taxes apply.

Do you carry motorcycles?

Yes, we perform safe transportation with special apparatused crating and fixing systems for motorcycles.

Can I carry personal belongings inside my vehicle?

Due to customs rules, it is not recommended to carry personal belongings inside the vehicle. However, in some cases it may be allowed with a limited amount and on condition of being declared; please consult us.

Is the delivery made to the door in France?

Yes, we make delivery to the door at every point where our trucks can enter; in narrow streets, we perform the delivery at the nearest suitable point.
