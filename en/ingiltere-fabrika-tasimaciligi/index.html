UK Factory Moving

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer industrial factory moving services to the UK. We provide project-based planning with special transport solutions for industrial equipment, machinery and heavy loads.

Our Factory Moving Services

  • Industrial Equipment Transport: Special transport solutions for heavy machinery, industrial equipment and large volume loads
  • Crane and Special Equipment Support: Safe loading and unloading with professional crane and special equipment
  • Project-Based Planning: Detailed planning and scheduling for factory moving
  • Insured Transport: All industrial equipment is protected with comprehensive insurance
  • Customs Procedures: Customs procedures and document preparation for industrial equipment

Factory Moving Process to UK

Our factory moving process consists of the following steps:

  • Inspection and Planning: Equipment inventory, volume calculation and route planning
  • Price Quote: Transparent pricing and special project opportunities
  • Special Equipment Preparation: Crane and special equipment preparation
  • Loading: Safe loading and securing operations
  • Transport: Safe shipping with special vehicle fleet
  • Customs Procedures: Customs procedures for industrial equipment
  • Delivery: Safe delivery and equipment inspection

Customs and Document Procedures

Customs procedures are important in factory moving to the UK for industrial equipment. We provide professional support regarding equipment list, customs declarations and tax calculations.
