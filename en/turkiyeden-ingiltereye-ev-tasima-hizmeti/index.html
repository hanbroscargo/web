Service Details

Packing Service

Our professional team carefully packs your household goods. Special packaging techniques are used for delicate items. All items are recorded in the inventory list and photographed.

Customs Procedures

We manage post-Brexit customs procedures. We provide full support regarding household goods list preparation, customs declarations and tax calculations.

Transport and Delivery

We transport safely with our modern vehicle fleet. You can track your goods throughout the process with GPS tracking system. We offer door to door delivery guarantee.

Insurance Options

  • Basic Insurance: Basic coverage for standard items
  • Comprehensive Insurance: Extended coverage for valuable items
  • Special Insurance: High value insurance for special items such as antiques, works of art

Pricing

Home moving prices from Turkey to the UK are determined by goods volume, distance, selected package and additional services. You get clear information in advance with transparent pricing.
