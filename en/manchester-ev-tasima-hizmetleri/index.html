Manchester Home Moving Services

When moving your home to Manchester, if you want reliable and professional service, Han Bros Cargo offers you the most suitable solutions. We provide special home moving services to Manchester, an important city in Northern England, with fast delivery and safe shipping guarantee.

Services Offered

  • Door to Door Service: Your household goods are collected, packed and delivered to your new address.

  • Professional Packing: All your items are packed and protected with special packaging materials.

  • Safe Transport: Your items are transported safely with special equipment and experienced staff.

  • Assembly Service: Your furniture is assembled and placed in your new home.

Moving Process

Our Manchester home moving process is transparent and professional:

  • Quote Preparation: A list of your household goods is made and a detailed quote is prepared.

  • Planning: Moving date is determined and route planning is done.

  • Packing: Professional team carefully packs and labels your items.

  • Transport: Transport to Manchester is carried out with safe vehicles.

  • Delivery: Delivery is made to your new address and assembly service is provided.
