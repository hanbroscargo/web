UK E-Commerce Fulfillment

Han Bros Cargo offers comprehensive e-commerce fulfillment services to the UK. We provide fully integrated solutions with inventory management, order processing, packing, labeling, cargo integration and customer delivery.

Our E-Commerce Fulfillment Services

  • Inventory Management: Professional stock tracking and management system, real-time stock updates
  • Order Processing: Fast order receiving, preparation and processing service
  • Packing and Labeling: Professional packing, labeling and cargo preparation
  • Cargo Integration: Full integration with cargo network, automatic shipping
  • API Integration: API integration support with your e-commerce platforms

Fulfillment Process

Our e-commerce fulfillment process consists of the following steps:

  • Stock Receipt: Product acceptance to storage facility and stock registration
  • Order Receiving: Automatic order receiving from your e-commerce platform
  • Order Preparation: Fast product selection, packing and labeling
  • Cargo Integration: Automatic integration with cargo company
  • Delivery: Fast and safe delivery to customer

API Integration

We offer API integration support with your e-commerce platforms. Orders are automatically transferred to our system and processed. Stock status is updated in real-time.

Inventory Management

You can track the status of your products in real-time with our professional stock tracking and management system. We facilitate your inventory management with low stock alerts, automatic order suggestions and detailed reporting.
