Furniture Transport Between Turkey and UK

When transporting furniture between Turkey and UK, if you want reliable and professional service, Han Bros Cargo offers you the most suitable solutions. Your furniture is transported safely with international furniture shipping, special packing, customs procedures and insured transport services.

Services Offered

  • Special Packing: Your furniture is packed with special packaging materials and protected from damage during transport.
  • Customs Procedures: All customs procedures and documentation processes are handled by our professional team.
  • Insured Transport: All your furniture is insured, you can transport safely.
  • Assembly Service: Your furniture is assembled and placed at your new address.

Transport Process

Our furniture transport process is planned and professional:

  • Quote Preparation: A list of your furniture is made and a detailed quote is prepared.
  • Packing: Professional team packs your furniture with special packaging materials.
  • Loading: Safe loading process is carried out with special equipment.
  • Transport: Transport from Turkey to UK is carried out with safe vehicles.
  • Customs Procedures: Customs procedures are completed in UK.
  • Delivery and Assembly: Your furniture is delivered to your new address and assembled.
