Budget-Friendly Solutions on the Road to Holland: Quality and Cheap Moving

The biggest expense item in an international moving process is usually logistics. In your search for the 'best price for household goods shipping to Holland', as Han Bros Cargo, we offer you not just a figure, but a real cost optimization. When shipping to a country with a very advanced logistics infrastructure like Holland, choosing the right routes and vehicle types can provide savings of up to 30% in the total price. We guarantee the most competitive prices with our directly 'from producer to consumer' logistics understanding, by removing intermediary brokers with our own fleet organizing regular flights to every point from Amsterdam to Maastricht.

Our Smart Logistics Strategies That Lower Costs

Our basic methods that allow us to offer the best price in the market are as follows:

  • Groupage (Partial) Shipping: If you do not have enough items to fill a truck, we allow you to share the truck space with our other customers, ensuring that you pay only as much as the volume (m3) you occupy.
  • Early Booking Model: We offer a fixed price advantage that is not affected by exchange rates and season intensity in reservations made 45-60 days before your moving date.
  • Customs Exemption Consultancy: By correctly applying the personal effects exemption (Verhuizing) conditions at Dutch customs, we legally save you from taxes amounting to thousands of Euros.
  • Zero Intermediary Policy: Since we use our own trucks and our own warehouses, we do not reflect extra agency commissions in our prices.

Transparent Pricing: No Room for Surprises

The biggest problem in the logistics sector is 'extras' that come out later. Our offer at Han Bros Cargo is clear. Every item from packaging materials to customs fees, from assembly service to insurance premium is clearly stated in our offer. We apply the 'Door-to-Door All-Inclusive' model by anticipating everything from the external elevator need in the narrow streets of Amsterdam to the terminal expenses at the Port of Rotterdam. Our goal is to protect your budget at your new start in Holland and to be a safe haven.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest way to move to Holland?

For small and medium-sized houses, 'Partial (Groupage)' shipping is undisputedly the most economical method.

Are prices determined over m3 (volume)?

Yes, the main unit in international shipping is m3. A net price is given by making a volume calculation according to your item list.

Do you have special discounts for students?

Yes, we have special 'Mini-Cargo' tariffs for our students who will only send boxes.

Are customs duties included in the price quote?

We manage the exemption procedures in personal effects moves and usually no tax occurs. If it is a commercial shipment, taxes are paid to the state and calculated by us.

Do prices change according to the seasons?

Prices are usually more advantageous in the winter months and early booking periods compared to the summer period.
