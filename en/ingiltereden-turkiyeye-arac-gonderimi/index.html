Vehicle Shipping from UK to Turkey

As Han Bros Cargo, we offer expert service for vehicle shipping from UK to Turkey. With our experienced team, we are with you regarding post-Brexit customs procedures, vehicle documents, insurance and safe delivery.

Our Service Scope

  • Sea Transport: Safe vehicle transport with ro-ro ships
  • Road Transport: Direct delivery with special vehicle carriers
  • Combined Transport: Flexible solutions with sea and road combination
  • Customs Procedures: Tracking of post-Brexit customs procedures
  • Document Preparation: Vehicle documents, registration and customs documents
  • Insurance: Comprehensive vehicle insurance options

Post-Brexit Process

After Brexit, customs procedures for vehicle shipping from UK to Turkey have changed. Our professional team manages the necessary documents, tax calculations and customs procedures for your vehicles.

Transport Process

  1. Application and Document Check: Vehicle documents and registration are checked
  2. Customs Preparation: Post-Brexit customs documents are prepared
  3. Transport Planning: Sea or road transport plan is created
  4. Loading: Your vehicles are loaded safely
  5. Tracking: Transport process is tracked throughout
  6. Delivery: Delivered in Turkey after customs procedures

Customs Procedures

  • Document Preparation: Vehicle registration, insurance, customs declaration
  • Tax Calculation: Special consumption tax, VAT and customs tax calculations
  • Customs Tracking: Tracking and coordination of customs procedures
  • Delivery: Vehicle delivery after customs procedures
