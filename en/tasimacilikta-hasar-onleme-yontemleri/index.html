Damage Prevention Methods in Shipping: Professional Protection Guide

Your Belongings are Safe with Us: Zero Damage Vision in Logistics

The biggest source of concern in international shipping is the possibility of damage to items traveling thousands of kilometers. As Han Bros Cargo, we do not leave this possibility to chance. 'Damage prevention' is not a choice for us, but an operational necessity. In this long journey that lasts from Turkey to the furthest points of Europe; there are many risk factors such as sudden brakes, road vibrations, humidity changes, and customs examinations. With our special packaging and loading protocols developed with engineering discipline, we have succeeded in reducing damage rates far below industry standards. Remember; the best insurance is to ensure that the damage never occurs.

Professional Techniques We Use to Prevent Damage

The basic layers of protection we apply for a safe shipment are as follows:

  • Multi-Layer Packaging (Premium Packing): For fragile items, beyond bubble wrap, we use kraft paper, corner protectors, and double-walled boxes. Each piece is 'armored' according to its own sensitivity.
  • Lashing (Cargo Securing): Movement of items inside the truck is the biggest cause of damage. With dunnage bags, securing straps, and special wedges, your load is virtually integrated into the truck floor.
  • Climate and Humidity Control: Especially for furniture and textile products, we use special desiccants that ensure humidity balance inside the container and truck.
  • Trained Operation Team: Carrying items is not just strength, it's technical knowledge. Our team receives continuous training on adjusting the center of gravity and stacking.

Special Protection Protocol for Each Item

An ordinary box and an antique vase or a 4K television cannot be carried in the same way. As Han Bros Cargo, we offer 'Custom Crating' service for your high-value and sensitive products. Special wooden scaffolds prepared by taking the dimensions of the item provide full protection throughout the shipment. Also, we digitally photograph and report every detail from 'allen' and 'screw' management to glass cover protection in furniture assembly and disassembly processes. With us, every piece reaches its new address with the quality at the moment it was packaged.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my belongings are damaged during transport?

All our shipments are within the scope of insurance. At the moment of damage, our team keeps a report and we compensate your financial loss by starting the insurance process immediately.

Who is responsible if I do the packaging myself?

According to logistics rules, insurance companies are usually not responsible for internal damage of items you package yourself. We recommend you to leave the packaging to Han Bros experts for full assurance.

Which items are most often damaged?

Angular furniture, electronic devices, and glass products are the riskiest. For these products, we apply extra 'double buffering'.

Do road conditions (rain, snow) affect my belongings?

The vehicles we use are covered with 'Hard-Box' or high-strength tarpaulins. Waterproofing checks are done periodically.

Are the packages ruined during customs examination?

Customs officers can open packages for examination. However, our personnel are beside them and restore the package to its original security after the examination.

Damage-free shipping is an art, and we are its master. Move in peace with Han Bros Cargo!
