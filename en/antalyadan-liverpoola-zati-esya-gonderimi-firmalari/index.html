Personal Effects Shipping Companies from Antalya to Liverpool

With our staff fully mastering the customs legislation that changed after Brexit, we are with you for your needs for personal effects shipping companies from Antalya to Liverpool. Our adventure starting from Antalya ends at your address in Liverpool.

Our Logistics Process from Antalya to Liverpool

For shipments from Antalya, we use road transport to access Europe. The distance between the two points is approximately 3,700 km, and our estimated delivery time is 14-16 days. We offer safe packaging with our Mediterranean region logistics network. We provide special solutions and advantages for those settling in the UK from touristic regions.

Personal Effects Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our service focused on Personal Belongings and Customs Exemption (ToR). Applying for 'Transfer of Residence' (ToR1) is critical when moving to the UK. If you get approval, you can be exempt from VAT and Customs Duty. We provide special boxes for packable products such as clothes, books, and kitchenware. Additionally, C3 forms and customs declarations are managed by us.

Professional Packaging and Customs

The safety of your belongings is our priority. For fragile items, we use a "kraft paper + bubble wrap" combination. On the customs side, our expert team manages the procedures that have become more complex after Brexit on your behalf.
