UK Vehicle Transport Service

Han Bros Cargo offers comprehensive vehicle transport services to the UK. We provide safe service with our experienced team in passenger car, SUV, commercial vehicle and motorcycle transport.

Our Vehicle Transport Services

  • Passenger Car Transport: Special transport solutions for cars and passenger vehicles
  • SUV Transport: Specially equipped carriers for large vehicles
  • Commercial Vehicle Transport: Service for vans, minibuses and commercial vehicles
  • Motorcycle Transport: Motorcycle and scooter transport service
  • Special Vehicles: Special service for classic and collection vehicles

Transport Options

  • Enclosed Vehicle Transport: Protected transport for valuable vehicles, complete protection from weather
  • Open Vehicle Transport: Economic solution, affordable option for standard vehicles
  • Container Transport: Safe transport with special container

Service Process

  1. Inspection and Planning: Vehicle type, dimensions and delivery points are determined
  2. Document Preparation: Vehicle registration, insurance documents and required paperwork are prepared
  3. Loading: Careful loading and securing by professional team
  4. Transport: Safe and insured shipping process
  5. Customs Procedures: Post-Brexit customs procedures
  6. Delivery: Door to door delivery and vehicle inspection

Insurance Coverage

All our vehicle shipments are protected with comprehensive insurance. Fast solution and compensation process is guaranteed in case of damage. We offer high value insurance options for valuable vehicles.

Why Han Bros Cargo?

  • Experienced team for all vehicle types
  • Enclosed and open vehicle transport options
  • Comprehensive insurance coverage and safe transport
  • Customs procedures and document preparation support
  • On-time delivery guarantee
