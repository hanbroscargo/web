Frequently Asked Questions

How is the shipping price from Eskisehir to Germany calculated?

Pricing is calculated according to the total m3 volume of the items, the pickup point in Eskisehir, and the distance of the destination city in Germany.

Which is the cheapest transport method?

If you don't have urgency in terms of time, regular partial (groupage) road transport is the most economical and budget-friendly choice.

Will there be extra fees after getting a price quote?

No, the quotes offered by Han Bros Cargo are all-inclusive (customs, insurance, fuel, etc.) and you will not encounter surprise costs later.

How long does the transport time take?

Road shipments from Eskisehir to Germany are completed within an average of 10 to 14 business days depending on customs density.

How can I track the cargo?

You can see the location of your vehicle via 24/7 live GPS through our customer panel or with the tracking number sent to you.
