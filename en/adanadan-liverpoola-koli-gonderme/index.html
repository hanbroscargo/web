Parcel Shipping from Adana to Liverpool

Are you looking for a reliable solution away from the complexity of logistics processes? With the corporate solutions we offer for parcel shipping from Adana to Liverpool, your belongings are collected from Adana and delivered to your door in Liverpool.

Professional Packaging and Customs Clearance

The safety of your belongings is our priority. We use a 'kraft paper + bubble wrap' combination for your fragile items. On the customs side, our expert team manages the procedures that became more difficult after Brexit on your behalf.

Parcel Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our Express Parcel and Package Shipping focused service. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable. Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and entered into the tracking system. Additionally, you can benefit from our 48-72 hour delivery option for urgent shipments.

Our Logistics Process from Adana to Liverpool

We use Mersin Port or road integration for shipments from Adana. The distance between the two points is approximately 3,900 km, and our estimated delivery time is 14-17 days. We serve with a large vehicle fleet from the Çukurova region. We offer special advantages to our customers with economic solutions for large-volume household goods shipments.

Process Steps

  • Survey: Volume detection at your address in Adana.
  • Operation: Parcels collected from your door are barcoded and processed in the tracking system.
  • Customs: Export procedures from Adana and import procedures to Liverpool.
  • Delivery: Scheduled delivery and installation support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the insurance cover?

Our All-Risk insurance covers risks such as accidents, fire, and theft during the process from packaging to delivery of the goods.

Are there customs duties?

If you receive ToR approval in the status of personal effects, you do not pay tax. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable.

Do you have storage services?

Yes, we have secure warehouses in both Turkey and the UK. You can store your belongings for as long as you wish.
