Customs Procedures in International Shipping: Detailed Guide

Don't Get Lost in the Customs Labyrinth: Professional Customs Clearance Guide

Customs clearance is undoubtedly the most time-consuming and technical stage in international shipping. As Han Bros Cargo, we overcome customs barriers with our digital and operational speed for your shipments from Turkey to European Union countries (Germany, France, Netherlands, Austria, Belgium, Luxembourg) and the United Kingdom. This guide is prepared to minimize the procedures you will encounter at border gates, whether it's your individual personal effects or commercial loads. Customs is not an obstacle, but a process that needs to be managed correctly.

Basic Documents and Their Importance in the Customs Process

For a successful customs clearance, it is vital that these documents are prepared completely and error-free:

  • ATR Circulation Certificate: It is the document that ensures the free circulation of industrial products without customs duty in accordance with the customs union agreement between Turkey and the EU.
  • EUR.1 Circulation Certificate: It allows benefiting from preferential tax rates in agricultural products and some coal-steel products.
  • Customs Declaration: It is the basic document where the nature, value, and quantity of the item are reported to the official authorities. It is prepared digitally by our expert consultants.
  • Packing List: It is a detailed breakdown containing what is in which box, weights, and measurements. It is the most referred source in customs examinations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do the customs procedures myself?

Although theoretically possible, it is strongly recommended that you use an authorized customs consultant or representative as technical terms and declaration filling processes are complex.

What is the difference between ATR and EUR.1 documents?

While ATR generally gives tax-free circulation rights for industrial products between Turkey and the EU, EUR.1 is used for countries with free trade agreements and certain product groups.

Is the personal effects (household goods) customs clearance process different?

Yes, personal effects are declared differently from commercial products and can usually be exempted from tax with a 'residence transfer' document.

Will my items be opened during customs procedures?

Customs directorates have the authority to physically check the cargo content within the framework of random selection or suspicious situation risk analysis.

What happens if my documents are missing?

Missing documents lead to the item being held at customs and additional storage (warehousing) costs. That's why Han Bros Cargo checks all documents before the truck departs.
