Frequently Asked Questions

Is customs duty paid when moving household goods to Luxembourg?

If you have documents proving your settlement in Luxembourg and your items have been in your use for at least 6 months, you may be exempted from customs duty within the scope of personal effects.

How many days does shipping from Turkey to Luxembourg take?

Our standard delivery time in road transport is 10-14 business days, including customs. This period can be further shortened with Express options.

Do you install my furniture in Luxembourg?

Yes, within our 'Premium' service package, de-assembly and assembly services in your new home are provided by our professional teams.

Do you have item storage facilities in Luxembourg?

In our contracted warehouses within Luxembourg or in border regions, we can safely store your belongings for as long as you wish.

Is there a limit in partial (piece goods) transport?

We can send piece goods in any volume starting from a minimum of 1-2 cubic meters.
