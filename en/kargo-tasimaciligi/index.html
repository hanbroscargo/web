Cargo Transportation Service

As Han Bros Cargo, we provide comprehensive services in international cargo transportation. We provide reliable, fast and economical transportation solutions for your parcel, package and cargo shipments.

Our Service Scope

Our cargo transportation service offers a wide range of services from small packages to large parcel shipments. Options include partial cargo, express cargo, standard cargo and economical cargo. The most suitable transportation method is determined according to customer needs.

Parcel and Package Shipping

We offer special services for small and medium-sized parcel shipments. Pricing is made according to parcel sizes and weights. Special price packages are available for standard parcel sizes. Packaging service is also offered for parcel shipments. Special cardboard boxes and protective materials are used for safe packaging.

Partial Cargo

We provide partial cargo service for medium-volume cargo shipments. In partial cargo transportation, cargo from different customers is combined in the same vehicle to provide cost-effective transportation. This method is economical especially for small and medium-sized businesses. Flexible delivery options are offered for partial cargo shipments.

Express Cargo

We provide express cargo service for urgent cargo shipments. In express cargo transportation, cargo is prioritized and delivered in the shortest time. Express cargo option is suitable especially for time-critical shipments. Online tracking and SMS notification services are offered for express cargo shipments.

Standard Cargo

Our standard cargo service offers economical pricing with normal delivery times. Standard cargo transportation is an ideal option for non-urgent shipments. Bulk shipment discounts are offered for standard cargo shipments. Special price packages are available for customers who make regular cargo shipments.

Economical Cargo

We provide economical cargo service for budget-friendly cargo shipments. In economical cargo transportation, cargo is combined and transported with longer delivery times and lower prices. Economical cargo option is suitable for non-urgent shipments.

Packaging and Wrapping

We provide professional packaging service for the safe transport of your cargo. Special packaging methods are applied according to product type. Special protective packaging for fragile products, anti-static packaging for electronic products are used. Packaging service is provided for standard parcel sizes and special parcel sizes.

Customs Procedures

Customs procedures are an important stage in international cargo transportation. Our team, familiar with customs legislation, provides professional support in preparing all customs documents, invoice preparation and following customs procedures. Import, export and transit procedures are managed. We provide special consultancy services for customs procedures.

Tracking and Notification

You can track your cargo transportation process in real-time through our online tracking system. Transportation route, location information and estimated delivery date are shared through the system. You are informed about cargo status through SMS and email notification services. You can also track through the mobile application.

Insurance and Guarantee

We offer comprehensive insurance options in all our cargo transportation services. An insurance policy is issued according to the value of your cargo and full protection is provided during transportation. In case of damage, the compensation process is managed in coordination with the insurance company. Insurance coverage and limits are determined according to customer needs.

Delivery Options

We offer different options for delivery of your cargo. Options include door-to-door delivery, warehouse delivery, terminal delivery and post office delivery. Delivery time and location are planned according to customer needs. Appointment can be made before delivery. Signature and photo confirmation are obtained after delivery.

Special Services

We offer special services for valuable goods, electronic products, medicines, food and dangerous goods transportation. Extra security measures are taken in valuable goods transportation. Special climate-controlled vehicles are used for cold chain transportation. Special permits and documents are prepared for dangerous goods transportation.

Reliable, professional and quality service in cargo transportation with Han Bros Cargo. Contact us for your cargo shipping needs.
