Our Flexible and Comprehensive Cargo Options

We offer logistics services optimized for every shipment type:

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum weight limit for sending cargo abroad?

No, we accept shipments of any scale, from a small 1 kg package to a 20-ton full truck load.

How do you calculate cargo fees?

Charging is done according to the actual weight and volumetric weight (desi) of your package, whichever is higher, and according to the destination country.

Can I send perishable food inside my cargo?

Certain products can be sent with our express service and special documentation; however, food shipments may be restricted according to the customs laws of countries. Please ask our consultant.

Do I have to pay tax during shipping?

VAT and customs duties of the destination country are applied to commercial products. Tax exemptions can be applied for personal effects and certain sample shipments.

What happens if my cargo is damaged?

All our cargos are under CMR insurance. In case of damage detection, the compensation process is initiated within the framework of international shipping contracts.
