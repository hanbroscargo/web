House-to-House Relocation Companies from Turkey to Scotland

How Does the Professional Relocation Process Work?

The most important point to consider in transport operations from Turkey to Scotland is customs procedures and packaging quality. We use packaging materials at international standards for the safety of your belongings. Thanks to our full mastery of the changed customs legislation after Brexit, we provide delivery without getting stuck in bureaucratic obstacles.

Transport and Logistics Planning

Each transport operation has unique requirements. In our house-to-house relocation service, we first perform a free survey and volume calculation. This way, we can create accurate planning. You can instantly monitor the process from the receipt of your items from Turkey to their delivery to Scotland via our online tracking system.

Step by Step Transport Process

  • Survey and Planning: On-site inspection of your items and creation of the transport plan.
  • Packaging and Wrapping: Packaging with special protective materials for fragile items, furniture, and electronic devices.
  • Customs Procedures: Preparation of necessary documents and management of Turkey export and Scotland entry customs clearance processes.
  • Logistics and Transfer: Realization of road or sea (intermodal) transport with our modern vehicle fleet.
  • Delivery and Installation: Delivery of items to the room at your new address and optional furniture installation.

Frequently Asked Questions and Detailed Information

The international transport sector requires experience and meticulousness. Every stage from company selection to customs clearance, from insurance to delivery, must be managed as a whole. Below, we have touched upon the issues that our customers are most curious about when receiving relocation services.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How long does the shipping take?

The average delivery time varies between 4 and 14 days, depending on the selected transport mode (Express Minivan or Truck) and customs density. We have regular flights on the Turkey to Scotland route.

2. Do I pay customs duty?

In personal effects (personal household items) transport, you may be exempt from customs duty if you meet certain conditions (Transfer of Residence - ToR). For commercial goods, taxation is made in accordance with the legislation. Our experts provide free consultancy on this subject.

3. Are my belongings insured?

Yes, your belongings are secured under All-Risk insurance or CMR insurance in all our transport operations. In case of possible damage, insurance procedures are followed by our company.

4. Can I do the packaging myself?

You can box your personal items (clothes, books, etc.), but it is strongly recommended that the packaging of furniture, white goods, and sensitive items be done by our professional team for insurance coverage and safe transport.
