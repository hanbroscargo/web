Choosing the Most Efficient Vehicle for the Austria Route

In international transport, 'moving the right item with the right vehicle' both reduces costs and increases security. When sending cargo from Turkey to Austria, we use different vehicle models according to the volume and urgency of your belongings and the physical conditions of the destination. The Han Bros Cargo fleet consists of GPS-tracked and insulated vehicles complying with European Union standards (Euro 6). Here are the types of vehicles you can choose according to your needs:

1. Mega Trucks and Solo Trucks (Full Transport)

If you are moving a 3+1 or larger house or have a volume of over 40 m3, our 'Full Transport' service is the best for you. The entire vehicle is allocated only for your belongings. Advantage: It does not make any other stops on the way, your belongings do not mix with anyone else's, and the delivery time is much shorter.

2. Groupage (Partial) Trucks

It is the most popular model for those looking for an economical solution. Beloning of multiple customers going on the same route are collected in the same truck. Thanks to Han Bros Cargo's advanced stacking technology, physical barriers are placed between belongings. Advantage: Instead of the cost of renting the entire vehicle, you only pay for the cubic meters you occupy.

3. Panel Vans and Small Tower Trucks (Express Service)

We use our 13 m3 or 17 m3 panel vans, especially for narrow-street Vienna regions or for low-volume (1-10 m3) items that need to be delivered urgently. Advantage: They can enter the city without being caught by truck bans and can deliver within 4-5 days under 'Express Shipping'.

4. Vehicle Carriers (Auto Logistics)

Our double-decker or single closed tow trucks specifically designed for car and SUV transport. We safely send your vehicle to Austria along with your belongings or in a separate shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which vehicle my items are suitable for?

During our free survey service, our expert measures your items and reports the most economical vehicle type to you.

Is there a possibility of items getting mixed in your trucks?

No, we perform special barcoding for each customer, and in partial shipments, we divide the items into compartments so that they do not mix with each other.

Can big trucks enter narrow streets in Austria?

Unfortunately, trucks cannot enter every street. In these cases, we provide 'shuttle' service by transferring your belongings to small panel vans in our warehouse.

What are the safety features of your vehicles?

All our vehicles have anti-vibration systems, fire-fighting equipment, and satellite systems that can be tracked from the center 24/7.

Is express panel van transport more expensive?

Due to time gain and operational speed, the unit cost is slightly higher than truck transport, but it is unrivaled for urgent moves.
