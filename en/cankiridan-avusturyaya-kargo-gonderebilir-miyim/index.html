Cankiri Austria Cargo and Logistics Services

Reliable Bridge from Cankiri to Europe: Austria Cargo Line

In all cargo shipments from Cankiri, which is progressing towards becoming one of the important production and logistics centers of the Central Anatolia Region, to Austria, the heart of Europe, Han Bros Cargo is with you. We deliver all kinds of loads from Cankiri's industrial products to individual cargo needs safely to all cities of Austria such as Vienna, Graz, Salzburg, and Linz. By combining the discipline of Central Anatolia with international transportation standards, we offer an uninterrupted service on the Cankiri-Austria route.

Why Should You Get Cankiri Origin Service with Han Bros Cargo?

Professional solutions we offer on the Cankiri route are as follows:

  • Fast Consolidation: We minimize waiting times by quickly loading cargos we receive from Cankiri onto Austria vehicles at our logistics centers.
  • Economic Partial Solutions: You don't need to rent a full truck for a single box or a few pallets of belongings you will send from Cankiri; we provide freight savings with shared loading.
  • Customs Consultancy: We guide our exporters and individual customers from Cankiri regarding Austria's (EU member) customs rules.
  • Door-to-Door Delivery: We deliver the cargos we pick up from your address in Cankiri to your recipient's door in Austria against signature.

Secure Shipment Process from Cankiri to Austria

The security of your shipment comes before everything for us. The modern vehicle fleet and reinforced packaging systems we use as Han Bros Cargo ensure that your cargo is protected from external factors until it leaves Cankiri, crosses the Alps, and reaches Austria. Thanks to GPS tracking, you can see where your cargo is instantly and be informed of every stage with our professional notifications. With our local partner network in Cankiri, we can pick up from every point within the city (Orta, Ilgaz, Kursunlu, etc.).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does it take for cargo from Cankiri to reach Austria?

Depending on the shipping mode, our road partial cargos reach the address in Austria in an average of 8 to 12 business days.

Can I send food products inside my cargo?

Due to European Union and Austria customs rules, non-commercial limited food products (dry food, etc.) can be sent with appropriate packaging; please call for details.

Can I have the recipient pay the cargo fee?

Yes, for corporate shipments we have agreements with, 'recipient pays' (Collect) shipping options are also available.

What should I do to prevent my cargo from getting stuck at Austria customs?

If you give us the item list and invoice/proforma values completely, customs processes are managed smoothly by Han Bros.

Is personal effects (used items) shipping possible?

Yes, we provide personal effects transport and customs exemption guidance service for individuals moving from Cankiri to Austria.
