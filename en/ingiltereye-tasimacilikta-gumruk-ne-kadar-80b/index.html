Customs costs for shipping to United Kingdom have changed significantly after Brexit. Post-Brexit customs processes have become more complex, unlike EU member countries; customs declarations, tax calculations, and special documents are required. Tax calculations are made according to item value and type; customs tax, VAT, and special consumption tax are calculated. Exemption conditions apply for tax exemptions under certain conditions; some exemptions are available for personal items, but exemptions for commercial goods are limited. Customs document requirements have become more detailed after Brexit; invoices, certificates of origin, EUR.1 forms, and special documents are required. Customs brokerage services include professional consulting and document preparation; our expert team manages all processes. Customs processing times have extended after Brexit; procedures that normally take 2-3 days can extend to 5-7 days. All customs costs are calculated in advance and presented transparently.
