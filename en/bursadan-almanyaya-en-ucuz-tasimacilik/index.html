Secrets of Low-Cost Logistics from Bursa to Germany

In shipments from Bursa, the city of industry and textiles, to Germany, looking for the "cheapest" option is not just focusing on the price, but also maximizing operational efficiency. At Han Bros Cargo, we develop budget-friendly logistics models to increase the competitive power of businesses and individuals in Bursa on the Germany route. On this important 2,500-kilometer commercial bridge on the Bursa-Germany line, we have compiled the ways to deliver your cargo with the most suitable freight fees for you.

'Han Bros' Strategies Reducing Costs

Our methods that will enable you to optimize your shipping budget between 30% and 50% are:

  • Groupage (Partial) Shipping: You don't need to fill an entire truck. In our partial vehicles departing from Bursa every week, you pay only for the area your item occupies.
  • Early Booking and Planning: By planning your shipment 15 days in advance, you can be protected from high freight prices that occur at the last minute and benefit from our special 'off-season' discounts.
  • Bursa Depot Optimization: If you deliver your load to our Bursa warehouse yourself or integrate it into our trucks departing from Istanbul, we provide savings in local pickup fees.
  • Document Accuracy: We reduce the waiting fees (demurrage) caused by incorrectly prepared customs documents to zero thanks to our expert consultancy.

Special Freight Quotes for Bursa Origin

We offer annual volume contracts specific to Bursa's power in the automotive, textile, and food sectors, reducing your unit cost as your number of shipments increases. Additionally, we increase efficiency by combining loads going to different cities in Germany such as Munich, Berlin, or Hamburg on the same route and reflect this advantage in our prices. With our transparent pricing policy, you know in advance what is included in the freight price and do not encounter surprise extra costs at customs.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the cheapest time to move from Bursa to Germany?

Generally, it is possible to reach the most economical freight fees in the winter months (January-February) when the shipping demand decreases and during early booking periods.

Is partial shipping or a full truck more profitable?

For loads with a volume less than 15 cubic meters (m3), partial (LTL) shipping is always much more advantageous in terms of cost.

Is insurance included in the cheap price?

International CMR responsibility insurance is included in all quotes within Han Bros Cargo. In this way, you do not compromise on your security despite the low price.

How can I reduce customs expenses?

Correct HS coding and the completeness of the ATR movement certificate provide savings by preventing extra inspection expenses that may occur at customs.

Can I send just one box from Bursa to Germany?

Yes, with our box-based express cargo service, you can send your small loads much more affordably and quickly compared to truck-based freights.
