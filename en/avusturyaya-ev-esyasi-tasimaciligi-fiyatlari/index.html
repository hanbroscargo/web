Household Goods Shipping Prices to Austria: Detailed Cost Analysis

When you decide to settle in Austria from Turkey (Vienna, Linz, Salzburg, Graz, etc.), one of the most important items in your budget is logistics costs. At Han Bros Cargo, we offer our customers completely transparent and all-inclusive price packages away from surprise costs. Below, you can find the factors that determine Austria shipping prices and saving methods with 2026 current data.

4 Main Elements Determining Shipping Prices

The figures you encounter when receiving a quote consist of these criteria:

  • Item Volume (Cubic Meter - m3): The space the truck occupies is the most decisive factor in international shipping. As the m3 value of your items increases, the total price increases, but the unit m3 price may decrease.
  • Customs and Consultancy: Procedures required for your items to be cleared at Austrian customs without tax under personal belongings status are included in the freight price.
  • Distance and City: Distance differences from different starting points like Antalya, Ankara, or Istanbul to different destination points like Vienna or Innsbruck are reflected in the price.
  • Additional Services: Comfort packages such as professional packaging, assembly work requiring carpentry, and elevator moving are added according to your preference.

How Can You Save?

Consider these tips to optimize your Austria moving budget:

  • Sort Items: Every item you no longer use is a burden to you as a cubic meter in the freight price. Making a sorting before moving can provide 15-20% savings.
  • Choose Partial Shipping: If you are not sending your entire home but only a few items, you can get much more affordable prices with our vehicle sharing (groupage) model.
  • Early Booking: Making a contract at least 1 month before the moving date provides a quota guarantee and flat-rate advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average m3 price for shipping to Austria?

Although it varies according to volume and route, our m3 unit prices are at market standards and competitive levels. You can use our free survey service for the net figure.

Are customs duties included in the price?

If your transported items fall under personal belongings status, you are exempt from tax. Only the "customs clearance service fee" is included in our prices. Taxes incurred for new commercial products are paid by the customer.

Is the use of an elevator in Vienna an extra cost?

Yes, in cases where building elevator use is not possible, modular elevators rented by our local teams are invoiced under additional service.

How can I make the payment?

You can make your payment through credit card or bank transfer in two stages: on the loading day and before delivery.

Are there hidden costs in your prices?

At Han Bros Cargo, everything is transparent. No extra costs outside the freight, customs, and insurance items specified in the contract will be reflected to you.
