Insurance and Financial Assurance for Shipping to France

Your Belongings are Safe on the France Journey: Insurance and Risk Management

When sending household goods, cargo, or vehicles from Turkey to France, one of your most basic needs is 'peace of mind'. Your belongings, which travel thousands of kilometers, must be financially protected against all kinds of risks on European highways. The 'France Shipping Insurance' we offer as Han Bros Cargo is not just a legal requirement (CMR), but also an indication of our respect for our customers. Regardless of Paris, Lyon, Marseille, or Bordeaux, you feel the assurance of Han Bros and the support of the most respected insurance organizations at every meter of your shipment.

Our Best Insurance Options for the France Line

Policy solutions you can choose according to the type and value of your shipment:

  • All-Risk (Full Coverage) Insurance: It is the safest method covering all external risks such as accident, theft, fire, and natural disasters from the moment your items are loaded until they are unloaded.
  • CMR Insurance: Mandatory in accordance with international road transport law, it provides basic legal protection against carrier faults.
  • Personal Effects and Vehicle Insurance: These are special policies aimed at protecting the market value for second-hand household goods and motor vehicles, which are also accepted at French customs.
  • Fragile Item Protection: We offer non-standard special guarantees for antiques, musical instruments, or artworks.

Why Should You Choose Han Bros Cargo's Insurance Support?

The true value of an insurance policy is understood when a damage occurs. As Han Bros Cargo, we act with the 'Fast Compensation Flow' principle in case of a possible setback. Thanks to our professional expertise network, we perform damage assessments immediately and follow the process with our lawyers who are dominant in French and Turkish insurance law. Our insurance secures not only the value of your item but also our packaging and transport labor. While we minimize risks with our vehicles that we monitor 7/24 via GPS, we create your financial armor against unexpected events with insurance support. We have thought of all the details for you to move to France with peace.

Sık Sorulan Sorular

Is every cargo sent to France insured?

All shipments made with Han Bros Cargo are under legal assurance with CMR insurance. However, 'All-Risk' policy is additionally recommended for full financial protection.

How is the report kept in case of damage?

You should check your items at the time of delivery and if you notice a damage, you must keep a 'Damage Assessment Record' together with the delivery personnel.

How is the insurance fee calculated?

The insurance premium is calculated over a low percentage according to the total financial value you declare of your items and the length/risk level of the transport route.

Can used (second-hand) items be insured?

Yes, we offer comprehensive insurance solutions for used furniture and household appliances in personal effects status.

When does the insurance process start?

Your policy becomes active with the loading of your items into our vehicle and ends the moment they are unloaded and delivered to you at your new address.
