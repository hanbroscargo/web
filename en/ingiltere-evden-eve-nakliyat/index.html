UK Door to Door Moving

Door to door moving to the UK is a process that requires careful planning and professional management. Han Bros Cargo is with you at every step of this process to provide a smooth moving experience.

Door to Door Moving Process - Step by Step Guide

Step 1: Pre-preparation and Planning (2-4 Weeks Before)

  • Creating goods inventory and identifying valuable items
  • Sorting out unnecessary items and planning sale/donation
  • Researching shipping companies and getting price quotes
  • Determining moving date

Step 2: Quote and Contract (1-2 Weeks Before)

  • Detailed inspection and volume calculation
  • Comparing price quotes
  • Signing contract and payment plan
  • Evaluating insurance options

Step 3: Packing and Preparation (1 Week Before)

  • Packing items not used daily
  • Special packaging for special items
  • Box labeling and creating inventory list
  • Preparing necessary documents for customs

Step 4: Loading Day

  • Team arrival and final checks
  • Professional loading and securing
  • Inventory check and signature
  • Delivery of customs documents

Step 5: Transport and Customs Procedures

  • Safe transport process
  • Management of customs procedures
  • Process tracking with online tracking system
  • Customs approval and release

Step 6: Delivery and Placement

  • Arrival at new address and unloading
  • Goods inspection and damage detection
  • Placement and assembly service (optional)
  • Signing delivery document

Important Points

  • Documents: Passport, visa, residence documents, household goods list should be ready
  • Prohibited Items: Flammable, explosive, toxic substances cannot be transported
  • Customs: Attention should be paid to new procedures after Brexit
  • Insurance: Additional insurance is recommended for valuable items
