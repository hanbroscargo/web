Transparent Budget Management for the Holland Journey: Plan Your Shipping Costs

The most critical stage of an international moving is budget planning. 'Household goods shipping prices to Holland' are fed by many different variables and offering a standard price list can be misleading. As Han Bros Cargo, we offer our customers not an 'estimated' but a 'definitive and transparent' cost breakdown. We protect your pocket with our special price models for the cities of Holland, each with different logistics dynamics (the narrow stairs of Amsterdam, the wide streets of Rotterdam). At the base of our pricing policy lies not selling any service to the customer that he does not need and only reflecting the price of the service he received in the most economical way.

Golden Items Determining Holland Shipping Prices

These criteria are mathematically calculated while our offer is being prepared:

  • Item Volume (Cubic Meter - m3): In international logistics, prices are determined over the area occupied by the items in the truck rather than their weight. The less m3, the lower the price.
  • Ground Floor / Floor Difference (Lifting): The floors where the items will be carried and delivered, the need for an elevator, and the truck berthing distance of the building affect the labor cost.
  • Packaging Packages: 'Standard' (you pack yourself) or 'Premium' (all packaging from us) options determine the price base.
  • Customs Clearance Service Fee: It is a fixed operation fee taken for the preparation of your personal effects exemption files and customs brokerage services.
  • KM Based Location: The distance difference between the Istanbul-Amsterdam line and the Hatay-Groningen line is reflected as fuel cost.

Our Suggestions for You to Save

You can lower your budget in Holland shipping with Han Bros Cargo with these methods: First, reduce your volume (m3) by getting rid of items you do not use. Second, be included in our 'Groupage' shipments by choosing flexible moving dates; so you lower the unit price by sharing the cost of a truck with our other customers. Third, do not be affected by current hikes by taking advantage of our early booking discounts. We minimize warehousing expenses by using our local strength in the Port of Rotterdam and reflect this saving directly to you. Transparency is the basis of trust.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of moving a complete house to Holland?

This depends on the number of rooms in the house and the density of items. There are serious differences between groupage shipping of a 2+1 house and complete moving of a 4+1 villa. Get a free expertise for a net figure.

Is insurance included in the price quote?

Yes, basic transport insurance (CMR) is included in every offer. Also, 'All-Risk' insurance options are offered for high-value items.

Will I pay an extra fee at Dutch customs?

If your personal items have been registered on you for more than 6 months, no tax occurs. Only our customs clearance service fee is included in the offer.

Do you give the price in foreign currency or TL?

Since international costs (fuel, etc.) are based on foreign currency, our offers are usually in Euro based but can be paid in TL at the exchange rate at the time of payment.

How much does the price drop if I do the packaging myself?

Since labor and material costs will drop, a significant discount is made in the price, but you should do a good risk analysis regarding damage responsibility.
