Best Departure and Arrival Cities in International Shipping

Logistics Strategy: Save by Choosing the Right City

One of the most critical factors determining costs and delivery times in international shipping is the cities where shipment starts or transfers are made. As Han Bros Cargo, we bring Turkey's industrial locomotive cities together with Europe's key distribution centers. Istanbul's power connecting continents, Izmir's proximity to Aegean ports, and Ankara's location in the center of Anatolia provide great advantages to our customers in our logistics operations. In this guide, we detail why you should choose the right city for your shipping processes and which cities offer which advantages.

Logistics Giants of Turkey and Their Advantages

The most strategic cities in the Han Bros Cargo operation network are:

  • Istanbul (Logistics Capital): With our warehouses on both the European and Anatolian sides, we provide the fastest road and air exits to Europe from both continents.
  • Izmir and Aegean Region: Especially in shipments to Mediterranean countries such as Spain, Italy, and France, we offer cost advantages with our port connections.
  • Ankara (Anatolia Center): Loads collected from every point of Central Anatolia are consolidated in our main Ankara warehouse and shipped to Europe with direct truck services.
  • Bursa and Kocaeli: In these regions where industrial production is intense, we prevent time loss by providing the fastest service in door-to-door collection processes.

Our Strategic Destination Points in Europe

The locations of our distribution centers not only in Turkey but also in Europe are of great importance. 'Best Cities' such as Brussels (Belgium), Frankfurt (Germany), and Vienna (Austria) are our main hubs for delivering your loads to their final address across Europe. Choosing these cities as departure or arrival points can reduce your 'Last Mile' delivery costs by up to 30%. Han Bros Cargo's smart route planning algorithm selects the most suitable city on your behalf according to the type of your load.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it more economical to depart from major cities?

Since load traffic is heavy in big cities, trucks fill up much faster in 'Partial (Groupage)' transport and waiting time decreases while freight prices fall.

Is there a direct line from Southeastern Anatolia cities to Europe?

Yes, we have regular express services to Europe, especially from our centers like Gaziantep and Diyarbakir.

How are city-based discounts applied?

Regionally incentivized discounts of up to 10% can be applied periodically in specific cities (for example, on all European loads originating from Izmir this month).

In which city do customs procedures proceed faster?

In centers like Istanbul and Edirne, which is close to border gates, procedures are completed quickly since our customs consultancy network is very wide.

How much does the departure city of my items affect the delivery day?

Distance to the border gate is important. For example, a truck leaving Istanbul reaches customs 6-8 hours earlier than one leaving Ankara.

Consult our experts to determine the most correct city and route in your logistics planning. With Han Bros Cargo, distances between cities are shortening, and your profit is increasing!
