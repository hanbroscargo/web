Guide to Shipping Household Goods from Turkey to Netherlands

A New Life in Netherlands: We Carry Your Belongings Safely

Moving from Turkey to the Netherlands (Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Den Haag, Eindhoven, etc.) is a process that needs to be well-planned logistically as much as it is a great excitement. As Han Bros Cargo, we don't just say 'yes' to the question 'Can we send our household goods to the Netherlands?', we manage the entire process with a turnkey comfort. With our decades of European shipping experience, we take your memories and valuable belongings from your door in Turkey and deliver them to your new address in the Netherlands in the safest way.

End-to-End Services We Offer in Netherlands Shipping

Professional steps waiting for you on your Netherlands journey:

  • Free Survey and Pricing: We calculate the volume (m3) of your items correctly and prepare the most suitable freight quote for your budget.
  • Packaging at International Standards: Every piece, from fragile kitchenware to white goods, is packaged with special materials durable for a journey of thousands of kilometers.
  • Personal Effects Customs Management: We provide expert support in preparing all documents required for tax exemption (verhuisboedel) at the Netherlands customs.
  • Storage Solutions: If your home in the Netherlands is not ready yet, we preserve your belongings in our secure warehouses in the region as long as you wish.
  • Door Delivery and Installation: We don't just leave your items at the door in the Netherlands; we carry them into the house and perform the assembly of your furniture.

Why Han Bros Cargo with Netherlands?

Our biggest advantage in Netherlands logistics is our command of local rules and geography. We have equipment suitable for all kinds of delivery scenarios, from narrow-street Amsterdam houses to modern buildings requiring elevators. While we put all your belongings under financial protection with CMR insurance, we allow you to monitor which country your truck is currently in with our 24/7 live GPS tracking system. Our operations support speaking Dutch and English ensures that processes proceed without getting stuck at local customs. We are here to clear your Netherlands dream of logistics stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to move household goods from Turkey to Netherlands?

Our shipments made via road are completed within an average of 10 to 14 days depending on customs density.

Do I pay tax at customs?

If you are returning permanently to the Netherlands or if you have a residence permit, you can benefit from tax exemption in 'personal effects' status. Our experts will guide you on this matter.

I want to send only a few boxes, not my entire house?

Yes, with our 'Partial (Groupage)' transport service, you can send even just a few boxes or a single room at economical prices.

What does the insurance coverage include?

Our CMR insurance protects the material value of your belongings against accidents, fire, and major damages that may occur during transport.

Do you provide assembly service in Netherlands?

Absolutely. We perform the disassembly of your furniture in Turkey and re-establish them in your home in the Netherlands with our professional teams.
