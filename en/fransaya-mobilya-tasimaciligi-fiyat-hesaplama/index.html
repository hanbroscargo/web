Cost Control in France Furniture Shipping: Accurate m3 Calculation

Sending furniture from Turkey to France (whether a new set or an antique piece) requires managing your logistics budget in the best way. The 'Furniture Price Calculation' methods we offer as Han Bros Cargo provide you with the fairest and most economical quote based on the volume (m3) occupied by your items and the route to be followed. Furniture is subject to a different calculation discipline from classic cargo due to its voluminous structure. In this guide, you will learn how you can optimize the freight cost of every piece from your sofa to your kitchen table and the budget impacts in the France customs processes.

4 Main Elements Determining Furniture Freight Quote

Criteria considered in the pricing of your France shipment:

  • Volumetric Calculation (M3): The total m3 is found by multiplying the width, length, and height measurements of your furniture. Since the m3 value of disassembled (dismantled) furniture will be lower, its price will also be more affordable.
  • Groupage (Partial) vs Complete Truck: If you are sending only one room or a few pieces of furniture, you pay only for the part of the truck allocated to you. This method reduces the cost by up to 50%.
  • Packaging and Labor Item: Wrapping the furniture with international road-durable packaging (bubble wrap, corner protector, etc.) and labor costs are included in the quote.
  • Delivery Region (Zone): A transport cost difference is applied between main centers such as Paris and remote points like the Alps regions of France or Corsica.

Han Bros Suggestions for Savings

Dismantle your furniture as much as possible to reduce your costs; this process saves space inside the vehicle, pulling down the m3-based price. Frequently Asked Questions

How is m3 calculation done in furniture?

Width x Length x Height measurements taken from the outermost points of the furniture are multiplied. For example; a sofa with dimensions 2m x 1m x 1m occupies 2 m3 space.

Does the price drop when I send disassembled furniture?

Absolutely yes. When furniture is dismantled into pieces, it occupies much less space inside the vehicle, which significantly reduces the freight cost calculated over m3.

How much tax arises for furniture at French customs?

If the furniture is 'New', France customs duties and VAT are applied over the invoice value. If it is used 'Personal Effects', tax exemption is in question.

Is the insurance fee included in the calculation?

Standard shipping insurance (CMR) is usually included in our quote. However, 'All-Risk' insurance can be calculated additionally for high-value furniture.

Can assembly service also be included in the calculation?

Yes, professional installation and assembly service to be provided at your address in France can also be added to your budget in line with your request.
