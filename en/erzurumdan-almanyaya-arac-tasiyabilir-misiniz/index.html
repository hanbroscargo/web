Vehicle Shipping and Customs from Erzurum to Germany

Secure Steering from Erzurum to Europe: Vehicle Shipping

Sending vehicles from Erzurum, the logistics center of Eastern Anatolia, to German metropolises such as Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, and Hamburg is now much easier with Han Bros Cargo's expert auto transport fleet. Whether you want to take your own vehicle to Germany as an expatriate or make a commercial vehicle sale; we pick up your vehicle from your door and deliver it safely to your address in Germany with our professional drivers who challenge even the harsh winter conditions of Erzurum. We manage this journey of thousands of kilometers on the Erzurum-Germany line by protecting the value and safety of your vehicle at the highest level.

Our Professional Steps in Erzurum-Germany Vehicle Shipment

Quality standards we offer in your vehicle's journey:

  • Multi and Single Auto Haulers: Affordable multi-tow trucks (Lohr type) or special single tow truck options for urgent shipments according to your budget.
  • Damage-Free Guarantee and Survey: While your vehicle is picked up in Erzurum, a detailed condition report (survey) is prepared and the insurance policy is activated instantly.
  • Customs and License Plate Procedures: Expert support in all documentation and tax processes required for temporary admission or final import of the vehicle at Germany customs.
  • High-Level Security: While our vehicles are tracked by GPS, we also provide the opportunity to put your personal belongings (within certain limits) inside the vehicle during shipment.

Logistics Expertise from Eastern Anatolia to Europe

In vehicle transportation from geographically distant regions like Erzurum to Europe, route optimization is of great importance. As Han Bros Cargo, we deliver our vehicles leaving Erzurum to border gates as soon as possible and combine them with our regular network on European highways. We not only ship passenger cars; we ship SUVs, motorcycles, classic vehicles, and all kinds of commercial light vehicles smoothly from Erzurum to the remotest towns of Germany. We owe our leadership in the sector to our knowledge of Erzurum's local conditions and the strength of our customs offices in Germany.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the vehicle shipping price from Erzurum to Germany?

Prices are determined according to the type of vehicle (passenger/SUV), size, and the destination city in Germany. Please share your vehicle information with us for an average price quote.

Can I carry personal belongings inside my vehicle?

Yes, you can carry a reasonable amount of your personal belongings (suitcases, boxes, etc.) inside the vehicle in accordance with customs rules and the weight capacity of the vehicle.

Who will meet my vehicle at Germany customs?

Han Bros Cargo's local partners in Germany or our own teams carry out customs procedures on your behalf. You just receive your vehicle at the delivery point.

How many days does transport take?

Arriving in Germany for a vehicle loaded from Erzurum takes an average of 10-15 days depending on customs and road conditions.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Absolutely. All our shipments are within the scope of international transport insurance (CMR) and everything is recorded with the report during loading.
