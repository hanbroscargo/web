The Most Economical Way to Send a Vehicle to Belgium

Sending a vehicle from Turkey to Belgium (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, etc.) is a much more economical and effortless process than expected when planned correctly. Basic Factors Determining Freight Prices

We follow a transparent pricing policy in Belgium vehicle shipping fees. When getting a quote for Belgium, the following items shape the price:

  • Vehicle Type and Size: Prices may change because the space occupied on the truck by a standard hatchback is different from a large SUV or commercial panel van.
  • Transport Model (Open/Closed): Multi-story open auto carrier trucks offer the most economical option; while the closed (box) trucks we use for luxury or classic vehicles are priced differently due to special security.
  • Customs and Legislative Expenses: Our quotes usually include border crossing and CMR insurance costs. Costs at the arrival customs in Belgium (if a customs-inclusive package is not selected) are specified separately.
  • Seasonal Density: The density formed during holiday periods and expatriate return seasons may affect 'Spot' freight prices; however, early booking protects you from this risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much are Belgium vehicle transport prices?

Prices can vary between 1,200€ and 2,500€ depending on the model of the vehicle and the delivery point. It is recommended to request a quote with a license photo for the most up-to-date and specific price for you.

Can I put personal items inside the vehicle? Does this increase the price?

According to customs rules, commercial goods cannot be inside the vehicle. However, taking 50-60 kg of personal belongings (personal effects) in the back seat in a way that does not block the driver's view does not bring additional load to the price.

Is the vehicle insured against damage risk? Is it included in the price?

Yes, all our vehicles are under road and cargo safety with CMR insurance. This insurance is included in our main freight price.

Who will clear my vehicle through customs in Belgium?

If you buy our 'Customs Clearance Included' service, we complete and deliver your vehicle's taxes and documents through our contracted consultants in Belgium.

What is the payment plan? Is there a cash discount?

You can pay a small deposit at the time of booking and the remaining payment at vehicle delivery. We apply 'best price' discounts between 5% and 10% for one-time cash payments.
