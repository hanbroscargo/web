Your Professional Solution Partner on Your United Kingdom Journey

With the trade and logistics rules changing after Brexit, sending goods or cargo from Turkey to the United Kingdom (London, Manchester, Birmingham, Liverpool, etc.) now requires more expertise. As Han Bros Cargo, we offer a unique service quality on the UK line with both our deep-rooted infrastructure in Turkey and our local office in London. By producing solutions fully compatible with British and Turkish legislation at every stage from customs processes to door delivery, we facilitate our customers' United Kingdom dreams.

Privileged Services We Offer on the UK Line

We are with you with our London-based operational power:

  • Post-Brexit Customs Consultancy: We prevent time losses and additional taxes by managing complex customs processes such as T1, T2 documents, and ATA Carnet with our professional team.
  • Weekly Regular Flights: We depart from Turkey to the UK regularly every week with our integrated road and sea transport models.
  • Personal Effects Transport (TOR1): We ensure that you benefit from customs duty exemption by guiding our citizens moving to the UK in Transfer of Residence (TOR) applications.
  • End-to-End Tracking and Insurance: In addition to road CMR insurance, we offer live GPS tracking on the entire UK route.
  • London and Surrounding Distribution Network: Through our London office, we can deliver to the door with small vehicles suitable for every point including narrow streets across the island.

Why Han Bros Cargo with the UK?

Our biggest difference in UK logistics is having our own physical offices in both countries. This situation allows us to minimize the waiting times at customs gates (Dover, Folkestone, etc.) and solve local problems instantly. We provide cost advantage with our 'Partial (Groupage)' service for all kinds of your shipments from household goods to commercial palletized loads. Our professional team provides 24/7 support in English and Turkish languages, constantly informing you about where your items are and the customs status. With Han Bros Cargo, the UK is no longer just an overseas country, but as close as your neighbor logistically.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to move items from Turkey to the UK?

Depending on the customs processes and the route used, our road shipments are completed in an average of 12 to 16 days.

What is a TOR (Transfer of Residence) document?

It is the approval code that people moving to the UK must obtain from HM Revenue and Customs in order to bring their household goods tax-free. Our experts support you in this process.

Will there be taxes at UK customs?

VAT and customs duty apply to new (commercial) products. However, used household goods brought under TOR are exempt from tax depending on compliance.

Do you deliver to cities other than London?

Yes, we deliver to all cities in the United Kingdom, including Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Glasgow, and Edinburgh.

Is furniture assembly also done in the UK?

Yes, we can offer installation service at your home in the UK with our professional assembly teams within our London office.
