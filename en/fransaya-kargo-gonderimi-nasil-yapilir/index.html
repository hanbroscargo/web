Parcel Shipping to France: Professional Roadmap and Legislation

Sending cargo to France, the heart of Europe, becomes a very simple and fast process when combined with the right information and preparation. As Han Bros Cargo, we provide the clearest answers to the question 'How is Cargo to France Done?' for our individual and corporate customers. Whether it is a gift package, a commercial sample, or a document; we manage a fast and reliable logistics process fully compatible with French customs legislation (TVA and EU standards). With our modern technological infrastructure, we receive your shipment from your door and deliver it safely to even the remotest towns of France.

Step-by-Step France Cargo Shipping Process

Corporate steps we follow for the trouble-free arrival of your cargo:

  • Analysis and Packaging: We optimize the volumetric weight (desi) by determining the most suitable packaging materials according to the type of your item. Correct packaging is the first and most important step for the safety of your cargo.
  • Customs Declaration Preparation: We provide expert support in preparing documents such as commercial invoice or proforma invoice required for French customs.
  • Online Tracking Infrastructure: From the moment your shipment is loaded into our vehicle, we define a special tracking number for you. With this number, you can monitor the location of your cargo 24/7.
  • Airport and Border Crossings: According to the service you prefer (Road Express or Air), we pass your cargo through customs gates in the fastest way.
  • In-France Distribution and Delivery: With our local distribution network in France, we deliver your package to the recipient at the door and report the delivery confirmation to you instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I send every kind of product to France by cargo?

You can send all legal products that are not on the prohibited list such as flammable, explosive, sharp, and fresh food products. Consult our experts for suspicious situations.

Who prepares the customs documents?

In line with the basic information (content, value, recipient information) we will receive from you, our professional team prepares the customs declarations on your behalf.

How is the France cargo fee calculated?

Pricing is done according to which of the actual weight or volumetric weight (desi) of the package is larger and depending on the selected delivery speed.

What happens if my cargo gets stuck at customs?

In cases such as missing documents or incorrect declarations, there may be a wait at customs; however, these risks are prevented by 99% with Han Bros Cargo's pre-control process.

Will the recipient in France be charged during delivery?

If customs duty (TVA) or local taxes arise, they may be collected from the recipient; however, the shipping fee is usually prepaid by the sender.
