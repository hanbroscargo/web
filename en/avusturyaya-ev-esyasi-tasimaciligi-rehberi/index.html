Complete A to Z Guide for Moving to Austria

The Manual for Those Settling in Austria

Austria is among the most preferred European countries by Turkish citizens due to its quality of life, unique culture, and economic stability. However, taking your belongings from Turkey to your new home on the shores of the Alps is much more than just renting a truck. In this comprehensive guide prepared by Han Bros Cargo, we have brought together everything you need to know, from logistics processes to customs laws.

'Partial' Convenience in Austrian Logistics

If you are not moving your entire home, you don't have to rent a full truck. By reserving your place in our trucks that depart regularly every week on the Austria route, you only pay for the space your belongings occupy. With this method, which we call Groupage (Partial), we offer economical delivery to cities like Vienna, Graz, and Linz, as well as to villages and towns.

Customs Exemption Conditions (Übersiedlungsgut)

There are some legal conditions for you to be able to bring your belongings into Austria without paying customs duty (Zoll) and VAT (Eust):

  • 6-Month Rule: The items to be moved must have been in your possession and use for at least 6 months.
  • Residence Document: A 'Meldezettel' or a work/education contract proving that you will reside in Austria is requested at customs.
  • Undertaking: You must undertake not to sell or rent the items to someone else for a certain period (usually 1 year) after clearing them from customs.

Local Service Details in Austria

An international move is not just a border crossing; comfort at the final point is very important. Han Bros Cargo's Austria team offers professionalism in these details:

  • Parking Permit (Halteverbot): Obtaining the necessary official permits for the truck to park in crowded cities like Vienna.
  • White Goods Setup: Installation of devices such as refrigerators, washing machines, and dishwashers in their new places.
  • Waste Management: Disposal of cardboard and plastic waste generated after packing according to local rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does it take to send items to Austria?

Our normal road shipping reaches Austria via Hungary in an average of 10-12 days after the Kapikule exit. In our express options, this period can drop to 5-7 days.

What should I pay attention to in the item list?

It is essential for the customs declaration that every item in your list (e.g., 5 boxes, 2 bed bases) is correct in terms of quantity and content. Han Bros Cargo helps you in the listing stage.

Who packs the kitchen utensils and clothes?

If you wish, our professional packing team can also provide service for kitchenware and textile products, or you can pack them yourself and we can only undertake the furniture/white goods.

Can I take antique items?

Yes, but special exit permits must be obtained from the Ministry of Culture for items that are antique or of historical value. Our experts will guide you in this matter.

What risks does the insurance cover?

Our transport insurance offers a broad coverage including accidents, natural disasters, fire, and physical damages (breakage, scratching) during transport.
