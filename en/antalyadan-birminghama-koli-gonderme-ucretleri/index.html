Parcel Shipping Rates from Antalya to Birmingham

The international transport sector requires trust and experience. As Turkey's leading brand in parcel shipping rates from Antalya to Birmingham, we safely complete your journey starting from Antalya to Birmingham.

Our Logistics Process from Antalya to Birmingham

We use road transport with access to Europe for the moves we carry out from Antalya. The distance between the two points is approximately 3,700 km, and our estimated delivery time is 14-16 days. We offer special advantages to our customers with secure packaging through our Mediterranean Region logistics network and special solutions for those settling in the UK from tourist regions.

2026 Pricing Policy

In operations departing from Antalya, prices are determined according to the volume (cbm) and weight of the goods, and the full address of destination. For loads arriving in Birmingham, highway fees and ferry crossing costs are reflected in the price. Please request a free survey for a net figure.

Parcel Shipping Service Details

Technical details are at the forefront of our service focused on Express parcel and package shipping. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable. Boxes collected from your door are barcoded and entered into the tracking system. In addition, you can benefit from our 48-72 hour delivery option for urgent shipments.

Transport Plan

  • Survey: Volume detection at your address in Antalya.
  • Operation: Boxes collected from your door are barcoded and entered into the tracking system.
  • Customs: Export procedures from Antalya and import procedures to Birmingham.
  • Delivery: Scheduled delivery and installation support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have storage services?

Yes, we have secure warehouses both in Turkey and the UK. We can keep your belongings for as long as you want.

Are there customs duties?

If you receive ToR approval in the personal effects status, you do not pay tax. For shipments under 300kg, Minivan Express or Air Cargo options are more suitable.

When is the payment made?

Reservation is made with a 30% down payment, and the rest is collected upon delivery.
